About this research shelf

These cards describe what Paramount’s promotional guides say, not what happened in a verified episode. They are neither approved Mission Briefings nor admitted Works, close reads, independent corroborations, or episode coverage. One corporate publisher family underlies the notes.

Images and clips are intentionally absent: this sidecar grants no artwork or audiovisual reuse rights. Source cards retain exact proposal paths, digests and guide locators for review.
