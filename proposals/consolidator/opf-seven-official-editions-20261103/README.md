**Independent hostile correction and first-party corroboration:** the accepted 347-row derivative's `isbn_checksum_valid` array is empty for `opf-local:0110`, but a separate hostile Auditor read the exact original owner-held OPF XML member (SHA-256 `4c35fb7b4991d34add2e92c450475edda12e77b773d99de2e14bcab33d16b0aa`) and found case-sensitive `<dc:Identifier>ISBN 0-7434-9997-2</dc:Identifier>` plus two UUID identifiers. The ISBN10 `0743499972` is checksum-valid, and conversion to ISBN13 yields `9780743499972`. By switching the publisher's **2019 trade-paperback product page to its eBook format**, the Auditor located an official **2004 Pocket Books/Star Trek ebook**, October 1, 2004, 384 pages, ISBN13 `9780743499972`; I independently opened the linked [first-party 2004 product page](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/A-Star-Trek-The-Next-Generation-Time-9-A-Time-for-War-A-Time-for-Peace/Keith-R-A-DeCandido/Star-Trek-The-Next-Generation/9780743499972) and rechecked these fields. This corroborates the original private OPF's `publisher:["Pocket Books"]` and `date:["2004"]` as a **candidate edition identifier**, not proof that the private converted content matches the publisher's eBook. The 2019 Gallery Books edition ISBN13 `9781982134907` remains distinct. A single publisher corporate family supplies both product pages. I record the source XML identifier extraction discrepancy and retain the accepted derivative unchanged pending a separate governed correction. For the other six rows, an empty derivative `isbn_checksum_valid` array **does not prove the original XML lacks a valid differently cased/encoded identifier**.

The local OPF XML title/creator labels are retained literally, including historical typos/series ambiguity. A source file may contain multiple Works or share a Work with a different source; **347 OPFs ≠ 347 distinct Works**.

`tools/build_opf_seven_publisher_crosschecks.py` hashes the exact accepted 347-OPF observations, 39-card sample, 1,214-row discovery baseline, and the prior accepted same-family publisher packet before projecting seven HELD metadata cards. The first source-local card now references **two distinct publisher product editions**, for eight products total, but only one candidate corresponds by mathematically valid ISBN10→13 conversion to the private source's original identifier. Neither publisher product is automatically admitted as the private file's proven edition.
