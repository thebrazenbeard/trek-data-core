## Reproducibility ceiling

- Each URL was inspected through a public web-reader view on 2026-11-02.
  These are retrievable source locators and observation-date notes, **not**
  independently signed or cryptographically fixed page captures.
- First-party branding does not prove that two distribution views are
  independent primary audiovisual witnesses.
- The observed StarTrek.com page supports only a series-level total. It does
  not independently establish a full per-episode listing or all special cases.
- The five-title comparison is a **small reconciliation test case**, not a
  denominator. Nothing in this proposal changes `registry/authoritative/`,
  `coverage/denominators/`, `registry/approved_projection_hashes.json`, or
  either Explorer index.

## Required next steps before admission

1. A Librarian first defines the reporting unit and scope in writing:
   original broadcast installments, currently streamable episode units,
   remastered releases, or another explicitly bounded edition. The choice
   must include territory, observation cutoff date, and the treatment of
   *The Cage*, two-part stories, specials, and other ambiguous units.
2. Obtain a permitted, provenance-fixed season-by-season roster with a stable
   source-version receipt; reconcile each source-local catalog entry to an
   explicit proposed Work without reusing unqualified ordinal IDs.
3. An independent reviewer checks proposed Work identities, accepted parent
   Mission Work, evidence scope, publisher/source ownership, and rights.
4. Accepted episode coverage requires a nonfixture authoritative Work and
   separately governed accepted coverage event. `SOURCE_BOUND` additionally
   requires a real accepted Source, `EVIDENCE_BEARING` Source↔Work binding,
   matching SHA-256 byte basis, and origin checks. A publisher listing alone
   cannot assert watched content, close-read completion, or audited claims.
5. To appear as an admitted **Mission Briefing** in the public Explorer,
   source-relative assertions must pass the canonical evidence/projection
   gates and an independently reviewed approved projection hash. A Work
   registry census `RESOLVED` status, if later reached, would still be only
   a census of accepted Works, not proof of complete Star Trek episode coverage.
