## What CC BY 2.0 establishes—and what it does not

The [Creative Commons BY 2.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/) grants copying and adaptations subject to creator/title attribution, source and license links, disclosure of relevant changes, and no implication of licensor endorsement. The [legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/legalcode) governs. The license's own notice warns that privacy, publicity and other rights may restrict a particular use. The photographer's license does **not** independently license Paramount's film artwork, incidental event backdrop/logos, Abrams's or Pegg's likeness for advertising, or a claim that the image is a scene/still/poster from the film.
