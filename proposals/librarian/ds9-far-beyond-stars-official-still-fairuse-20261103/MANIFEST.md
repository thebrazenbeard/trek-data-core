## Four-factor U.S. fair-use review, not a mechanical veto or grant

Primary authority: [17 U.S.C. §107](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#107) and [U.S. Copyright Office's Fair Use Index](https://copyright.gov/fair-use/). Source-grounded analogy: [*Perfect 10 v. Amazon*, Ninth Circuit opinion](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2007/12/03/0655405.pdf). This case upheld a particular image-search thumbnail function; it does **not** license every entertainment still. The [Supreme Court's *Warhol v. Goldsmith* opinion](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/22pdf/21-869_87ad.pdf) underscores that purpose must be assessed for the actual use.

1. **Purpose/character — conditional favor**: noncommercial, informational, episode-specific research-index thumbnail with actual source link and meaningful catalog context; not pure decoration, page hero, advertisement or standalone gallery. Calling an ordinary decorative image “research” would not suffice.
2. **Nature — against**: this is copyrighted, creative fictional audiovisual expression.
3. **Amount/substantiality — mixed**: only one highly compressed, 160×122 still, not a moving sequence or complete episode; however the mirror motif may be qualitatively central, and an entire individual still's composition is reproduced.
4. **Market — conditional favor with uncertainty**: a 5.4KB thumbnail cannot substitute for an episode viewing experience or a high-quality licensed still, but studio still licensing and cumulative aggregation across many entries remain material review questions.

**Disposition:** `BOUNDED_FOUR_FACTOR_PLAUSIBLE_THUMBNAIL_CANDIDATE_NOT_LEGAL_CLEARANCE`. Lack of a CC grant is **not** an automatic veto: §107 applies case-by-case. Equally, Patrick's noncommercial research purpose is **not** a blanket guarantee. Independent exact-use analysis and browser/network consent are release gates.
