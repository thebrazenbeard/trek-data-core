# *First Contact* film-prop photographs — HELD source-only packet

This research packet identifies two **actual documentary photographs of props**
from *Star Trek: First Contact* (1996), not frames, film stills, posters, or
episode artwork. It contains **no image bytes**, no image embeds/hotlinks, and
does not display or release anything on the Explorer. The row association
`film:film-first-contact` is a source-local catalogue locator, **not** proof
of an underlying rights grant, film-canon evidence, or research coverage.

## Primary photo source and lineage

Both photographs were made by **Joseph (Joe) Ross / Joetography LLC** at the
*Star Trek: Exploring New Worlds* exhibition, Henry Ford Museum, Dearborn,
Michigan on **August 22, 2019**. The photographer's Flickr originals were
mirrored to Wikimedia Commons; **Flickr + Commons form one provenance family**,
not two independent rights holders.

1. **Borg cube:** [photographer's Flickr original](https://www.flickr.com/photos/joeross/49786189402/), [Wikimedia Commons file/rights page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Borg_Collective_-_Star_Trek-_First_Contact_movie_1996_-_Star_Trek_-_Exploring_New_Worlds_exhibit_at_the_Henry_Ford_Museum,_Dearborn,_Michigan.jpg).
2. **Hand phaser and phaser rifle:** [photographer's Flickr original](https://www.flickr.com/photos/joeross/49786193777/), [Wikimedia Commons file/rights page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Phasers_from_Star_Trek-_First_Contact_movie_1996_-_Star_Trek_-_Exploring_New_Worlds_exhibit_at_the_Henry_Ford_Museum,_Dearborn,_Michigan.jpg).

The two Commons pages identify the film-specific props and carry
**Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0 Generic (CC BY-SA 2.0)** for the
*photographer's contribution*, checked against the originating Flickr license
by Wikimedia's Flickr reviewer on **June 15, 2023**. That does **not**
automatically license Paramount's underlying film-prop design, characters,
artwork, trademarks, or any publicity rights. [License deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/);
[license legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/legalcode).

The [first-party film entry](https://www.startrek.com/movies/star-trek-viii-first-contact)
