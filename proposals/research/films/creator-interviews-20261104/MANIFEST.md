## Evidence discipline and original analysis

The source is each published **interview account**, not the audiovisual film. Evidence is source-relative paraphrase of a specific person answering an interview question, joined to a local interview-account entity. Assertions describe what that person said or recalled, not an objectively established scene, intention of every writer, final screenplay, or production fact. The three original summaries describe the **accounts of their development**, not the movies' plots. This adds testimony not already present in the accepted publisher-card discovery descriptions.

- **Beyond:** Jung describes interrogating Federation ideals and writing interpersonal pairings. He says the name *Ben* was used for his role in the script but not spoken on screen; film dialogue has **not** been independently checked. His interview statement about not yet watching a fully rendered cut raises a **date-of-interview vs date-of-publication** question; the article published after the public theatrical opening, so this is not evidence that he had never watched the cut by July 28, 2016.
- **First Contact:** Frakes retrospectively describes editorial and design decisions, a desired Q appearance that did not occur, and music licensing constrained by budget. The stated studio rationale for Q is secondhand and explicitly uncertain.
- **Insurrection:** Berman says Piller's first treatment was much darker and changed through contributions of several people. Stillwell says he processed multiple revisions and helped with a **completed book that remained unpublished**. These independently attributable speaker accounts are not independently inspected draft/version evidence, and the book itself was not read.

All four accounts reside in **one corporate publisher family**, so `independent_publisher_family_count=1`. Different eyewitnesses may offer differently situated testimony; their common hosting platform does not magically create four independent source lineages. Berman's producer account and Stillwell's coordinator account are not yet reconciled or version-diffed.
