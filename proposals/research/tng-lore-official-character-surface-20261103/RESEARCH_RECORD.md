# Lore official-source character and artwork discovery — frozen research plan

## Preregistration (frozen before source retrieval)

Question: What can a bounded inspection of first-party Star Trek publisher pages
actually support about the fictional character Lore, and which official artwork
locators could be considered without asserting publication rights?

This is a **purposive, user-suggested case**, not preference-blind selection from
the Trek research denominator. It must not become a salience or prevalence claim.

- **H1:** At least one current official StarTrek.com or Paramount page explicitly
  identifies Lore in relation to Data. **Falsifier:** no accessed first-party page
  actually supports that relation.
- **H2:** First-party pages give at least one episode-context locator for Lore.
  **Falsifier:** the only evidence is a secondary recap or model recollection.
- **H3:** First-party publisher pages expose an image candidate, but inspection
  alone does not establish republication permission. **Falsifier to stronger
  reuse hypothesis:** no explicit reusable license applies to the exact asset.

Confirmatory probes:

- **M1:** Open at most three directly relevant official StarTrek.com articles or
  character pages, record exact URL, accessed date, which visible page portions
  were actually read, and decisive publisher-supported metadata.
- **M2:** Locate at most two official Paramount+ episode detail/guide entries as
  independent page-type metadata, preserving the same upstream rights-holder
  lineage rather than counting it as independent corroboration.
- **M3:** Inventory at most three official artwork URL candidates (locator,
  contextual page, medium) without downloading or committing image bytes;
  default rights to `HELD_NOT_VERIFIED`.

Stopping rule: stop after M1–M3 or earlier if official pages are unavailable.
Do not claim a complete episode watch, a sourced mind-upload event, or a canon
fact about any current AI model. A future Explorer Lore persona is opt-in
**fictional UI behavior**, not a research conclusion.
