S4E8's apparent sixteen-year passage is a character-facing discovery, not externally reconciled elapsed time. S4E13's woman merely *claims* to be the devil; the description does not prove her ontology.

> **Hostile challenge:** The S4E16 *surrogate mother* label for Enterprise is figurative, not biological identity. S4E21 implicates Picard during a spy search without proving treason. S4E23 describes changing bodily hosts without proving one global personhood continuity model. These distinctions remain unresolved rather than being forced into a canonical claim.

A separate website/persona decision to use Badgey instead of Lore must not rewrite this historical episode description. The regression test rejects replacing the original literal source mention `Lore` with `Badgey`.
