## The Wire

Local entities: Garak, Bashir, Odo, Quark, Enabran Tain, Obsidian Order, Garak's cranial implant, Garak autobiographical versions, Elim referent.

Evidence:
W01 Garak suffers escalating pain/neurological crisis while initially refusing medical care.
W02 Bashir discovers a long-standing implant connected throughout Garak's nervous system; Quark's attempt to source its classified technology triggers fear of the Obsidian Order.
W03 Garak explains the implant was designed to release pleasure/pain-suppressing effects under torture and says he has been using it continuously in exile, producing dependence and tissue damage.
W04 During withdrawal/illness Garak gives Bashir multiple incompatible explanations of his exile, including claims about killing prisoners, releasing children, betrayal by an associate named Elim, and Obsidian Order service.
W05 Bashir treats the medical condition independently of whether any autobiographical version is true.
W06 Tain confirms Garak was important to the Obsidian Order and supplies Cardassian biochemical data needed to save him, while expressing hostility toward Garak and wishing him prolonged exile.
W07 Tain reveals that “Elim” is Garak's first name, directly disconfirming at least the literal form of Garak's story about a separate friend Elim.
W08 Garak recovers and refuses to identify a single true autobiographical version, treating contradictory narratives as part of deliberate obfuscation.

Assertions:
WA01 PROPOSED/CONTESTED — Garak's specific exile narrative remains unresolved because his testimony is deliberately contradictory; individual utterances are evidence that he said them, not automatic biography. W04,W07-W08.
WA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Garak served in/was closely connected to the Obsidian Order and Enabran Tain at a high level. W02,W06.
WA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Continuous implant use produced physiological dependence and life-threatening withdrawal/toxicity. W02-W06.
WA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Bashir's duty of care is maintained despite unresolved truth, suspected crimes, and Garak's hostility. W05-W06.

## Crossover

Local entities: prime Kira, prime Bashir, mirror Intendant Kira, mirror Garak, mirror Sisko, mirror O'Brien, mirror Odo, mirror Quark, Alliance, Terran workers.

Evidence:
C01 Kira/Bashir cross through the wormhole under abnormal conditions and arrive at a Terok Nor ruled by a Klingon-Cardassian Alliance.
C02 Mirror-world history attributes a major political divergence to the earlier Kirk-era crossover and subsequent reforms; this is local historical testimony for that universe.
C03 Prime Kira encounters a physically similar counterpart who is politically/socially distinct and exercises authority as Intendant.
C04 Mirror Terrans are a subordinated labor population; mirror Odo oversees ore processing and mirror Quark covertly aids escapes.
C05 Prime Kira initially attempts to use counterpart similarity and political persuasion to secure release, while the Intendant treats her as both fascination and controllable asset.
C06 Mirror Garak seeks to use prime Kira as a replacement for the Intendant in a coup, further demonstrating counterpart identity does not imply shared agency/loyalty.
C07 Prime Bashir kills mirror Odo while escaping; mirror O'Brien is inspired by evidence that another history permits Terrans different lives/status and chooses resistance rather than fleeing to the prime universe.
C08 Kira/Bashir return through the wormhole; mirror Sisko and O'Brien remain to pursue local resistance.

Assertions:
CA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Prime and mirror counterparts are distinct local entities despite names/appearances/history relations; no global SAME_AS merge is appropriate at worker level. C01-C08.
CA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Cross-universe information changes mirror O'Brien's political agency without making him identical to prime O'Brien. C04,C07.
CA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Mirror history is a divergent local continuity whose causal account is source testimony within that frame. C02.

## The Collaborator

Local entities: Kira, Bareil, Winn, Kubus Oak, Prylar Bek, Kai Opaka, Opaka's son, Kendra Valley resistance cell, Vedek Assembly.

Evidence:
K01 Kubus, a documented occupation collaborator, returns seeking sanctuary and information leverage during the Kai selection.
K02 Winn uses Kubus's testimony to implicate Bareil as the superior behind Prylar Bek's disclosure of the Kendra Valley resistance base.
K03 Bareil acknowledges visiting Bek and later admits erasing relevant transmission records; these facts initially strengthen suspicion.
K04 Bareil explains that Cardassian forces were poised to eliminate surrounding villages and that the disclosure was intended to sacrifice a smaller resistance cell to save roughly 1,200 civilians.
K05 Bareil withdraws from the Kai election before Kira fully resolves the evidence, allowing Winn to be selected.
K06 Transit records later prove Bareil was at Dakeen Monastery and could not have ordered Bek's disclosure at the relevant time.
K07 Kira infers, and Bareil confirms, that Kai Opaka herself authorized the disclosure, sacrificing the cell including her own son to save the larger civilian population; Bareil erased records and accepted blame to protect Opaka's legacy.
K08 Bareil refuses to reopen the matter after Winn's selection, treating the political/spiritual consequence as already fixed.
K09 Counterframe: Kira experiences dream/vision material involving Opaka, Bareil and Bek; these experiences are preserved as vision evidence rather than used independently to prove the historical chain.

Assertions:
KA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Bareil was not the original Kendra Valley collaborator/order-giver; transit evidence disconfirms that attribution. K03-K07.
KA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Bareil deliberately concealed evidence and accepted political ruin to protect Opaka's responsibility/legacy. K05-K08.
KA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Opaka's decision is represented as a tragic wartime sacrifice calculation, not as evidence that collaborators share one motive or moral category. K04,K07.
KA04 PROPOSED/UNKNOWN — Religious interpretation of the Kai outcome and Kira's vision remains source-relative; political causation is separately supported by the investigation. K01-K09.

## Tribunal

Local entities: Miles O'Brien, Keiko, Odo, Sisko, Makbar, Conservator Kovat, Gul Evek, Raymond Boone original, surgically altered Cardassian Boone substitute, Maquis referent, Cardassian court.

Evidence:
T01 O'Brien is seized outside ordinary Cardassian jurisdictional expectations and taken to Cardassia without initially being told the charge.
T02 Cardassian officials explicitly state the guilty verdict and execution schedule precede the public trial; the proceeding is designed to demonstrate how guilt was established and reaffirm state order.
T03 Manufactured station evidence includes O'Brien's reconstructed voice opening a weapons locker, manipulated logs, and stolen photon warheads planted in his runabout.
T04 DS9 analysis proves the voice recording was assembled phoneme-by-phoneme rather than spoken by O'Brien.
T05 Investigation focuses on Raymond Boone, an old Rutledge acquaintance whose family says his personality/history changed after Cardassian captivity.
T06 DNA/dental evidence establishes the present Boone is Cardassian and surgically altered; the original Boone was taken prisoner and apparently killed.
T07 The substituted Boone created/assisted the evidentiary frame intended to connect O'Brien, Starfleet/Federation, and Maquis weapons activity.
T08 The Cardassian trial refuses exculpatory evidence because the verdict is already fixed, while publicly using O'Brien's prior anti-Cardassian prejudice and wartime history as character evidence.
T09 Cardassian authorities release O'Brien only after the Boone substitution is exposed in a way that threatens to embarrass the High Command publicly.
T10 Sisko/Odo infer the trial's larger purpose was political: discredit Federation legitimacy and frame Maquis support as institutionally sanctioned.

Assertions:
TA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — The prosecution evidence against O'Brien is deliberately manufactured. T03-T07.
TA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Present Boone and original Raymond Boone are distinct local entities; appearance/memory/social continuity claims cannot override DNA/dental/substitution evidence. T05-T07.
TA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — The Cardassian proceeding is explicitly verdict-first and politically performative rather than an open evidentiary adjudication. T02,T08-T10.
TA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — O'Brien's genuine prejudice/war history is factually distinct from the fabricated specific crime evidence. T08.

## The Jem'Hadar

Local entities: Sisko, Jake, Quark, Nog, Eris presented-victim persona, Eris Dominion agent, Jem'Hadar, Talak'talan, Odyssey crew, Dominion referent, New Bajor colony.

Evidence:
J01 Sisko/Jake/Nog/Quark travel to the Gamma Quadrant for a science outing; Sisko/Quark encounter Eris and are captured by Jem'Hadar.
J02 Eris presents herself as a refugee from Kurill Prime, claims Dominion conquest after refusal to join, and describes a telekinetic ability supposedly suppressed by a collar.
J03 Jem'Hadar identify themselves as Dominion soldiers and explicitly warn DS9 personnel to stop Gamma Quadrant incursions; they provide a list of destroyed vessels and say New Bajor fought and was destroyed.
J04 Rescue forces encounter Jem'Hadar weapons that penetrate normal Federation shields despite harmonic changes.
J05 During withdrawal a Jem'Hadar ship deliberately makes a suicide run into the Odyssey, destroying the larger Federation starship despite the Jem'Hadar already having tactical advantage.
J06 Quark's examination reveals Eris's “telepathic/telekinetic suppressor” collar is functionally only a locking device; she could have used her abilities throughout captivity.
J07 Eris then acknowledges the capture/escape was staged so she could infiltrate/spying on the Federation; her prior victim narrative is therefore deliberate cover testimony.
J08 Eris rejects Sisko's assumption that she is a Founder and escapes via an untraceable transporter signature; her precise Dominion rank/species relationship is not established in this episode alone.
J09 Sisko concludes the Dominion confrontation has begun and plans DS9 defenses around the wormhole.

Assertions:
JA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Eris is a Dominion agent whose prisoner/refugee presentation was an infiltration operation. J02,J06-J08.
JA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Eris's detailed Kurill/Dominion story is testimony contaminated by demonstrated deception and should not be promoted wholesale to world-state fact without independent support. J02,J06-J08.
JA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Jem'Hadar military capability is directly demonstrated by shield penetration and deliberate destruction of the Odyssey. J03-J05.
JA04 PROPOSED/UNRESOLVED — Eris's exact place in Dominion identity hierarchy remains unresolved within the work. J08.
