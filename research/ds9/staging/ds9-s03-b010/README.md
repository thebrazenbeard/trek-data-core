## The Search, Part I

Local entities: Sisko, Odo, Kira, Dax, O'Brien, Bashir, Quark, T'Rul, Defiant, Karemma contact, Jem'Hadar attackers, Omarion destination impulse.

Evidence:
S101 Starfleet assigns the Defiant, a warship developed against the Borg, to DS9; a Romulan cloaking device is supplied under Romulan supervision.
S102 The mission objective is to locate the Founders and seek a negotiated relationship rather than begin an offensive campaign.
S103 Odo learns Starfleet has considered replacing him as security chief because of chain-of-command conflicts; he understands the rationale but does not agree with it.
S104 Quark traces Dominion commercial contact through the Karemma, whose representative identifies Vorta as their Dominion intermediaries and expresses fear of Jem'Hadar enforcement.
S105 A Dominion communications relay directs the Defiant toward the Omarion Nebula; Dax/O'Brien are stranded when a shield activates and Jem'Hadar ships arrive.
S106 Sisko refuses to treat Dax/O'Brien as expendable but ultimately leaves the relay rather than sacrifice the Defiant immediately, expecting them to be interrogated.
S107 Odo reports an increasingly compelling instinctive pull toward the Omarion Nebula and asks to leave immediately despite not knowing why.
S108 Jem'Hadar forces penetrate the Defiant's cloak, disable and board the ship; Kira is wounded.
S109 Odo removes the unconscious Kira in a shuttle and proceeds toward the Omarion Nebula rather than returning directly to the wormhole.
S110 A rogue class-M planet in the nebula triggers Odo's recognition/description of arrival as home.

Assertions:
S1A01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Odo's movement toward Omarion is depicted as an internally experienced compulsion/instinct whose mechanism is not yet established in Part I. S107-S110.
S1A02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Karemma testimony establishes their local commercial relationship to Vorta/Dominion structures, not a complete Dominion hierarchy. S104.
S1A03 PROPOSED/STABLE — The Defiant mission begins as attempted contact/negotiation and becomes a military survival/capture event after Jem'Hadar intervention. S102,S105-S109.
S1A04 PROPOSED/UNRESOLVED — Fate/status of Dax, O'Brien, Sisko and others is unresolved at Part I's endpoint from Kira/Odo's perspective. S105-S110.

## The Search, Part II

Local entities: Odo, Kira, Female Changeling/local Founder, Great Link, captured Federation/DS9 crew, simulated Sisko, simulated Nechayev, simulated Dominion treaty environment, Jem'Hadar/Vorta simulation operators.

Evidence:
S201 Odo encounters a community of shapeshifters who welcome him and identify their communal state as the Great Link.
S202 The Female Changeling says Odo was sent away while newly formed, frames solids as historically dangerous to changelings, and teaches him controlled linking.
S203 Odo experiences the Link as deeply attractive but remains concerned about Kira and the captured crew.
S204 The apparent DS9 sequence shows Sisko and crew returning, negotiating a Dominion treaty, accepting Dominion presence in the Alpha Quadrant, excluding Romulans, and ultimately contemplating destruction of the wormhole.
S205 The apparent sequence escalates through events inconsistent with established loyalties and produces resistance by Sisko's group.
S206 The Founder reveals the apparent DS9/treaty sequence was an experiment imposed on the captured crew to test how the Federation would respond to a Dominion foothold.
S207 The crew had remained physically captive since being taken from the Defiant; their simulated experiences were subjectively convincing but did not occur as depicted in external Alpha Quadrant reality.
S208 The Female Changeling tells Odo that changelings are the Dominion/Founders and explains control of solids as protection/order after historical persecution.
S209 Odo explicitly rejects the Founders' justification, identifies his commitment to justice and his existing social bond with the solids, and demands the crew's release.
S210 The Founder releases the crew rather than harm Odo, citing the changeling rule that no changeling has harmed another; Odo leaves with the solids despite the Link's appeal.

Assertions:
S2A01 PROPOSED/STABLE — The treaty/collapse-the-wormhole sequence is a Dominion simulation/experiment and must not be projected as externally occurring Alpha Quadrant history. S204-S207.
S2A02 PROPOSED/STABLE — The changeling community identifies itself as the Founders controlling the Dominion. S208.
S2A03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Odo's encounter supplies strong biological/social-origin evidence linking him to the Great Link while his present chosen affiliations remain distinct from Founder political identity. S201-S210.
S2A04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Subjectively experienced simulated events remain evidence about participant responses and Dominion experimental aims, not evidence that simulated political events occurred. S204-S207.
S2A05 PROPOSED/CONTESTED — Founder historical claims about persecution of changelings are group testimony supporting their self-justification; their truth/scope requires independent evidence. S202,S208-S209.

## The House of Quark

Local entities: Quark, Grilka, Kozak, D'Ghor, Gowron, Klingon High Council, Rom.

Evidence:
Q01 Kozak dies accidentally by falling on his own blade during a confrontation in Quark's bar; Quark initially invents an honorable-killing story for commercial reputation.
Q02 Grilka exposes Quark's lie but uses the invented victory claim within Klingon inheritance rules, coercively marrying him to preserve her House rather than allow D'Ghor to absorb it.
Q03 Quark's financial analysis reveals D'Ghor has weakened Kozak's House for years through debt, devaluation and creditor manipulation.
Q04 Grilka and Quark characterize D'Ghor's method as dishonorable because it avoids open Klingon challenge while pursuing the same assets.
Q05 Before the Council D'Ghor counters with proof that Kozak died accidentally and demands personal combat with Quark.
Q06 Quark cannot realistically defeat D'Ghor and eventually appears unarmed, explicitly reframing the ritual combat as a public execution without honor or glory.
Q07 Gowron stops D'Ghor from killing Quark, judges the act incompatible with honor, and removes D'Ghor's standing in the Hall.
Q08 Grilka receives special dispensation to lead her House herself.
Q09 Grilka grants Quark the divorce he requests after he restores her House/status.
Q10 Counterevidence: Quark's initial deception is real and self-interested even though his later financial analysis and refusal to perform a sham honorable duel expose D'Ghor.

Assertions:
QA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Kozak's death is accidental; Quark's initial heroic-killing account is knowingly fabricated. Q01-Q02,Q05.
QA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Quark defeats D'Ghor politically by exposing the mismatch between Klingon honor norms and D'Ghor's financial/execution behavior, not by martial victory. Q03-Q08.
QA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Grilka's marriage to Quark begins under coercive/instrumental inheritance conditions and ends by requested divorce; it should not be flattened into ordinary romantic marriage evidence. Q02,Q08-Q09.

## Equilibrium

Local entities: Jadzia Dax, Dax symbiont, Joran Belar/Joran Dax suppressed host, Torias Dax referent, Curzon Dax referent, Yolad Belar, Trill Symbiosis Commission, Guardian Timor, Dr Renhol.

Evidence:
E01 Jadzia develops unfamiliar music, hallucinations, anger and falling isoboramine levels without an initially identified external cause.
E02 Guardian Timor infers a host/symbiont memory imbalance and becomes reluctant after institutional pressure appears to discourage investigation.
E03 The music is identified as Joran Belar's composition; viewing Joran's image triggers neural crisis in Jadzia.
E04 Public/central records concerning Joran are partially purged and official Dax records show Torias followed directly by Curzon.
E05 Joran's brother recalls Joran saying he had been joined for roughly six months before Joran's death and noticed marked personality change/confidence.
E06 Sisko/Bashir infer Joran was actually joined to Dax between Torias and Curzon, contradicting altered official records.
E07 The Commission admits Joran Dax existed for six months and that his successful joining disproved the public claim that only roughly one in a thousand Trill can safely host a symbiont; nearly half the population is physiologically capable.
E08 The Commission says the secret was preserved because symbionts are scarce and broader knowledge could destabilize Trill society and endanger symbionts.
E09 Joran's memories were deliberately suppressed/removed from Dax's accessible memory; deterioration of that block causes Jadzia's medical crisis.
E10 Jadzia chooses reintegration of Joran's memories despite risk; afterward she explicitly recognizes Joran as part of herself and stabilizes.

Assertions:
EA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Joran Belar was a genuine Dax host between Torias and Curzon for approximately six months despite official record erasure. E03-E09.
EA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — The Symbiosis Commission deliberately falsified/suppressed records and Dax memory to preserve a social scarcity/selectivity regime. E04-E09.
EA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Jadzia's survival requires restoration/integration rather than continued erasure of the prior-host memories. E09-E10.
EA04 PROPOSED/UNRESOLVED — Joran's violent personality remains part of Dax's remembered history without establishing that Jadzia is morally/legal identical to Joran. E05-E10.
EA05 PROPOSED/STABLE — The source directly falsifies the stated one-in-a-thousand physiological-suitability claim; institutional scarcity remains partly material because symbiont numbers are limited. E07-E08.

## Second Skin

Local entities: Kira Nerys, fabricated Iliana-Ghemor identity presentation, actual Iliana Ghemor referent, Tekeny Ghemor, Entek, Cardassian agent posing as Elemspur witness, Garak, Sisko, Odo, Obsidian Order, Cardassian dissident network.

Evidence:
SS01 Bajoran records and a claimed former prisoner unexpectedly place Kira at Elemspur, which she denies remembering.
SS02 Kira is abducted and wakes surgically altered to Cardassian appearance; Entek and Ghemor tell her she is Iliana Ghemor, a Cardassian operative whose Kira memories were implanted for infiltration.
SS03 Entek demonstrates knowledge of intimate Kira memories and shows a pre-surgery recording of a woman presented as Iliana volunteering for Bajor infiltration; these are powerful manipulative exhibits but initially remain under Obsidian Order control.
SS04 Kira repeatedly rejects the identity claim and attempts escape rather than treating inaccessible “original” memories as proof.
SS05 Ghemor increasingly protects Kira as his daughter and reveals/acts through connections to a Cardassian dissident movement opposing Central Command/Obsidian Order power.
SS06 Kira infers the operation was designed not to recover Iliana but to force Ghemor to expose his dissident ties while rescuing someone he believed was his daughter.
SS07 Entek explicitly confirms the operation was meant to unmask Ghemor and the dissident movement.
SS08 Garak/Sisko/Odo intervention enables escape; Ghemor leaves Cardassia as an exposed dissident.
SS09 Bashir's genetic examination establishes Kira is entirely Bajoran; her Cardassian appearance was surgical.
SS10 The Elemspur witness disappears and is suspected to have been a Cardassian agent who altered detention records.
SS11 The actual Iliana's fate remains unknown. Ghemor reasons she may still be alive/on Bajor, but this is hope/inference rather than confirmation.
SS12 Ghemor gives Kira a family object and acknowledges she is not literally his daughter while preserving an emotionally meaningful kin-like bond.

Assertions:
SSA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Kira is not source-supported as Iliana Ghemor; the Iliana identity scenario was an Obsidian Order deception using surgery, fabricated/corrupted records and controlled evidence. SS01-SS10.
SSA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — The operation's primary demonstrated purpose was to expose Tekeny Ghemor's dissident affiliation. SS05-SS08.
SSA03 PROPOSED/UNRESOLVED — Actual Iliana Ghemor's survival/location remains unknown. SS03,SS11.
SSA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Kira/Ghemor develop a chosen/emotional familial relation after the biological/biographical identity claim is disproven; social kinship and literal daughter identity remain distinct. SS09-SS12.
SSA05 PROPOSED/STABLE — Intimate-memory knowledge and documentary/video evidence can be fabricated or extracted by the Obsidian Order in this source, so they cannot independently establish identity when contradicted by genetic/provenance evidence. SS03,SS07-SS10.

## Cross-work hypothesis tests

Hypothesis A — institutions may manipulate memory/records to preserve political or social systems.
Support: Dominion simulation; Symbiosis Commission erasure of Joran; Obsidian Order false-Iliana operation.
Counterevidence: mechanisms and goals differ radically and require separate provenance/frame predicates. Status PROVISIONAL.

Hypothesis B — social/biographical identity can remain meaningful after literal identity claims are falsified.
Support: Ghemor's continuing kin-like bond with Kira; Quark's temporary House membership; Odo's chosen bond with solids despite Founder origin.
Counterevidence: these are different social relations and should not be promoted to biological/legal sameness. Status PROVISIONAL.

Hypothesis C — hidden institutional facts can be exposed through incompatible independent evidence.
Support: Joran record-compression/history mismatch; Kira genetic test versus Iliana narrative; simulation reveal versus apparent treaty events.
Counterevidence: not every contradiction produces a unique explanation until provenance is resolved. Status PROVISIONAL.
