## The Abandoned

Evidence:
A01 A rapidly maturing humanoid found in Gamma Quadrant wreckage is genetically engineered; cognitive development and cellular growth are artificially accelerated.
A02 Genetic/physiological evidence identifies him as Jem'Hadar and reveals dependence on an isogenic drug/enzyme supplied through Dominion engineering.
A03 Starfleet initially plans transfer to specialists; Odo objects to treating a sentient individual as a laboratory specimen and assumes responsibility for attempting social alternatives.
A04 The youth shows strong deference to Odo/changelings and explicit violent drives; Odo teaches restraint, equality and independent choice while acknowledging Founder responsibility for Jem'Hadar design.
A05 Odo compares the youth's engineered role with his own intended Founder role and Kira's Resistance conditioning, arguing origin/design does not guarantee present choice.
A06 The youth nevertheless increasingly identifies non-Jem'Hadar as enemies and says he wants to live with his people.
A07 Facing forced Starfleet transfer, the youth demands departure; Odo offers unexplored space and continued mentorship, but the youth chooses return to the Gamma Quadrant/Jem'Hadar.
A08 Sisko permits Odo to take him back rather than force transfer, partly because preventing departure would likely require lethal force.
A09 Odo later concedes Kira's warning that he could not simply reshape the youth's genetically conditioned identity/behavior.

Assertions:
AA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — The youth is a sentient local individual whose Jem'Hadar biology includes engineered growth, dependency and aggression/deference traits. A01-A04.
AA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Odo's intervention supplies evidence that engineered predisposition and social identity are analytically distinct, even though this individual ultimately chooses Jem'Hadar affiliation. A04-A09.
AA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Return to the Gamma Quadrant is the youth's expressed preference after alternatives are offered; it is not simply Odo delivering passive property back to the Dominion. A06-A08.
AA04 PROPOSED/UNRESOLVED — The source does not establish how much of the youth's final preference is genetically compelled versus autonomously endorsed. A02-A09.

## Civil Defense

Evidence:
C01 Accidental activation of an old Cardassian labor-control program identifies present occupants as rebelling Bajoran workers and seals the station.
C02 Recorded Dukat messages escalate from surrender demands to neurocine-gas threats and stationwide counterinsurgency measures.
C03 Jake's physical access through ore-processing infrastructure allows escape from the first sealed area, which the system interprets as further rebellion and escalates.
C04 Cardassian-era security architecture includes force fields, lethal gas, hostage-family logic and a final self-destruct contingency if Bajorans seize the station.
C05 Garak's surviving Cardassian access code permits limited movement but lacks authority to shut down Dukat's top-level program.
C06 Present Dukat arrives after receiving his own prerecorded distress signal and initially tries to trade rescue for restoration of a permanent Cardassian garrison.
C07 Kira rejects the coerced bargain and states she would destroy the station rather than surrender it to Cardassian control.
C08 When Dukat attempts to leave, another prerecorded Cardassian authority message identifies his departure during a revolt as cowardice, rescinds his codes and traps him too.
C09 Present personnel exploit station systems, old emitters, shielding and reactor controls to survive the final automated destruct sequence.
C10 Odo notes that occupation-era Cardassian authorities considered their own security chief a security risk, preserving evidence that the coercive system distrusted collaborators/officials as well as Bajoran workers.

Assertions:
CA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — The crisis is caused by an automated occupation-era counterinsurgency program applying obsolete role classifications to present inhabitants. C01-C09.
CA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — The program embeds progressively lethal collective-punishment logic rather than calibrated present-time threat assessment. C02-C04.
CA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Present Dukat did not intentionally trigger the crisis and eventually becomes subject to his own era's higher-level coercive safeguards. C06-C09.
CA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Historical Cardassian security architecture is itself source evidence about occupation governance, not merely a neutral technical malfunction. C02-C04,C10.

## Meridian

Evidence:
M01 Meridian periodically alternates between corporeal space and a noncorporeal dimension; residents describe existence there as pure consciousness and age only while corporeal.
M02 The shrinking corporeal interval threatens eventual failure to return, population decline and extinction; Defiant scientific work identifies stellar/quantum factors and a way to stabilize future cycles.
M03 Jadzia and Deral form a rapid reciprocal relationship; Deral initially plans to leave Meridian, then feels obligation toward the small recovering community.
M04 Jadzia independently proposes altering her quantum state so she can remain through Meridian's dimensional transition, accepting a proposed sixty-year absence from Starfleet/DS9.
M05 Sisko tests whether she has considered the decision and explicitly distinguishes Jadzia's judgment from Curzon's recurrent impulsive romances.
M06 During the transition Jadzia's altered presence creates a singularity/anchor that destabilizes Meridian's matrix and threatens the planet.
M07 The Defiant beams Jadzia away; Meridian then completes the shift normally, leaving her separated from Deral for approximately sixty years.
M08 In a separate plot, Tiron commissions Quark to create a sexualized Kira holographic simulation; Quark obtains Kira's biometric/personnel data without consent.
M09 Kira/Odo discover the data theft and subvert the program, providing direct evidence that a simulation using someone's likeness is not the person depicted.

Assertions:
MA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Meridian's residents and planet undergo recurring substrate/state transition; their own testimony describes continuity across noncorporeal intervals but the mechanism remains only partly understood. M01-M02.
MA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Jadzia's attempted transition is voluntary and identity-continuity conscious, but fails for material compatibility reasons rather than because command forbids it. M03-M07.
MA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — The Kira holosuite image is an unauthorized simulation derived from copied personal data, not a local occurrence of Kira herself. M08-M09.

## Defiant

Evidence:
D01 A man presenting as William Riker gains Kira's trust/access and steals the Defiant using valid physical/security characteristics.
D02 Starfleet identifies him as Thomas Riker, created by an earlier transporter accident that produced two physically/DNA-identical William T. Rikers; Thomas uses a different name to distinguish himself.
D03 The simultaneous histories of William and Thomas establish divergent causal/social careers despite shared pre-duplication history and DNA.
D04 Thomas is now Maquis-aligned and says deception/use of Kira was operational necessity.
D05 Thomas's objective is not ordinary border raiding but investigation of suspected covert Cardassian military activity in the Orias system.
D06 Kira challenges both his tactics and a self-concept driven by comparison with William, suggesting the mission partly functions as an attempt to distinguish himself.
D07 Obsidian Order representative Korinas blocks Central Command access to Orias and states the Order will destroy Cardassian ships that enter, despite legal prohibition on Order military equipment.
D08 Defiant sensors reveal Keldon-class ships associated with the protected Orias activity, supplying material evidence that a secret Cardassian force exists even though its full purpose remains unknown.
D09 Sisko/Dukat negotiate surrender: Kira, the Defiant and other Maquis return to Federation custody/trial, while Thomas accepts Cardassian conviction and life imprisonment rather than death.
D10 Thomas turns command over to Kira and surrenders to protect his crew once the Orias information is secured.

Assertions:
DA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Thomas Riker and William Riker are distinct post-duplication local entities sharing pre-accident causal history and identical DNA, not one uninterrupted individual. D01-D03.
DA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Thomas's Maquis mission produces genuine evidence of secret Obsidian Order military activity; his illegal conduct does not make the discovered evidence false. D05-D09.
DA03 PROPOSED/UNRESOLVED — Full purpose/composition of the Orias force remains unresolved at this work's endpoint. D07-D09.
DA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Thomas ultimately accepts personal punishment to secure release of Kira/crew and preserve the sensor evidence. D09-D10.

## Fascination

Evidence:
F01 During the Gratitude Festival, several characters suddenly express anomalous attractions and relationship behaviors inconsistent with their ordinary positions.
F02 Jake develops attraction to Kira; Bareil pursues Dax; Dax pursues Sisko; Bashir/Kira show reciprocal compulsion; these episodes cluster around social proximity to Lwaxana Troi.
F03 Initial medical examinations show ordinary vital signs and no intoxication/infection in affected individuals, delaying causal identification.
F04 Odo remains emotionally interested in Kira but is not under the same projected compulsion; Lwaxana is herself strongly focused on Odo.
F05 Bashir diagnoses Zanthi fever in Lwaxana, a condition of mature Betazoids that causes empathic projection of emotion onto nearby persons with pre-existing latent attraction.
F06 Antiviral treatment resolves the condition and affected individuals recover within roughly a day.
F07 Keiko is separately experiencing real relationship strain with Miles over an extended Bajoran survey; she is not affected by Zanthi fever and their reconciliation discussion remains distinct from the projected-affect incidents.
F08 Odo later clarifies to Lwaxana that his feelings for Kira are not caused by the fever, preserving a distinction between authentic unreciprocated attraction and externally amplified/projection-driven behavior.

Assertions:
FA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — The anomalous festival attractions are causally supported as Zanthi-fever empathic projections acting on latent attraction, not durable consent or relationship-state changes by themselves. F01-F06.
FA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Behavior during projected affect should remain altered-state evidence and not automatically update ordinary romantic/consensual relationship assertions. F01-F06.
FA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Odo's attraction to Kira and Miles/Keiko's conflict remain independently supported rather than artifacts of the fever. F07-F08.

## Cross-work hypothesis tests

Hypothesis A — DS9 repeatedly distinguishes strong predisposition from settled person identity/agency.
Support: Jem'Hadar genetic programming; Thomas Riker post-duplicate divergence; Zanthi-fever affect acting on latent attraction.
Counterevidence: mechanisms differ and no single autonomy predicate should collapse genetics, causal duplication and transient affect. Status PROVISIONAL.

Hypothesis B — systems built for prior political orders can continue exerting coercive agency-like effects after those orders end.
Support: Civil Defense automation; earlier occupation records/security systems.
Counterevidence: automated execution is not independent moral agency and remains attributable to historical designers/rules. Status PROVISIONAL.

Hypothesis C — copied likeness/biology does not alone establish global identity.
Support: Thomas/William transporter duplicate; unauthorized Kira holosuite representation.
Counterevidence: duplicate person and simulation are materially different relations requiring separate typing. Status PROVISIONAL.
