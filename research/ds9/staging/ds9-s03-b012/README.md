## Past Tense, Part I

Evidence:
P101 A transporter accident involving a micro-singularity and polarized chroniton particles displaces Sisko, Bashir and Dax to San Francisco in 2024.
P102 Dax lands separately with her commbadge and is aided by Chris Brynner; Sisko/Bashir are detained for lacking identification and placed in a Sanctuary District.
P103 Sanctuary Districts confine unemployed, homeless and mentally ill people behind walls; conditions include overcrowding, inadequate care and weak routes back into ordinary society.
P104 Sisko recognizes the period immediately before the Bell Riots as historically pivotal and initially insists on noninterference.
P105 Gabriel Bell intervenes in violence involving Sisko/Bashir and is stabbed to death in circumstances altered by their presence.
P106 Sisko concludes Bell's death threatens the survival of hostages and the historical consequences of the riots; the Defiant simultaneously observes a changed future in which Federation/Starfleet infrastructure has disappeared.
P107 Sisko changes strategy from noninterference to active restoration of the historically necessary outcome.
P108 As the Processing Centre is seized, Sisko publicly identifies himself as Gabriel Bell.

Assertions:
P1A01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Bell's death is causally linked to the displaced officers' presence and produces a material timeline divergence. P104-P108.
P1A02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Sisko's later use of Bell's identity is deliberate historical substitution after Bell's death, not evidence that Sisko and Bell are literally the same person. P105-P108.
P1A03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Sanctuary District suffering is depicted as institutional exclusion/neglect rather than simply individual criminality or pathology. P102-P103.

## Past Tense, Part II

Evidence:
P201 Sisko continues publicly as “Gabriel Bell” while privately coordinating with Bashir to preserve hostages and the historically required political effect of the riots.
P202 Sisko restrains BC's violence and works with Webb to formulate demands centered on restoring access to employment, housing and social participation.
P203 Dax locates Sisko/Bashir through Sanctuary records and obtains network/media access through Brynner.
P204 Residents are given direct public network access to tell their stories, broadening outside awareness of Sanctuary conditions.
P205 National Guard forces storm the district; Webb and BC die, Sisko is wounded, and the protected hostages survive.
P206 Vin and Bernardo help Sisko/Bashir leave under substituted casualty/identity records and promise to preserve what actually happened.
P207 Kira/O'Brien recover the displaced officers and the familiar Federation timeline returns.
P208 Historical records now display Sisko's image as Gabriel Bell, demonstrating that restoration preserved the social/historical role while altering the biographical image/identity record.

Assertions:
P2A01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Sisko performs Bell's historical role without becoming Bell; role continuity, historical causal function and personal identity diverge. P201-P208.
P2A02 PROPOSED/STABLE — The restored timeline is not byte-for-byte/biographically identical to the original history because Bell's historical image is replaced by Sisko's. P206-P208.
P2A03 PROPOSED/STABLE — The work depicts public testimony/network access as a material causal component of the riots' political legacy. P203-P205.
P2A04 PROPOSED/UNRESOLVED — The exact degree to which every original 2024 event was restored remains unknowable from the characters' surviving historical comparison. P205-P208.

## Life Support

Evidence:
L01 Bareil suffers catastrophic injuries during transport to Cardassian-Bajoran peace negotiations and is revived through aggressive neurogenic treatment.
L02 Bashir recommends stasis to preserve Bareil while safer treatment becomes possible; Bareil refuses because he wants to complete the negotiations.
L03 Bareil voluntarily accepts vasokin despite substantial risk of organ damage; the drug keeps him functional long enough to continue work.
L04 Vasokin causes severe internal-organ and later brain damage; artificial organ replacement initially succeeds.
L05 Bashir asks Winn to release Bareil from perceived obligation; Winn refuses to interfere with Bareil's choice while also retaining an obvious political interest in the negotiations.
L06 After irreversible brain damage, Kira and Winn support a partial positronic brain implant based on their interpretation of Bareil's wishes; Bashir warns the person who wakes may not be the same in familiar experiential terms.
L07 After partial replacement Bareil remains awake, retains negotiation knowledge and can advise Winn, but describes touch as a distant memory rather than ordinary sensation.
L08 The peace treaty is signed.
L09 When Bareil's remaining organic brain fails, Bashir refuses total replacement, saying the last “spark of life” would be lost and a fully machine replacement would not be Bareil.
L10 Kira disagrees but ultimately accepts Bashir's decision and stays with Bareil until expected brain death.

Assertions:
LA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Bareil repeatedly exercises treatment autonomy under severe medical risk; his decisions are influenced by religious/political obligation but are not depicted as simple unconscious incapacity. L02-L07.
LA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Partial positronic replacement preserves substantial memory/task function while altering reported sensory experience. L06-L07.
LA03 PROPOSED/CONTESTED — Bashir draws a personhood/identity boundary at total brain replacement. This is source evidence of Bashir's medical-ethical judgment, not a corpus-wide metaphysical rule. L09-L10.
LA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Winn has both institutional need and political self-interest in Bareil's continued participation; those interests do not erase Bareil's own expressed choice. L02-L08.

## Heart of Stone

Evidence:
H01 Odo and Kira pursue a Maquis suspect to a moon; apparent Kira becomes trapped in a rapidly growing crystalline structure.
H02 Repeated technical attempts to free her fail and the apparent threat escalates toward death.
H03 During the ordeal Odo confesses his love for Kira; apparent Kira eventually claims reciprocal love.
H04 Odo becomes suspicious because the apparent Kira's physical position makes aspects of the phaser/crystal interaction inconsistent and because her reciprocal declaration conflicts with his accumulated knowledge of Kira's behavior.
H05 The apparent Kira is revealed as the Female Changeling impersonating her; the true Kira has been detained elsewhere and is unharmed.
H06 The Founder says the entire scenario was constructed to learn why Odo chooses solids over the Great Link.
H07 Odo threatens that harming Kira could make him the first changeling to harm another changeling but does not carry out that threat.
H08 Odo rescues the real Kira and does not tell her that he confessed love during the false-Kira scenario.
H09 In a separate plot Nog asks Sisko to sponsor his Starfleet Academy application; he explains he does not want to repeat Rom's economic dependence despite believing Rom is a gifted engineer.
H10 Sisko tests Nog's commitment and ultimately recommends him; Rom supports Nog while Quark opposes the choice.

Assertions:
HA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — The trapped/reciprocal-love Kira is a Founder impersonation; her declarations must not update the real Kira's relationship state. H01-H06.
HA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Odo's love confession is an authentic utterance by Odo produced inside a deceptive scenario, so the utterance remains evidence about Odo even though its addressee was not Kira. H03-H08.
HA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Odo detects the deception by combining material inconsistency with relationship/behavioral evidence. H04-H05.
HA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Nog's Starfleet decision is explicitly framed as an attempt to pursue aptitude/identity beyond Ferengi commercial expectations. H09-H10.

## Destiny

Evidence:
D01 Federation and Cardassian scientists cooperate on a communications relay intended to operate through the wormhole.
D02 Defrocked Vedek Yarka cites Trakor's Third Prophecy involving a river, three vipers, a Sword of Stars, the Emissary and the opening/burning of heaven's gates.
D03 Three Cardassian scientists arrive, followed by discovery of a comet described as the Sword of Stars; Sisko becomes concerned that contemporary events may correspond to the prophecy.
D04 Odo warns that both Sisko and Yarka may be interpreting ambiguous events through prior expectations.
D05 Kira states continuing belief in Sisko as the Emissary while also working within Starfleet/scientific explanations.
D06 The relay experiment perturbs the wormhole and alters the comet's trajectory; Cardassian modeling had identified a small risk that was not disclosed because of military reporting rules.
D07 O'Brien's first attempt to destroy/divert the comet is sabotaged. Technical/DNA evidence identifies Dejar as an Obsidian Order agent opposed to Bajoran-Cardassian peace and the relay project.
D08 The comet divides into three fragments, creating a concrete correspondence with the “three vipers” imagery.
D09 Sisko/Kira use a shuttle subspace field to carry/divert the fragments through the wormhole; leaked silithium forms a subspace filament that ultimately makes stable relay communication possible.
D10 Yarka and believers interpret the completed sequence as fulfillment of Trakor's prophecy and endorsement of peace; the physical sequence itself is independently observable even though its prophetic/metaphysical interpretation remains interpretive.

Assertions:
DA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Multiple observed events correspond strikingly to elements of the recorded prophecy: comet/Sword, three fragments/vipers, Emissary decision and a wormhole-changing silithium event. D02-D10.
DA02 PROPOSED/CONTESTED — The correspondence supports serious prophetic interpretation within the story but does not by itself prove a unique causal mechanism or that Trakor possessed exhaustive future knowledge. D02-D10.
DA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Dejar sabotages the project as an Obsidian Order operation opposed to peace/cooperation. D07.
DA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Odo explicitly supplies confirmation-bias counterevidence against premature prophetic matching. D04.

## Cross-work hypothesis tests

Hypothesis A — identity roles can be historically or socially transferred without literal person identity transferring.
Support: Sisko performing Bell's role; Founder impersonating Kira; earlier Li Nalas legend.
Counterevidence: mechanisms differ and require separate predicates (historical substitution, shapeshifting impersonation, legend). Status PROVISIONAL.

Hypothesis B — source characters repeatedly confront evidence that seems metaphysical while maintaining multiple explanatory frames.
Support: Destiny prophecy correspondence; Orb/Prophet evidence in earlier batches; Meridian state transition.
Counterevidence: some mechanisms later receive material explanation, while others remain interpretively open. Status PROVISIONAL.

Hypothesis C — preserving functional continuity does not automatically settle person continuity.
Support: Bareil partial positronic replacement; Sisko/Bell historical role replacement; apparent-Kira simulation/deception.
Counterevidence: these involve brain replacement, historical role and impersonation respectively and must not be collapsed. Status PROVISIONAL.
