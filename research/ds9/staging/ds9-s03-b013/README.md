## Prophet Motive

Evidence:
PM01 Grand Nagus Zek arrives with radically revised Rules of Acquisition promoting refunds, friendship, dignity, generosity and benevolence.
PM02 Bashir finds no ordinary disease, trauma or endocrine/neurological defect explaining Zek's changed values.
PM03 Quark discovers Zek recently obtained a Bajoran Orb, entered the wormhole and sought future knowledge for commercial advantage.
PM04 Quark's Orb experience directs him toward the inference that the wormhole entities changed Zek.
PM05 Inside the wormhole the entities explicitly confirm that Zek tried to learn future outcomes for gain, that they found Ferengi acquisitiveness aggressive/adversarial, and that they altered Zek toward an earlier less acquisitive state.
PM06 The entities threaten to similarly alter Quark; Quark bargains that leaving Ferengi unchanged will reduce future Ferengi intrusions/contact.
PM07 The entities restore Zek to his prior acquisitive behavioral state; Zek immediately resumes profit-seeking and orders destruction of the revised Rules.
PM08 Rom separately embezzles money from the benevolent association, demonstrating that Zek's alteration did not automatically alter other Ferengi.

Assertions:
PMA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Zek's temporary behavioral/value transformation is explicitly caused by intervention from the wormhole entities rather than ordinary illness. PM02-PM07.
PMA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — The entities characterize their intervention as restoration to an earlier Ferengi state, but that historical/evolutionary characterization is their testimony and should not be treated as a complete species history without separate support. PM05.
PMA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Zek before alteration, altered Zek and restored Zek are one continuing local individual with externally modified dispositions; the work does not depict simultaneous duplicate persons. PM01-PM07.
PMA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Quark successfully changes the entities' decision through bargaining framed around future unwanted contact, supplying direct evidence that linguistic persuasion can influence their actions. PM05-PM07.

## Visionary

Evidence:
V01 O'Brien receives radiation poisoning during a technical accident while a quantum singularity later associated with a cloaked Romulan warbird orbits DS9.
V02 At intervals he shifts roughly five hours into the future and observes future events, including his own death and later destruction of the station.
V03 Future medical information allows O'Brien to return and instruct Bashir to perform a basilar arterial scan, preventing the death he observed.
V04 Odo's investigation independently identifies Klingon operatives spying on the visiting Romulan delegation; this plot is initially a plausible candidate explanation for danger but is not the cause of station destruction.
V05 To learn the disaster's cause O'Brien deliberately accepts additional radiation risk and travels to the future.
V06 In that future a Romulan warbird decloaks, attacks DS9 and causes the catastrophic sequence; the same singularity in its warp system is identified as the source triggering the time shifts.
V07 The irradiated O'Brien cannot survive another shift, so the future O'Brien activates the return mechanism and travels into the earlier present carrying the warning.
V08 The surviving present-timeline O'Brien warns Sisko, enabling shields/weapons preparation and forcing the Romulans to depart without carrying out the observed attack.
V09 Sisko infers the Romulan motive is to destroy DS9 and collapse the wormhole because of fear of the Dominion; the Romulan representative never confesses this plan, so motive remains strongly inferred rather than directly admitted.
V10 Afterward the surviving O'Brien expresses discomfort that the life he occupies may belong to “the other Miles O'Brien”; Bashir answers that he remains Miles with memories the other did not have.

Assertions:
VA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Temporal information from witnessed future events changes present actions and prevents at least some previously observed outcomes. V02-V08.
VA02 PROPOSED/UNRESOLVED — The O'Brien who continues after the episode has a causal path through the future while an earlier temporal version dies; local temporal-self relations must be preserved rather than silently treated as an eventless reset. V02-V10.
VA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — The Romulan warbird/singularity is causally supported as the trigger for O'Brien's time shifts. V01,V06.
VA04 PROPOSED/CONTESTED — Sisko's specific explanation of the Romulans' strategic motive is an evidence-backed inference, not a confessed world-state fact. V08-V09.

## Distant Voices

Evidence:
D01 Altovar seeks restricted biomimetic gel and telepathically attacks Bashir after refusal.
D02 Bashir enters a telepathically induced coma; real-world medical voices intermittently penetrate his subjective experience.
D03 Within the coma Bashir experiences an increasingly damaged/empty DS9 and rapidly ages while familiar people behave unlike their ordinary selves.
D04 Bashir eventually recognizes that the subjective station represents his mind and that several familiar figures embody aspects of his personality: confidence/adventure, aggression, suspicion/fear, pessimism and professional skill.
D05 Subjective persons in the mindscape possess no independent life signs when Bashir scans them; their behavior changes with the condition of the mindscape.
D06 Bashir concludes the hostile Lethean presence represents/involves the actual telepathic injury rather than simply another self-aspect.
D07 The hostile presence uses Bashir's private memories and insecurities, including tennis ambitions, medical-school rank and feelings toward Dax, to encourage surrender/death.
D08 Bashir rejects the imposed self-interpretation, reasserts chosen identity as a doctor/friend and uses control of his own mindscape to defeat the hostile presence.
D09 Bashir wakes; the real Altovar had triggered station security and was arrested by Odo. Bashir's physical age is normal and the subjective decades are not external elapsed time.
D10 Real Garak later discusses the mindscape role assigned to his likeness, emphasizing the distinction between real Garak and Bashir's internal representation.

Assertions:
DA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Most DS9 characters experienced during Bashir's coma are internal mindscape representations rather than external occurrences of those persons. D02-D10.
DA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Subjective aging and station destruction are features of the telepathically induced mindscape, not external world-state changes. D03-D09.
DA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Bashir's self-descriptions inside the mindscape are evidence of his own self-concept and conflict, not objective proof that each interpreted trait exhausts the real person represented. D04-D10.
DA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Altovar's telepathic attack has real physiological danger even though most experienced events are internal. D01-D09.

## Through the Looking Glass

Evidence:
T01 Mirror O'Brien abducts prime Sisko into the alternate universe and says mirror Benjamin Sisko has died while leading the Terran rebellion.
T02 Rebels ask prime Sisko to impersonate the dead mirror Sisko long enough to complete a mission to recruit/rescue mirror Jennifer Sisko, whose sensor project could expose rebel hiding places.
T03 Prime Sisko knows Jennifer resembles his deceased prime wife but explicitly recognizes she is a different person in a different universe.
T04 To maintain the impersonation he adopts expected behavior toward mirror counterparts, including relationships/social roles that belonged to mirror Sisko.
T05 Jennifer initially rejects him based on her history with mirror Sisko and support for ending rebellion violence; prime Sisko persuades her that Alliance domination will not produce genuine peace.
T06 Jennifer chooses to join/assist the rebels; the choice is made before she knows the man persuading her is a prime-universe counterpart rather than her husband.
T07 Prime Sisko uses knowledge of Cardassian station architecture plus a self-destruct bluff/control-code change to force escape from the Intendant.
T08 Jennifer later learns mirror Sisko is dead and that prime Sisko is a different counterpart; she remains with the rebellion rather than treating the deception as proof she is her counterpart's wife.
T09 Prime Sisko returns to his universe; mirror Jennifer, O'Brien, Dax and other rebel counterparts remain in theirs.

Assertions:
TA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Prime Sisko and mirror Sisko are distinct local entities; prime Sisko temporarily impersonates the deceased counterpart for a mission. T01-T09.
TA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Prime Jennifer and mirror Jennifer are distinct despite physical/name correspondence and relationship structures. T03,T08-T09.
TA03 PROPOSED/CONTESTED — Jennifer's decision to aid the rebellion occurs under material identity deception about Sisko, even though the political arguments she accepts can be independently evaluated. T02-T08.
TA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Cross-universe counterpart resemblance provides strategic impersonation capability but is not identity equivalence. T01-T09.

## Improbable Cause

Evidence:
I01 An explosion in Garak's shop initially resembles a power-conduit accident; nitrilin residue and a pheromonic sensor support deliberate assassination design.
I02 A Flaxian assassin is investigated because his methods/materials fit the scene; after departure his ship is destroyed by a Romulan-style forced neutrino inversion.
I03 Romulan representatives admit destroying the Flaxian but give an independent legal-criminal explanation and deny/avoid linkage to Garak; Odo correctly treats the assassination attribution as unresolved at that stage.
I04 Cardassian intelligence contacts report five other former Obsidian Order operatives associated with Enabran Tain died in suspiciously clustered “accidents” or natural causes.
I05 Odo deduces Garak himself blew up the shop after spotting the Flaxian, using the apparent assassination attempt to force Odo into investigating a broader threat Garak could not safely expose directly.
I06 Garak admits the dead operatives were former trusted Tain associates and travels with Odo toward Tain's secret refuge.
I07 A Romulan warbird captures them; Tain reveals he is directing a joint Tal Shiar/Obsidian Order fleet secretly built in the Orias system.
I08 Tain says the fleet plans a pre-emptive strike on the Founders' homeworld, believing killing the Founders in one blow will collapse the Dominion.
I09 Tain admits he ordered elimination of former associates because he intends to resume command afterward and feared their knowledge could threaten him.
I10 Tain offers Garak restoration to his old role despite having exiled him and recently ordered his death; Garak chooses to join him, while Odo remains prisoner.

Assertions:
IA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Garak destroys his own shop to trigger Odo's investigation after detecting a genuine Flaxian threat. I01-I06.
IA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — The Orias military buildup seen in `Defiant` is now identified as a secret joint Obsidian Order/Tal Shiar fleet, providing cross-work provenance continuity without implying accepted global reconciliation. I07-I08.
IA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Tain orders the purge of former associates as internal security for his return to power, not because each was independently proven to be a Dominion agent. I04,I09.
IA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Garak's choice to rejoin Tain demonstrates attachment to former identity/status despite Tain's exile and kill order; motive includes explicit personal loyalty/longing but should not be reduced to a single cause. I06-I10.

## Cross-work hypothesis tests

Hypothesis A — altered subjective worlds can have real evidentiary value without being external world-state events.
Support: Bashir mindscape; prior Dominion simulation; Orb/Prophet encounters.
Counterevidence: Visionary future observations involve actual temporal displacement with externally actionable information rather than a private symbolic experience. Status PROVISIONAL.

Hypothesis B — identity continuity can be strained by temporal/counterpart substitution without producing one uniform relation.
Support: surviving future-path O'Brien, prime/mirror Sisko, prior Thomas Riker and O'Brien replicant.
Counterevidence: temporal self, alternate counterpart, transporter duplicate and artificial replicant are causally different mechanisms. Status PROVISIONAL.

Hypothesis C — intelligence actors repeatedly manipulate provenance to control what investigators can know.
Support: Garak's staged shop explosion, Romulan cover explanation, Tain's purge, earlier Obsidian Order identity operations.
Counterevidence: deliberate deception does not make every intelligence-source statement false; independent material evidence can corroborate particular claims. Status PROVISIONAL.
