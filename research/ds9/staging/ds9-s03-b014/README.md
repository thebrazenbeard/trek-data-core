## The Die Is Cast

Evidence:
DC01 Tain's joint Obsidian Order/Tal Shiar fleet travels to the presumed Founder homeworld intending a pre-emptive exterminating strike.
DC02 Garak is ordered to interrogate Odo and uses an Order prototype stasis device that prevents shapeshifting/regeneration, producing severe pain and physical deterioration.
DC03 Under torture Odo finally discloses a private desire to return to his people and experience the Great Link again.
DC04 Garak later tells Tain Odo revealed nothing, deliberately withholding the confession rather than passing it upward.
DC05 Planet sensors apparently show liquid lifeforms while bombardment fails to reduce the count; the readings prove to be transmitter-generated deception and the planet is deserted.
DC06 A massive Jem'Hadar force appears after the fleet commits to orbit, demonstrating the attack was anticipated and trapped.
DC07 Colonel Lovok reveals himself as a Founder/changeling impersonating the Romulan commander. He says Tain originated the strike plan but the Founders learned of it and deliberately helped carry it forward to destroy two Alpha Quadrant intelligence services.
DC08 Founder-Lovok releases Odo because no changeling has harmed another, invites him to the Link, and Odo refuses.
DC09 Garak attempts to rescue Tain because of personal debt/attachment despite exile and a recent kill order; Tain refuses escape and admits pride overrode his old instincts.
DC10 Eddington's earlier Defiant sabotage is revealed as obedience to Admiral Toddman's order preventing Sisko's unauthorized pursuit; Sisko confines him during crisis and later restores him to duty because security expertise is needed.

Assertions:
DCA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — The Lovok commanding the operation is a Founder impersonation; the identity/status of the actual Romulan Lovok is not established by this work. DC05-DC08.
DCA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — The fleet's annihilation is a Founder counterintelligence trap; the apparent planetary life-sign evidence was fabricated. DC05-DC07.
DCA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Odo's tortured confession establishes a desire for the Great Link, not political consent to Founder/Dominion governance. DC02-DC08.
DCA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Garak tortures Odo under pressure to prove restored loyalty, yet separately protects Odo's disclosed secret from Tain. DC02-DC04.
DCA05 PROPOSED/STABLE — Tain genuinely originated the attack concept even though the Founders infiltrated/facilitated its execution. DC07.

## Explorers

Evidence:
EX01 Sisko builds a working replica of an ancient Bajoran solar-sail vessel from old plans and deliberately uses period-appropriate tools/technology except for a gravity net.
EX02 He intends to test whether ancient Bajorans could plausibly have sailed from Bajor toward Cardassia; Cardassian officials, especially Dukat, express skepticism.
EX03 Jake initially declines the trip because of his own social life, then joins after receiving a writing-fellowship offer from Pennington School and wanting time with his father.
EX04 Jake shows Sisko fiction he wrote; Sisko gives substantive criticism and strongly encourages him to keep writing.
EX05 Jake says he will defer Pennington for a year, initially partly from concern that Sisko will be lonely and later because he wants more lived experience before leaving DS9.
EX06 A tachyon eddy interacting with the unusually large sail-area-to-mass ratio accelerates the ship to warp-like velocity and carries it into the Cardassian system.
EX07 Their arrival materially demonstrates the ancient design could traverse the route under naturally occurring tachyon conditions.
EX08 Dukat announces that Cardassia has just discovered archaeological remains of an ancient Bajoran solar vessel and gives the travelers a celebratory reception; the extraordinary timing prompts Sisko's skeptical reaction but the archaeological claim is not independently tested in this work.
EX09 Bashir discovers he and valedictorian Elizabeth Lense had each idealized the other's career. Lense had mistaken another Andorian student for Bashir and later says she envied Bashir's long-term Bajor work.
EX10 Lense's testimony undercuts Bashir's assumption that she deliberately ignored him and demonstrates reciprocal career envy based on incomplete information.

Assertions:
EXA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Sisko/Jake's voyage establishes practical plausibility of the ancient Bajoran solar-vessel route under tachyon-eddy conditions. EX01-EX07.
EXA02 PROPOSED/CONTESTED — Cardassia's conveniently timed archaeological discovery supports the ancient-contact claim but lacks independent verification inside the episode. EX08.
EXA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Jake's Pennington deferral is his stated choice and should not be reduced solely to dependence on Sisko; he gives both concern for his father and desire for more experience. EX03-EX05.
EXA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Bashir's interpretation of Lense's avoidance is falsified by direct conversation; neither career matched the other's imagined prestige. EX09-EX10.

## Family Business

Evidence:
FB01 FCA Liquidator Brunt serves Quark with a Writ of Accountability because Ferengi law holds the eldest male responsible for a female family member's prohibited profit-making.
FB02 Ishka openly wears clothes, addresses male strangers and admits investing her stipend, directly violating multiple gendered Ferengi restrictions described in the source.
FB03 Ishka initially admits only three bars of profit, while Quark later discovers a much larger financial network run through many aliases that could financially ruin him under restitution law.
FB04 Ishka says the issue is not need for money but her ability/right to participate in commerce; she also says Keldar's business failures were mitigated by her own uncredited financial competence.
FB05 Rom corroborates that their father repeatedly failed commercially and that Ishka's contribution kept the family afloat after Quark had left home.
FB06 Quark attempts to report the full enterprise to the FCA after a family confrontation; Rom lies that Ishka will split profits fifty-fifty to force Quark back into negotiation.
FB07 Ishka ultimately signs the confession and pays restitution because Quark is her son, but later tells Rom she surrendered only one-third of her actual profits and concealed the remainder successfully.
FB08 Quark bribes Brunt after the confession, reasoning that public admission of the scale of a female's commercial success would embarrass the FCA/social order.
FB09 The episode therefore directly depicts institutional proof of Ishka's business competence coexisting with legal prohibition grounded in sex/gender hierarchy.
FB10 In the parallel plot Jake successfully introduces Sisko and freighter captain Kasidy Yates; they independently discover a shared enthusiasm for baseball and choose to continue seeing each other.

Assertions:
FBA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Ferengi exclusion of women from profit-making is institutionally enforced and directly contradicted by Ishka's demonstrated commercial competence. FB01-FB09.
FBA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Quark benefits from and eventually helps conceal the very female commercial success that contradicts the social rules he defends. FB03-FB08.
FBA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Ishka's confession is strategically incomplete; it does not establish full financial disclosure or abandonment of her commercial activity. FB07-FB08.
FBA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Rom's loyalty to Ishka includes deliberate deception of Quark to force reconciliation; the false profit-sharing claim should remain false testimony even though it produces a family conversation. FB05-FB07.

## Facets

Evidence:
FC01 During Jadzia's zhian'tara, a Guardian temporarily removes individual prior-host memory sets from Dax/Jadzia and transfers them into conscious volunteer bodies.
FC02 Volunteers remain conscious and can reassert control, while prior-host memories/personality expression temporarily dominate behavior.
FC03 Lela embodied through Kira remembers her own initiate history while Jadzia temporarily cannot access Lela's memories, directly demonstrating controlled memory partition/transfer.
FC04 Joran embodied through Sisko is physically contained because of violent history; Joran attempts intimidation/manipulation while Sisko can later reassert himself.
FC05 Quark is deceived into participation and receives Audrid's memories/personality expression despite his objections to acting as host for a woman.
FC06 Curzon's memories transferred into Odo produce an unusual combined state: the entity calls itself both Odo and Curzon, and the Guardian treats shapeshifter physiology as enabling a joining-like blend rather than ordinary temporary possession.
FC07 Curzon-Odo and Odo jointly decide at first to retain the blended state permanently, which would leave Jadzia without Curzon's memories; the Guardian says Curzon's memories cannot be removed without cooperation.
FC08 Jadzia confronts Curzon. Curzon admits he washed Jadzia out of the initiate program partly because he had fallen in love with her and felt shame/conflict as her supervisor, not because she lacked merit.
FC09 Jadzia rejects Curzon-Odo's attempt to keep the memories, says Curzon is part of her, and Curzon ultimately returns to Dax/Jadzia.
FC10 After reintegration Jadzia retains Curzon's memories of what it felt like to be a changeling; Odo retains experiential understanding of Curzon/humanoid pleasures, demonstrating reciprocal memory/experience consequences despite restoration of separate ordinary identities.
FC11 In the parallel plot Quark secretly reconfigures Nog's spatial-orientation test so Nog fails; Rom exposes the sabotage, Sisko permits retesting, and Nog passes and continues toward Starfleet Academy.

Assertions:
FCA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Ordinary zhian'tara embodiments are temporary memory/personality transfers into still-conscious volunteers, not global identity mergers. FC01-FC05.
FCA02 PROPOSED/UNRESOLVED — Curzon-Odo is depicted as an exceptional combined/joined state in which both identify with the resulting entity; its exact global identity relation should remain unresolved and typed separately from ordinary zhian'tara transfer. FC06-FC10.
FCA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Curzon's historical rejection of Jadzia is directly recontextualized by his admitted conflict/love rather than lack of Jadzia's merit. FC08-FC09.
FCA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Reintegration produces cross-perspective memory consequences: Jadzia remembers Curzon's changeling experience and Odo reports experiential insight into Curzon/humanoid activities. FC09-FC10.
FCA05 PROPOSED/STABLE — Nog's initial test failure is caused by Quark's sabotage and therefore is not valid evidence of Nog's aptitude. FC11.
