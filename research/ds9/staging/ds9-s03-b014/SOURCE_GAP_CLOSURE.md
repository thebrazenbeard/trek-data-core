## Revalidated evidence

SH01 After the Bajoran First Minister dies, Kai Winn temporarily assumes governmental duties while remaining Kai and prepares to seek the elected First Minister office.

SH02 Winn orders soil reclamators transferred from Dahkur to Rakantha. Shakaar and the Dahkur farmers argue that they waited years for the equipment, were promised a full period of use, and need it to restore their own food-producing land. The source presents a real allocation conflict rather than proving one agricultural plan objectively optimal.

SH03 Kira initially agrees to mediate because she recognizes the importance of both Bajoran recovery projects and has personal standing with Shakaar from their resistance-cell history.

SH04 Negotiation fails. Winn escalates the dispute into an arrest/security action; Kira sides with Shakaar and former resistance comrades rather than carrying out Winn's demand.

SH05 Winn interprets Shakaar's defiance through religious language about the Prophets and treats the political crisis as a test of her authority. This remains Winn's professed religious/political interpretation, not independent evidence that the Prophets authorized coercion.

SH06 Sisko refuses to turn the Federation into Winn's enforcement arm in an internal Bajoran dispute and warns against escalation.

SH07 Kira, Shakaar, Lupaza and Furel reuse resistance tactics while explicitly confronting the moral difference between fighting Cardassian occupation forces and potentially killing fellow Bajorans.

SH08 Colonel Lenaris and Shakaar ultimately stop short of turning the confrontation into a firefight. Lenaris recognizes the danger of civil war rather than treating obedience to Winn as sufficient reason to continue escalation.

SH09 Shakaar chooses electoral opposition rather than armed seizure of power and announces candidacy for First Minister. His emerging legitimacy is therefore political/electoral, not a military coup produced by the standoff.

SH10 The ending establishes that Winn's bid is politically damaged by the crisis and Shakaar becomes the major electoral alternative. Exact later election outcome belongs to later-source evidence rather than being backfilled into this episode.

## Assertions

SHA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — The reclamator dispute combines genuine resource scarcity with a political escalation driven by competing authority and recovery priorities. The source does not establish a mathematically optimal agricultural allocation.

SHA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Winn's invocation of the Prophets establishes her religious-political framing only; no Prophet appearance or independent revelation verifies her policy.

SHA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Shakaar/Kira and Lenaris consciously distinguish resistance against occupation from civil violence among Bajorans and avert the immediate armed confrontation.

SHA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Shakaar redirects the conflict into electoral politics rather than seizing governmental authority by force.

SHA05 PROPOSED/STABLE — Kira's loyalty to former resistance comrades does not erase her Starfleet/Bajoran institutional role; the episode depicts a conflict among present duty, personal history, agricultural justice and fear of civil conflict rather than a simple reversion to her occupation-era identity.
