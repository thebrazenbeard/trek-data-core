## The Adversary
AD01 Ambassador Krajensky's apparent Tzenkethi coup intelligence sends the Defiant on a mission; the real Krajensky later proves missing and the coup never occurred.
AD02 The apparent Krajensky is a Founder/changeling who takes control of Defiant systems and programs an attack on a Tzenkethi settlement intended to trigger war.
AD03 The infiltrator can pass humanoid scans and impersonate crew/objects, producing justified mutual-identification uncertainty aboard ship.
AD04 Odo physically fights the Founder after refusing an invitation to escape/link; the other changeling dies from contact with the warp core. Odo says he never wanted to harm him.
AD05 The dying Founder tells Odo that the Founders are already widespread, a warning utterance whose exact scope is not independently measured in the work.
Assertions: apparent Krajensky and real Krajensky are distinct local entities; Odo's killing is direct evidence that the prior Founder claim “no changeling has harmed another” has now been violated; the manufactured coup/attack plan is a Dominion destabilization operation, while the final ubiquity claim remains testimony.

## The Way of the Warrior
WW01 Gowron deploys a large Klingon force after Cardassian civilian overthrow of Central Command, claiming a Dominion/Founder coup despite Worf stating he knows of no proof.
WW02 Klingon troops search/detain neutral traffic and assault Cardassia; Federation leadership condemns the unprovoked invasion and the Khitomer political relationship collapses.
WW03 Sisko indirectly warns Cardassia through Garak, then Defiant rescues the Detapa Council while deliberately taking the risk that the Klingon suspicion could theoretically be true.
WW04 Worf rejects Gowron's invasion despite personal debt and threatened loss of House/status, framing keeping his Starfleet oath as his own honor obligation.
WW05 Klingon forces attack DS9 after Sisko shelters the Cardassian government. DS9's Dominion-oriented fortification enables defense; Gowron stops full escalation but retains conquered Cardassian colonies.
WW06 Worf chooses to remain in Starfleet and becomes DS9 strategic-operations officer.
Assertions: Klingon invasion is motivated by an asserted Dominion infiltration that lacks proof in this work; Worf's political/Klingon identity and Starfleet oath conflict without one automatically erasing the other; Cardassian civilian government remains distinct from prior Central Command; the Federation-Klingon political break is a consequence of the invasion, not proof Klingon suspicions were true.

## The Visitor
VI01 A Defiant accident during a wormhole/subspace inversion knocks Sisko out of linear time; he intermittently reappears near Jake while not aging normally.
VI02 In the resulting future Jake becomes a successful author, marries Korena, later abandons writing and his marriage to study subspace mechanics after Sisko continues reappearing.
VI03 Decades later Jake recreates the inversion with an older DS9 crew but fails to recover Sisko; Sisko urges Jake to stop sacrificing his life for him.
VI04 Old Jake eventually concludes the two are temporally linked and that severing the link while Sisko is present can return Sisko to the original accident.
VI05 Jake deliberately dies by poison while Sisko is present; the link breaks and Sisko returns to the original instant with foreknowledge, avoids the discharge, and the long future ceases to be the active timeline.
Assertions: old Jake, intermediate Jake, and young Jake belong to one temporal life-path inside the erased/overwritten future, not simultaneous duplicates; the future is causally efficacious because it supplies Sisko with foreknowledge despite being replaced; Jake's deliberate death is a source-depicted self-sacrifice inside that future, not an ordinary present-timeline death after restoration.

## Hippocratic Oath
HO01 Goran'Agar is a Jem'Hadar who no longer requires ketracel-white and seeks Bashir's help freeing his soldiers from Dominion chemical dependence.
HO02 Bashir finds Goran'Agar physiologically produces/maintains needed enzyme levels but cannot establish why; he hypothesizes mutation or environmental effects.
HO03 Bashir interprets Goran'Agar's independent moral reasoning as evidence that removing white dependence might enable broader Jem'Hadar autonomy.
HO04 O'Brien treats the project as strategically dangerous and doubts the other Jem'Hadar are becoming independent. The soldiers themselves reject Goran'Agar and say freedom from white is undesirable if it produces behavior they see as weakness.
HO05 O'Brien destroys Bashir's research and forces departure because he believes it is the only way to save Bashir, explicitly disobeying Bashir's command/judgment; both acknowledge the rupture afterward.
Assertions: Goran'Agar is directly demonstrated as white-independent but the mechanism and generalizability are unresolved; drug independence does not automatically produce the same social/moral choice in other Jem'Hadar; O'Brien's intervention is intentional command disobedience motivated by protection, not proof Bashir's medical hypothesis was false.

## Indiscretion
IN01 Kira and Dukat investigate the six-year-old Ravinok disappearance for separate personal reasons; Kira seeks resistance acquaintance Lorit Akrem while Dukat eventually admits civilian Tora Naprem and their daughter Ziyal were aboard.
IN02 Evidence leads to Breen captivity; surviving prisoners confirm Akrem died and Ziyal survives.
IN03 Dukat says he plans to kill Ziyal because disclosure of his half-Bajoran daughter could destroy his position and harm his Cardassian family; Kira explicitly rejects this justification.
IN04 When confronted with Ziyal, Dukat holds a weapon on her but ultimately drops it after she identifies him as the father whose imagined rescue kept her alive.
IN05 Dukat chooses to take Ziyal openly to Cardassia despite anticipated political/familial cost.
IN06 Sisko separately admits fear of Kasidy relocating near DS9 because Jennifer's death made serious attachment feel dangerous; Kasidy has already accepted the job and retains her own decision authority.
Assertions: Ziyal is Dukat/Naprem's surviving daughter and is distinct from Dukat's other family roles; Dukat's initial planned killing is a contemplated political/familial self-protection act he abandons; Kira's moral pressure contributes but Ziyal's direct recognition is also causally salient; Kasidy's relocation is her decision and not evidence Sisko commanded or passively accepted her sacrifice.

## Cross-work notes
- Identity authentication becomes a central security problem in `The Adversary`, but changeling impersonation remains distinct from transporter duplicates, simulations and temporal selves.
- `The Visitor` shows an overwritten future can remain causally relevant without being current world history.
- Goran'Agar and the earlier Jem'Hadar youth provide counterposed evidence against simple genetic determinism: both are engineered, but one develops independence from white/Founders while another actively chooses Jem'Hadar affiliation.
