## Homefront

Evidence:
H01 A recording of the Antwerp diplomatic conference shows a decorative object morphing immediately before the bombing; no changeling remains afterward. This is direct evidence of a changeling-linked attack on Earth, not merely Starfleet speculation.
H02 Leyton appoints Sisko acting head of Starfleet Security on Earth because of Sisko/Odo's Dominion experience.
H03 Sisko/Odo demonstrate that the Federation President's office can be penetrated by a disguised changeling and obtain authorization for blood screenings/phaser sweeps at sensitive facilities.
H04 Security expansion creates immediate civil/institutional friction, including Joseph Sisko's refusal of blood testing and Sisko briefly suspecting his own father.
H05 Odo detects a changeling impersonating Admiral Leyton at Starfleet Headquarters; the infiltrator escapes. Real Leyton therefore coexists separately from a Founder impersonator.
H06 Real Leyton uses the infiltration as evidence that existing security is insufficient and argues the President is too reluctant to disrupt civilian life.
H07 Earth's entire power grid then fails. Sisko, Odo and Leyton initially infer Dominion/changeling sabotage and ask the President for emergency authority; at this episode endpoint the attribution is not yet disproved.
H08 The President reluctantly grants emergency authority and Starfleet security forces deploy throughout Earth.
H09 Counterevidence: the genuine changeling threat does not independently prove that the later grid failure or every security proposal originates with the Dominion.

Assertions:
HA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — A genuine changeling infiltration/attack threat exists on Earth before Leyton's coup evidence is uncovered. H01,H05.
HA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Founder-Leyton and real Leyton are distinct local entities; impersonation does not establish that Leyton himself was a changeling. H05-H06.
HA03 PROPOSED/UNRESOLVED at Homefront endpoint — Attribution of the global blackout to the Dominion is initially a reasonable inference under incomplete evidence, not yet an established cause. H07-H09.
HA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Security countermeasures themselves generate measurable trust/civil-order costs even before the later conspiracy is known. H03-H04,H08.

## Paradise Lost

Evidence:
P01 Sisko finds Red Squad was demobilized immediately after the blackout despite general cadet mobilization, an anomaly inconsistent with the public security narrative.
P02 Cadet Shepard, verified human by Odo, admits Red Squad entered Planetary Operations with supplied access codes and intentionally downloaded the disordering protocols that disabled Earth's power relays.
P03 Sisko/Odo infer that Starfleet personnel, not changelings, caused the blackout; the result of the sabotage was precisely the militarization of Earth that Leyton had advocated.
P04 Leyton admits to Sisko that he knowingly concealed the truth because he expected Sisko would object and says military control is justified if necessary to stop the Dominion.
P05 A changeling impersonating O'Brien tells Sisko there may be only four Founders on Earth and boasts that fear itself is producing large effects. The numerical claim remains hostile testimony, not verified census data.
P06 Investigation at DS9 finds Lieutenant Arriaga attached a subspace modulator to the Gamma-side relay under Leyton's orders, causing random wormhole openings to simulate possible cloaked Dominion fleet movement.
P07 Leyton's faction therefore manufactured both the planetary blackout and the wormhole-fleet appearance while exploiting a separately genuine changeling threat.
P08 Leyton has reassigned hundreds of prior Okinawa associates into key Earth/sector positions around the intended date of presidential displacement.
P09 Sisko is framed by a falsified blood screening as a changeling and imprisoned; Odo later frees him.
P10 Leyton orders Lakota to intercept Defiant carrying Arriaga/evidence and lies to Lakota crew that Defiant's personnel have been replaced by changelings.
P11 Defiant and Lakota exchange fire and suffer casualties. When Leyton escalates to an explicit destruction order, Benteen refuses, powers down weapons and allows Defiant through.
P12 With Benteen defecting and evidence reaching the President, Leyton abandons the coup/resigns. Armed street deployment ends, while the genuine possibility of Founder infiltration remains.

Assertions:
PA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Leyton's conspiracy deliberately manufactures evidence of imminent Dominion invasion and sabotages Earth to justify expanded military control. P01-P12.
PA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — The coup exploits a genuine changeling security threat; exposing Leyton does not falsify the Antwerp attack or Founder infiltration. H01,H05,P05.
PA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Red Squad participants act under covert superior orders, while Leyton's senior conspiracy retains distinct command responsibility. P01-P04.
PA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Leyton's false claim that Defiant was crewed by changelings directly causes Starfleet-on-Starfleet armed conflict and casualties. P10-P11.
PA05 PROPOSED/UNRESOLVED — The changeling's claim that only four Founders are on Earth remains unverified adversarial testimony. P05.
PA06 PROPOSED/STABLE — Benteen changes sides when Leyton's orders require knowingly destroying ordinary Starfleet personnel, providing counterevidence to treating Leyton's officer network as uniformly committed to a coup at any cost. P10-P12.

## Crossfire

Evidence:
C01 First Minister Shakaar visits DS9 while the True Way has a credible assassination plan in progress.
C02 Odo leads protective security and also experiences intense jealousy as Kira and Shakaar develop a romantic relationship.
C03 Shakaar privately tells Odo he believes he may be falling in love with Kira; Odo advises patience while concealing his own feelings.
C04 Kira/Shakaar later agree to dinner and kiss, establishing reciprocal present romantic interest independently of Odo's interpretation.
C05 During a turbolift movement, an attacker synthesizes Worf's voice. Distracted by Kira/Shakaar, Odo transfers control without requesting Worf's required verification code.
C06 The false instruction causes a turbolift free fall that Odo physically stops. Sisko explicitly identifies Odo's failure to verify as an uncharacteristic job-performance lapse.
C07 Worf traces the technical attack and arrests a True Way operative who confesses; the operative is independently responsible for the assassination attempt.
C08 Kira later tells Odo that she and Shakaar are romantically involved; Odo outwardly claims happiness for her, then privately destroys his quarters in grief/anger.
C09 Quark recognizes Odo's longstanding love for Kira and urges either disclosure or moving on; Odo later reinforces/soundproofs his floor.

Assertions:
CA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Kira/Shakaar's romantic relationship is directly reciprocal and should not be represented as merely Odo's jealous inference. C03-C08.
CA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Odo's jealousy causes a specific security-procedure failure but does not cause the True Way assassination plot. C05-C07.
CA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Odo's love for Kira remains privately genuine despite Kira's independent relationship with Shakaar. C08-C09.

## Return to Grace

Evidence:
R01 Dukat has lost rank/status after publicly recognizing Ziyal; his mother disowned him and his wife/other children left him, while Ziyal stays with him on the military freighter Groumall.
R02 Kira directly characterizes Ziyal's willingness to share Dukat's exile as courage rather than shame.
R03 A Klingon bird-of-prey attacks a Cardassian outpost and dismisses Groumall as militarily insignificant.
R04 Kira/Dukat improve the freighter's weapons and create a deception that lures the Klingon ship into lowering its guard.
R05 They disable and board the bird-of-prey, obtaining Klingon operational intelligence. Their tactical cooperation is effective despite continuing moral/political hostility.
R06 Dukat deliberately destroys the Groumall after Klingon prisoners are left aboard; Kira questions whether killing them is necessary.
R07 Cardassian civilian authorities ask Dukat to return the captured ship and resume advisory service, preferring diplomacy over escalation.
R08 Dukat refuses, recruits his crew into an independent anti-Klingon campaign, and explicitly hopes success will restore Cardassian pride and his own status.
R09 Kira refuses Dukat's invitation to join his resistance campaign and argues Ziyal should not share the dangerous life he is choosing.
R10 Dukat accepts that Ziyal will remain on DS9 with Kira and promises to return for her.

Assertions:
RA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Dukat's political/familial fall is a demonstrated consequence of openly accepting Ziyal as his daughter. R01-R02.
RA02 PROPOSED/STABLE — Kira and Dukat can cooperate tactically without erasing their historical hostility, divergent ethics or political aims. R03-R06.
RA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Dukat's post-victory killing of the Klingon prisoners is deliberate and is explicitly challenged by Kira. R06.
RA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — Ziyal's move to DS9 is a protective family decision accepted by Dukat, not evidence that either Ziyal or Dukat repudiates their relationship. R09-R10.
RA05 PROPOSED/STABLE — Dukat's independent resistance motive mixes Cardassian defense with explicit ambition for restored status; neither motive should be erased. R07-R08.

## The Sons of Mogh

Evidence:
S01 Kurn arrives after Gowron stripped the House of Mogh of lands, Council seat, ships and social honor because Worf opposed the Cardassian war.
S02 Kurn explicitly requests Mauk-to'Vor and asks Worf to kill him so he can die honorably. Worf initially carries out the ritual and stabs him; Dax/Odo intervene and Bashir resuscitates him.
S03 Sisko treats the attempted ritual killing as a serious Starfleet/legal violation; Worf later says he can no longer treat another attempt as merely a Klingon rite because his Federation life makes it look like murder.
S04 Kurn says he has no life or honorable future and tells Worf, as elder brother, to decide what becomes of him. This expresses extreme despair/delegation but does not specify informed consent to later memory/genetic alteration.
S05 Odo gives Kurn a security role, but Kurn deliberately allows an assailant to shoot him; Odo removes him because a death wish endangers the team.
S06 Klingon forces are secretly deploying cloaked mines around Bajoran space. Kurn/Worf infiltrate a Klingon ship, obtain mine coordinates/detonation codes and expose the deployment.
S07 During infiltration Kurn kills a Klingon officer he believes is about to kill Worf. Worf's inability to anticipate the intent intensifies his realization that he cannot simply return to his former Klingon life.
S08 Bajoran/Defiant personnel detonate the covert minefield and drive hidden Klingon ships away.
S09 Kurn later prepares to kill himself with a disruptor; Worf disarms him. Kurn again speaks in terms of no future and not burdening Worf.
S10 While Kurn is unconscious, Worf authorizes Bashir to irreversibly erase Kurn's autobiographical memory engrams, genetically alter him and surgically change his appearance.
S11 Bashir explicitly warns Worf that Kurn will no longer remember Worf or his real life and asks whether Worf is absolutely certain. Worf says it is the only way and orders the procedure.
S12 Noggra supplies a fabricated identity/family history: Kurn wakes as 'Rodek', is told he is Noggra's son with amnesia from a plasma accident, and does not recognize Worf.

Assertions:
SA01 PROPOSED/STABLE — Kurn's request for ritual death and repeated suicidal behavior are genuine and persistent; Worf's later alternative is motivated by an attempt to end that suffering without physical death. S01-S12.
SA02 PROPOSED/CONTESTED — Kurn does not provide depicted informed consent to the specific irreversible memory erasure, genetic alteration, surgery and fabricated identity. His earlier delegation of his life to Worf is not equivalent to explicit procedural consent. S04,S09-S12.
SA03 PROPOSED/STABLE — Rodek is physically/causally continuous with Kurn after deliberate memory and appearance alteration, while autobiographical/social identity continuity has been intentionally severed and replaced. S10-S12.
SA04 PROPOSED/STABLE — The fabricated plasma-accident/amnesia story given to Rodek is knowingly false testimony used to stabilize the new identity. S10-S12.
SA05 PROPOSED/STABLE — Worf's own identity position shifts: he explicitly concludes he can no longer simply return to the Klingon Empire and interprets Mauk-to'Vor through Federation murder norms as well as Klingon tradition. S03,S07-S09.
SA06 PROPOSED/STABLE — Secret Klingon mine deployment is materially exposed and neutralized; Kurn's despair does not make his military evidence or tactical competence invalid. S05-S08.

## Cross-work hypothesis tests

Hypothesis A — genuine threats can be exploited by institutions to manufacture false evidence and justify unrelated power expansion.
Support: real Founder attack/infiltration alongside Leyton's fabricated blackout and wormhole fleet.
Counterevidence: exposure of the coup does not make all security precautions or Founder warnings fictional. Status PROVISIONAL.

Hypothesis B — emotional/psychological state can materially change security behavior without making external attackers imaginary.
Support: Odo's Crossfire lapse, Kurn's death wish during security duty.
Counterevidence: True Way and covert Klingon operations are independently demonstrated external threats. Status PROVISIONAL.

Hypothesis C — memory alteration can create radical autobiographical identity discontinuity without erasing bodily/causal continuity.
Support: Kurn-to-Rodek; earlier Joran suppression/restoration and fabricated Kira/Iliana memory evidence provide different comparison cases.
Counterevidence: Kurn's case is deliberate medical erasure plus false social history, not symbiont memory suppression or implanted false identity evidence. Status PROVISIONAL.
