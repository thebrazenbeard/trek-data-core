## The Assignment
AS01 Keiko returns from the Bajoran Fire Caves and immediately tells Miles that an entity has taken possession of her body; it demonstrates control by stopping/restarting Keiko's heart and threatens lethal brain injury if Miles disobeys.
AS02 Keiko remains conscious inside the possessed body but cannot control ordinary action; after release she describes being trapped inside and sensing the entity's rage.
AS03 Miles reconfigures DS9 systems under coercion while concealing the situation to prevent the possessor from killing Keiko.
AS04 Rom identifies the intended technical effect: a chroniton/temporal disruption lethal to wormhole entities. Bajoran legend identifies Pah-wraiths as exiled/false Prophets, helping Miles infer the possessor's goal.
AS05 The possessor orders Miles to aim the station beam through its runabout toward the wormhole so it can kill the Prophets.
AS06 Miles secretly reconfigures the beam so it strikes Keiko's possessed body instead. The Pah-wraith is expelled/destroyed and Keiko survives.
AS07 Rom knowingly assists Miles's deception and is later promoted by O'Brien for engineering competence.
Assertions: possessed-Keiko behavior belongs to a controlling Pah-wraith, while Keiko remains separately conscious and coerced inside her body; apparent consent/actions by the possessing entity must not be attributed to Keiko. Miles's sabotage of station systems occurs under explicit lethal coercion but his final counter-plan is intentional resistance. Bajoran Pah-wraith lore becomes materially corroborated here by an entity acting consistently with the described exile/conflict, though the legend's full historical detail remains separately source-relative.

## Trials and Tribble-ations
TT01 Elderly Arne Darvin uses a Bajoran Orb to displace himself and the Defiant crew 105 years into the past around K-7/USS Enterprise.
TT02 Darvin's younger self is present simultaneously as an earlier temporal stage of the same life history, still posing as a human Federation official before Kirk exposes him as Klingon.
TT03 Old Darvin intends to alter his own history by killing Kirk rather than merely warning his younger self; he plants an explosive inside a tribble where Kirk is expected to encounter it.
TT04 Sisko/Dax/Odo/Worf/O'Brien/Bashir interact physically with the historical environment while attempting to avoid changing events; archival/historical persons are therefore not simulations in this work.
TT05 Dax recognizes prior-host memories involving McCoy/Koloth but does not claim current-host identity with those earlier hosts.
TT06 Sisko/Dax locate the explosive tribble and Kira transports it into space, where it detonates harmlessly.
TT07 History proceeds sufficiently for Kirk to expose younger Darvin as Klingon; old Darvin is returned to the present to face charges.
TT08 Sisko deliberately allows himself one brief encounter with Kirk before leaving, introducing a small intentional historical interaction without preventing the key recorded sequence.
Assertions: old Darvin and younger Darvin are temporally separated stages of one causal biography rather than unrelated duplicates; DS9 crew participation is actual temporal displacement into the historical frame, not a holosuite reconstruction; Dax's prior-host recollections remain inherited memory evidence; history is preserved in its principal causal outcome despite additional DS9 interactions.

## Let He Who Is Without Sin...
LS01 Worf/Dax vacation on Risa amid relationship tension over Worf's jealousy, control and Dax's prior relationships/independence.
LS02 New Essentialists led by Pascal Fullerton argue Federation comfort has weakened society and stage disruptive demonstrations, initially using nonlethal theatrical violence.
LS03 Worf becomes sympathetic and independently disables Risa's weather-control grid, imposing natural storms on residents/visitors as a social lesson.
LS04 Dax identifies Worf's stated Federation critique as partly displaced relationship anxiety. Worf recounts a childhood soccer accident in which his uncontrolled strength killed another child and says the event made lifelong self-control central to him.
LS05 Fullerton escalates beyond Worf's weather sabotage by manipulating tectonic regulators strongly enough to threaten buildings/lives.
LS06 Worf stops the tectonic escalation, repudiates the Essentialists, and identifies trust as a Federation value their coercion violates.
LS07 Dax explicitly distinguishes Arandis's past relationship with Curzon from her own present-host relationship status.
Assertions: Worf is responsible for deliberate weather-control sabotage and temporary Essentialist support, even though he later stops their more dangerous escalation; his personal control history helps explain but does not erase agency/responsibility. Arandis/Curzon history is prior-host relational evidence, not evidence Jadzia herself had that relationship. The Essentialists' critique and coercive tactics remain separately representable.

## Things Past
TP01 After a plasma-storm encounter, Sisko, Dax, Garak and solid Odo become unconscious with unusual synchronized neural activity while their runabout returns automatically to DS9.
TP02 Subjectively they experience Terok Nor during the Occupation, appearing in the roles/bodies of Bajoran prisoners while retaining current self-awareness.
TP03 Odo knows details he initially suppresses and reacts with psychosomatic effects; the reconstructed setting repeatedly resets around a case involving three Bajorans accused of an assassination attempt.
TP04 The apparent security chief Thrax eventually reveals itself as part of Odo's internal/linked reconstruction rather than a literal distinct changeling in the Occupation.
TP05 Odo finally admits that seven years earlier he, not Thrax, was security chief and wrongly charged Timor, Ishan and Jillur, allowing Dukat to execute them despite evidence inconsistencies.
TP06 Bashir identifies residual morphogenic enzymes in solid Odo. The plasma storm activated them, producing a Link-like telepathic response that drew Sisko/Dax/Garak into Odo's unresolved memory.
TP07 The experience ends when Odo admits the truth; Kira later confronts him and asks whether other innocents died under his watch. Odo says he is not sure and hopes not.
Assertions: the Terok Nor sequence is a telepathic/memory reconstruction centered on Odo's guilt, not physical time travel; the historical wrongful executions are independently confessed by Odo within/after the reconstruction and remain historical claims tied to his direct memory. Thrax in the subjective sequence is a representational role, not proof of a separate Founder. Odo's “justice” self-concept is materially challenged by a documented past investigative failure.

## The Ascent
AT01 Odo escorts Quark toward a Federation grand jury investigation involving the Orion Syndicate. Quark is not established as a Syndicate member; he says he knows intermediaries connected to it.
AT02 A planted bomb damages the runabout. Odo/Quark survive by emergency landing on a barely habitable planet with no communications, replicator and minimal food.
AT03 Quark removes the subspace transmitter and devises a plan to carry it to sufficient altitude for a distress signal.
AT04 Extended survival produces mutual hostility and practical interdependence. Odo, now solid, cannot rely on shapeshifting physiology and is eventually incapacitated by a broken leg/exposure.
AT05 Odo repeatedly urges Quark to leave him and carry the transmitter alone; Quark initially resists but ultimately continues upward with the device while Odo can no longer move.
AT06 Quark reaches the summit and activates the transmitter before collapsing. Rescuers find him there, and his signal allows them to locate both men.
AT07 Odo records what he believes will be a final log acknowledging Quark's likely death and later learns Quark saved them both.
AT08 On DS9, Nog's disciplined Academy habits initially make him incompatible with Jake as a roommate; after Sisko forces a practical accommodation, both choose to remain friends and adapt.
Assertions: Quark directly saves both lives by carrying/activating the transmitter despite severe exhaustion, while Odo's earlier engineering/security work also contributes to survival. Their antagonism and reciprocal dependence coexist. Odo's solid condition creates a different embodied survival profile without replacing his autobiographical identity. Jake/Nog roommate conflict reflects changed habits after Academy, not dissolution of their friendship.

## Cross-work hypothesis tests

Hypothesis A — bodily control and personal agency are separable.
Support: Keiko/Pah-wraith possession; prior Kurn/Rodek memory alteration; earlier Founder impersonations.
Counterevidence: possession, surgery/memory erasure and external impersonation are distinct causal mechanisms. Status PROVISIONAL.

Hypothesis B — historical or memory frames can be experientially vivid while demanding different world-state treatment.
Support: actual time travel in Trials; Odo memory-Link reconstruction in Things Past.
Counterevidence: one physically interacts with past history; the other is a shared telepathic reconstruction. Status PROVISIONAL.

Hypothesis C — adversarial relationships can generate genuine protective/cooperative action without resolving underlying conflict.
Support: Quark saving Odo; Worf/Dax repairing relationship after coercive conflict; O'Brien/Rom countering Pah-wraith.
Counterevidence: cooperation does not erase prior deception, control or hostility. Status PROVISIONAL.
