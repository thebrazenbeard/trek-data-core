## Behind the Lines
BL01 Starfleet identifies and destroys a Dominion sensor array that had provided a five-sector tactical intelligence advantage. Sisko is reassigned from Defiant command to Admiral Ross's staff; Dax commands the successful strike.
BL02 On occupied DS9, Kira's resistance engineers conflict between Cardassian and Jem'Hadar personnel using Damar's stolen memorandum. Odo had opposed the tactic but Kira proceeds with Rom/Jake after he leaves the meeting.
BL03 A female Founder arrives specifically to reconnect with Odo. Their conversations and Links give Odo experiential access to Great-Link concepts he says ordinary words cannot convey.
BL04 The Founder describes Link individuality paradoxically: one and many, drop and ocean. This is changeling self-description, not a worker-imposed ontology.
BL05 Odo promises Kira not to link again until the crisis is over, then returns to the Link anyway while Rom's sabotage depends on Odo interrupting security sensors.
BL06 Odo fails to disable the alarm. Rom is captured. When Kira confronts Odo, he says he did not forget but that while linked the resistance crisis ceased to matter to him.
BL07 Quark independently learns from an intoxicated Damar that the station deflector may be used in a new minefield-deactivation method and warns the resistance.
Assertions: Odo's missed action has direct causal consequences, but the source does not require a binary verdict between voluntary betrayal and external mind control. His own account is altered priority/experience during the Link. The female Founder's account of changeling individuality remains first-party species testimony. Dax's temporary command role is institutional command, not identity substitution for Sisko.

## Favor the Bold
FB01 Sisko proposes a major Federation offensive to retake DS9 before Dominion engineers can deactivate the wormhole minefield. Starfleet Command accepts despite the strategic risk of concentrating forces.
FB02 Martok initially reports Gowron's reluctance to commit a large Klingon fleet; Sisko argues both Martok and Worf should press Gowron because concordant advice from an ally and former opponent may be persuasive.
FB03 On occupied DS9, Weyoun confirms Rom has been sentenced to death for sabotage. Bajoran and Ferengi political appeals fail to obtain his release.
FB04 Dominion engineers report that mine self-replication can be disabled with an anti-graviton beam generated through the station deflector; minefield destruction is expected within days.
FB05 Kira, Jake, Leeta and Quark continue resistance planning while treating Odo as unreliable after his Link withdrawal. Odo later apologizes to Kira, but apology does not retroactively repair the operational damage.
FB06 Ziyal asks Dukat to pardon Rom as proof of compassion. Dukat refuses. Damar separately pressures Ziyal toward Cardassian loyalty and insults her mixed heritage; Kira intervenes physically.
FB07 Sisko consults ancient Bajoran texts before battle and explicitly says he seeks guidance but cannot yet make sense of the prophecies. The scene does not depict a clear prophetic instruction.
FB08 Dominion intelligence detects the Federation offensive. Dukat recalls forces to defend DS9 while expecting Gamma-quadrant reinforcements once the mines fall.
Assertions: Rom's “terrorist” label is Dominion legal/political classification tied to a depicted sabotage act, not neutral moral truth. Sisko's prophetic study establishes religious inquiry, not foreknowledge. Odo's later regret is evidence of changed appraisal but does not erase his earlier resistance failure.

## Sacrifice of Angels
SA01 Federation/Klingon forces attack a larger Dominion/Cardassian fleet to reach DS9 before the minefield is destroyed. Sisko deliberately uses fighter waves to open a temporary gap in enemy lines.
SA02 The station resistance is arrested, then escapes with Quark's assistance. Odo ultimately re-enters resistance action, creating false security alarms and helping Kira's group reach the computer systems.
SA03 The minefield is destroyed before the resistance can stop the deactivation system. Thousands of Dominion ships begin transiting the wormhole toward the Alpha Quadrant.
SA04 Sisko takes the Defiant into the wormhole despite the obvious disparity, expecting to fight even at likely fatal cost.
SA05 The Prophets remove Sisko from ordinary space/time, reject his planned self-sacrifice, and respond after Sisko argues that Dominion conquest will destroy Bajor and that they have previously intervened in Bajoran corporeal affairs.
SA06 After the encounter, the Dominion reinforcement fleet disappears from both wormhole exit and Gamma-quadrant monitoring. The episode directly connects this disappearance to the Prophet encounter while not specifying a conventional physical mechanism or later location.
SA07 The Prophets tell Sisko that a “penance” will be exacted and that he will “find no rest” on Bajor. Those statements are Prophet testimony/foreshadowing, not worker-generated certainty about later events.
SA08 With reinforcements gone and the Federation breakthrough approaching, Dominion/Cardassian forces evacuate DS9. Federation forces retake the station.
SA09 Ziyal refuses to evacuate with Dukat, tells him she helped Kira's group escape, and says she belongs on DS9. Damar shoots her as a traitor. Dukat remains with dying Ziyal and subsequently speaks to her after death in a visibly disorganized state.
Assertions: the disappearance of the Dominion fleet is a directly depicted anomalous intervention associated with the Prophets; the source does not license inventing a hidden scientific mechanism. Ziyal's Cardassian biological/familial relation and her chosen political/social affiliation are separate dimensions. Odo's return to resistance is a new action, not retroactive cancellation of his earlier failure.

## You Are Cordially Invited
YCI01 Worf and Jadzia prepare a traditional Klingon wedding after DS9 is retaken. Worf places strong importance on Klingon ritual; Jadzia initially treats some requirements more lightly.
YCI02 Martok's wife Sirella controls admission of Jadzia to the House of Martok and subjects her to ritual/historical questioning. Dax investigates Sirella's claimed imperial ancestry and finds the received family genealogy historically false.
YCI03 Sisko explicitly tells Jadzia that carrying Curzon's memories does not make her Curzon and that Sirella sees a young woman choosing marriage into her family, not the former Federation ambassador.
YCI04 Jadzia invokes Curzon's age/status while resisting deference, then ultimately accepts that her present relationship requires choices not supplied automatically by inherited memories.
YCI05 Worf and his male companions undertake Kal'Hyah trials of deprivation, blood, pain, sacrifice, anguish and symbolic death. Their ritual suffering is culturally framed and consensual within the wedding process, not ordinary Starfleet discipline.
YCI06 Conflict between Jadzia and Sirella temporarily cancels the wedding. Worf and Jadzia separately reconsider their pride and differences; Jadzia reconciles with Sirella.
YCI07 Worf and Jadzia complete the Klingon ceremony and are married. Sirella welcomes Jadzia into the House of Martok.
Assertions: Jadzia's memories of Curzon support psychological/experiential continuity without global identity merger. Received genealogy and biological ancestry are explicitly separable because Sirella's family narrative preserves names/titles across an actual dynastic break. House membership and marriage are social/legal relations rather than person replacement.

## Resurrection
R01 A man physically resembling deceased Vedek Bareil arrives through a multidimensional transporter from the Mirror universe. He states that his counterpart in this universe is dead and repeatedly distinguishes himself from that man.
R02 Kira's emotional recognition is driven by resemblance to prime Bareil, but she learns this Bareil has a criminal history and no prior religious life. Mirror Bareil resists being treated as the vedek.
R03 Mirror Bareil and Kira begin an intimate relationship. Their relationship is with the present counterpart, even though Kira's prior attachment to prime Bareil affects her perception.
R04 Mirror Bareil undergoes an Orb of Prophecy and Change experience. He reports confused imagery in which he and the deceased vedek seemed to overlap. Kira warns that Orb experiences are not meant to be casually interpreted/shared.
R05 The Intendant is revealed to be Mirror Bareil's existing partner and co-conspirator. Their plan is to steal the Orb, return to the Mirror universe and use its symbolic power to mobilize Bajorans there.
R06 Mirror Bareil deceives Kira and assists the theft attempt, while the Intendant impersonates Kira using clothing and stolen authorization access. Neither resemblance nor disguise merges counterpart identities.
R07 At the shrine, Mirror Bareil refuses to complete the Orb theft and incapacitates the Intendant instead. He admits he originally came for the Orb but says the relationship with Kira became real.
R08 Mirror Bareil tells Kira his Orb vision included a possible life and family with her, but he believes he would eventually ruin that life and chooses to return with the Intendant.
Assertions: Mirror Bareil and prime Bareil remain distinct local entities despite physical resemblance and anomalous vision overlap. An Orb experience is evidence that Mirror Bareil experienced imagery, not proof that he became, contained, or objectively fused with prime Bareil. The Intendant's Kira impersonation is operational disguise, not identity fact. Mirror Bareil's final self-judgment that he is “a thief” is autobiographical testimony, not a metaphysical essence.

## Cross-work observations
- The arc repeatedly distinguishes internal experience from external allegiance: Odo's Link, Sisko's Prophet encounter, Jadzia's inherited memories and Mirror Bareil's Orb experience all matter causally without automatically resolving personhood or metaphysics.
- Deception appears in multiple provenance classes: resistance disinformation, tactical feints, political labels, false genealogy, disguise and criminal conspiracy. These should not be collapsed into one generic “unreliable” bucket.
- Institutional affiliation changes are additive and contextual: retaking DS9, House-of-Martok admission, Klingon marriage and resistance membership change social status without rewriting prior personal histories.
