## Source-relative evidence

### Exploration and incomplete Vulcan information

- **Dialogue / star-chart limits:** Archer and Tucker note that Vulcan star charts omit at least one recently encountered phenomenon, providing direct evidence that Vulcan navigational data is useful but not exhaustive.
- **Dialogue / P'Jem representation:** T'Pol describes P'Jem as an ancient Vulcan spiritual retreat devoted to meditation and Kolinahr. That description accurately represents its overt institutional identity but does not reveal the concealed surveillance facility.
- **Depiction / anomaly detection:** T'Pol notices inconsistencies in the monastery's behavior and physical arrangement before the Andorians openly confront the visitors. Her observations support suspicion without yet explaining the cause.

### Andorian claims and Vulcan counterclaims

- **Andorian testimony:** Shran and his group accuse Vulcans of operating surveillance technology at P'Jem and treat Enterprise's Vulcan officer and weapons as evidence of collaboration.
- **Vulcan testimony:** The elder and T'Pol describe Andorians as suspicious/volatile and say treaty arrangements were meant to reduce longstanding conflict. Vulcan representatives deny that P'Jem contains the technology the Andorians seek.
- **Epistemic conflict:** At the hostage stage, the source supplies mutually opposed interested-party claims. The worker must not choose one as objective truth before the concealed facility is directly discovered.
- **Direct discovery / counterevidence:** A hidden high-technology complex is revealed beneath the monastery. The finding materially corroborates the core Andorian surveillance accusation and contradicts the Vulcan denial that the site contains such technology.

### Treaty and institutional deception

- **Depiction / concealment:** The surveillance installation is physically hidden behind the monastery/reliquary structure. Its concealment is deliberate and institutionally protected by Vulcans at the site.
- **Vulcan coercion:** After the installation is exposed, an armed Vulcan attempts to prevent Archer from sharing the evidence and threatens lethal force. This is direct action evidence distinct from earlier verbal denials.
- **Archer decision:** Archer orders T'Pol's imaging evidence given to Shran. He states that Andoria should know the Vulcans violated the treaty. The episode depicts Archer making an independent interspecies-political judgment rather than automatically aligning with Earth's Vulcan partners.
- **T'Pol compliance:** T'Pol follows Archer's order and communicates that the Andorian ship is free to depart. Her conduct supports command compliance despite the disclosure being politically damaging to Vulcan authorities.

### Hostage conduct and force

- **Andorian coercion:** Shran's group detains, strikes, threatens, and interrogates the Enterprise party and Vulcan residents. Their correct suspicion about surveillance does not retroactively neutralize those coercive acts.
- **Command restraint:** Archer initially orders Enterprise not to provoke the Andorians while the hostages seek an escape route.
- **Escalation:** Reed's team transports into the monastery/catacombs and a firefight follows. The source depicts risk to hostages and sanctuary property rather than an abstractly clean rescue action.
- **Vulcan pacifist norm:** Vulcan residents explicitly frame violence as contrary to their current way, while one Vulcan later chooses armed participation to protect what he considers theirs. The episode therefore contains both institutional norm and local deviation.

### Early interspecies politics

- **Andorian-Vulcan history claims:** T'Pol and the monks say the neighboring species have been in conflict for years and describe Andorian fears of Vulcan conquest. These are Vulcan characterizations until the surveillance discovery supplies independent reason for some Andorian suspicion.
- **Shran state change:** Shran moves from treating Archer as a Vulcan collaborator to acknowledging a debt after Archer supplies the evidence. This is a work-local interpersonal/political change, not yet a broad Human-Andorian alliance.
- **Human institutional position:** Enterprise is neither formally Andorian nor Vulcan authority. Archer nevertheless becomes an evidentiary intermediary whose disclosure changes the local balance of information.

## Candidate interpretive assertions

1. **Interested-party testimony must remain provisional until direct evidence arrives.** The episode deliberately begins with mutually incompatible Vulcan and Andorian accounts and later supplies physical evidence that changes their relative support.
2. **Correct suspicion does not erase wrongful conduct.** Andorian claims about surveillance are substantially vindicated, while hostage-taking, assault, and threats remain separately evidenced acts.
3. **P'Jem demonstrates divergence between overt institution and hidden function.** A religious sanctuary can simultaneously house a covert state surveillance role without the worker collapsing either layer.
4. **Archer exercises independent political agency before a mature Federation framework exists.** His decision favors disclosure of treaty-relevant evidence over automatic solidarity with Vulcan authorities.
5. **T'Pol's loyalty dimensions are not binary.** She remains Vulcan, serves aboard Enterprise, objects to violence, and nevertheless follows Archer's disclosure order against Vulcan institutional interests.

## Counterevidence and unresolved points

- The surveillance discovery corroborates the Andorian accusation that P'Jem contains surveillance equipment, but it does not prove every Andorian claim about Vulcan plans for conquest.
- Vulcan descriptions of Andorians as inherently suspicious or volatile are culturally interested characterizations, not species-level objective facts.
- Andorian violence does not prove the Vulcan surveillance program was lawful or necessary; those are separate questions.
- The precise treaty text, authorized surveillance boundaries, and chain of command behind P'Jem are not fully supplied in this work.
- Later consequences from `Shadows of P'Jem` must not be imported into this episode's local assertions.
- Transcript sources are derivative and not direct audiovisual verification.
