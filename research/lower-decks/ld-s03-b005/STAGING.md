## S03E01 — Grounded

### Local entity candidates

Episode-local occurrences include Freeman, Mariner, Boimler, Tendi, Rutherford, Ransom, Admiral Freeman, Starfleet investigators, Morgan Bateson, Tuvok, Pakled conspirators, Klingon/Pakled investigation targets, the impounded Cerritos and the Verugament encounter. Cross-series characters remain local occurrences/references pending global reconciliation.

### Evidence / frame discipline

- FNN coverage reports Freeman as accused of destroying Pakled Planet and repeats interpretations of motive. The broadcast establishes public reporting and accusation, not guilt.
- Surveillance footage appears to place Freeman at the bombing. Later Starfleet investigation establishes that the footage was fabricated. Preserve the apparently incriminating record and its later disconfirmation.
- Mariner distrusts the legal/investigative process and attempts an unauthorized independent investigation. Admiral Freeman urges reliance on Starfleet procedure. These are competing strategies and beliefs, not competing world-state facts.
- Boimler possesses copied captain's logs but admits he contaminated them with irrelevant/personal material. Their existence therefore does not automatically make them courtroom-grade evidence.
- Mariner and friends steal/commandeer the impounded Cerritos trying to locate Klingon evidence. Their conduct is directly depicted wrongdoing even though their motive is to exonerate Freeman.
- Freeman later reports the secret Starfleet investigation: Morgan Bateson traced the fabricated incriminating footage; a forger was captured in the Romulan Neutral Zone; Tuvok's questioning exposed that Pakled conspirators bombed their own capital in order to frame Freeman and obtain Federation relocation to a resource-rich world.
- The narrative presents this investigation as the adjudicative reveal that clears Freeman. That later result resolves the prior legal accusation but does not erase the earlier arrest, public suspicion, fabricated footage or Mariner's unauthorized conduct.
- Freeman returns exonerated and assigns Ransom final authority over Mariner's discipline because Mariner's actions showed continued inability to follow Freeman's orders. Exoneration of Freeman therefore does not exonerate every action taken on her behalf.

### Provisional assertions / regression candidates

- Arrest/public accusation -> not guilt; later investigation directly exonerates Freeman.
- Incriminating surveillance -> later established fabrication.
- Benevolent motive -> does not erase Mariner's unauthorized commandeering.
- Secret investigative conclusion should supersede the obsolete culpability hypothesis without deleting the historical accusation/evidence chain.

## S03E02 — The Least Dangerous Game

### Local entity candidates

Episode-local occurrences include Mariner, Ransom, Boimler, K'ranch, Tendi, Rutherford, mission personnel and the Bat'leths & BiHnuchs game representation of Martok. Game/program representations are artifacts rather than imported global character occurrences.

### Evidence / frame discipline

- The lower-deckers play a licensed Klingon role-playing/game program using a Martok presentation. Statements issued by the game representation belong to the game frame; they are not testimony from the globally reconciled historical Martok.
- Under Ransom's direct oversight, Mariner tries rigid obedience and suppresses objections because she expects any disagreement to be punished. Her apparent compliance is therefore strategic behavior under a supervisory constraint, not proof that her institutional views changed.
- When the mission develops complications, Mariner's practical judgment becomes useful despite the earlier obedience performance. Ransom's assessment of her work must be read against what she actually does rather than a simple rule-following score.
- Boimler adopts an intentional 'bold Boimler' strategy and repeatedly accepts opportunities/challenges he would ordinarily avoid. This is a self-directed behavioral experiment, not evidence that his earlier caution was fraudulent.
- K'ranch identifies Boimler as prey and conducts a culturally framed hunt. Boimler initially interprets the pursuit primarily as lethal threat. K'ranch later explains his species' hunt conventions and completes the ritual without simply murdering Boimler.
- Boimler's fear during the hunt remains genuine even though the hunter understands the event through a different ritual frame. Preserve both perspectives rather than retroactively treating the pursuit as harmless.
- The episode therefore distinguishes participant intention, target interpretation and actual outcome. Cultural framing does not automatically neutralize physical danger.

### Provisional assertions / regression candidates

- Game representation -> not global historical person testimony.
- Strategic obedience -> not equivalent to belief change.
- 'Bold' self-experiment -> present behavior shift, not permanent personality replacement.
- K'ranch hunt -> same event carries different cultural/experiential meanings for hunter and target.

## S03E03 — Mining the Mind's Mines

### Local entity candidates

Episode-local occurrences include Cerritos and Carlsbad crews, Tendi, Mariner, Boimler, Rutherford, Jennifer projections, fantasy/fear projections, scientists/outpost personnel and Scrubble. Psychic projections remain generated manifestations, not global merges with the people/images represented.

### Evidence / frame discipline

- Psychic stones read private thoughts and manifest personalized fantasy imagery. Manifested teachers, lovers, imagined versions of Jennifer and other figures are projections generated from minds, not independent occurrences of the referenced persons.
- Direct contact with manifestations can turn victims to stone, so the projected frame produces real physical consequences. 'Imaginary' origin must not be confused with 'physically irrelevant.'
- Cerritos and Carlsbad crews each infer that the other group looks down on them. Later conversation shows mutual admiration and insecurity. The earlier hostile interpretation is therefore disconfirmed without implying either group irrationally invented all social cues.
- Damaged stones manifest fears/nightmares as well as fantasies, showing that stone output is not a transparent report of desire or objective reality.
- Tendi begins senior science-officer training and initially struggles to assert a scientific judgment in a negotiation context. Her eventual interruption and discovery are evidence of role development at this stage, not proof of completed mastery.
- The crew discovers that the stones retain extensive private thought data, including sensitive access information. Federation-linked equipment is involved in extracting/recording that information.
- The apparent conflict between outpost scientists and Scrubble is revealed as a manufactured deception. The parties were cooperating to harvest Starfleet personnel's psychic data for black-market sale.
- Freeman's supplemental account records the manufactured conflict and expected Federation tribunal. This later record should replace the earlier 'genuine local war' interpretation while preserving the deceptive presentation that produced it.

### Provisional assertions / regression candidates

- Psychic projection -> not represented-person identity.
- Mental manifestation -> can have genuine physical causal consequences.
- Mutual contempt inference -> later mutual-admiration reveal.
- Apparent conflict -> deliberately manufactured intelligence-harvesting scheme.
- Thought-derived data -> source-sensitive privacy/security evidence, not omniscient mind-reading truth outside the artifact.

## S03E04 — Room for Growth

### Local entity candidates

Episode-local occurrences include Freeman, Cerritos engineers, Beta/Delta shift competitors, Dove spa personnel, Minooki possession state, holodeck criminal-fantasy roles and ship systems. Holodeck characters remain simulation occurrences.

### Evidence / frame discipline

- The opening Minooki incident depicts Freeman under an external possession/altered-control state. Actions during that state should not be flattened into ordinary autonomous Freeman behavior.
- Engineering personnel are exhausted and ordered into restorative leave/spa treatment. They manipulate stress-monitoring results with cucumber so Freeman believes they have relaxed while they secretly continue engineering work.
- The falsified wellness readings are instrument outputs deliberately corrupted by users; they cannot be promoted to objective evidence that the engineers were rested.
- Spa diagnostics later indicate Freeman herself is carrying severe accumulated stress. Her command focus on others' wellness therefore coexists with evidence that she has neglected her own state.
- Engineers eventually devise a technical intervention that genuinely relieves their stress. Successful outcome does not make the earlier falsification truthful.
- Beta and Delta shifts race/cheat to secure scarce private quarters. Their assumptions about how the other shift will use the room are later undermined when Delta shares it among the group.
- Some hazards arise within a holodeck crime fantasy. The environment/roles remain simulation, while danger to users can become real when safety conditions fail or physical effects carry through.
- Crew rumors about senior officers remain hearsay unless the episode separately depicts the claimed event.

### Provisional assertions / regression candidates

- Possessed Freeman -> altered agency frame.
- Falsified stress data -> instrument output is only as reliable as acquisition integrity.
- Successful later cure -> does not retroactively validate deceptive readings.
- Rival-shift stereotype -> disconfirmed by actual sharing behavior.
- Holodeck fiction and physical user risk can coexist without collapsing frames.

## S03E05 — Reflections

### Local entity candidates

Episode-local occurrences include present Rutherford, his younger/older-memory personality state, Tendi, Mariner, Boimler, Petra Aberdeen, recruitment-booth visitors and unidentified Starfleet personnel associated with Rutherford's implant procedure. The two Rutherford personality/memory states remain local identity candidates without a global same/different verdict.

### Evidence / frame discipline

- Rutherford experiences recurrent memory/dream material linked to his implant. Dream imagery is not objective history by itself.
- After Tendi clears implant cache, an earlier Rutherford personality/memory backup gains bodily control. T'Ana finds no external/anaphasic possessor and describes the event as Rutherford's own older memory/personality state overwriting current control.
- Mindscape scenes depict two Rutherford self-states with distinct memories, preferences and goals sharing one body/history. The narrative does not require this worker to force a binary global identity resolution.
- The younger Rutherford says one version will dominate and the other be erased. They compete using imagined vehicles/crews. Mindscape events are internal/representational even though their outcome affects bodily control.
- Present Rutherford wins partly through representations of his current relationships and then rescues the younger version rather than allowing him to die.
- Recovered memory shows that the official/elective origin story for Rutherford's implant was programmed/fabricated. Unidentified Starfleet-linked personnel deliberately erased memories and installed a false explanation after an earlier explosion.
- The exact responsible higher-up remains unresolved in this episode. Do not import later-season identification into the S03E05 evidence record.
- Younger Rutherford rejects a simple merger framing because he believes it would erase both selves, then voluntarily yields after recognizing present Rutherford's growth. The disappearance/yield should not be labeled a simple technical merge without stronger evidence.
- Petra Aberdeen claims former Starfleet status and criticizes the institution. Her theft/liberation of an artifact and attempt to recruit Mariner outside Starfleet are directly depicted; broader autobiographical claims remain testimony unless corroborated.
- Boimler's heated defense of Starfleet unintentionally persuades a bystander to enlist. The effect is depicted even though his speech is not neutral evidence that all of his institutional claims are objectively true.

### Provisional assertions / regression candidates

- Dream/memory fragment -> not objective history until corroborated.
- Older personality backup and present Rutherford require multi-dimensional continuity, not binary sameness.
- Implant 'elective' history -> later revealed programmed false memory.
- Deliberate memory erasure is established; responsible higher-up remains unresolved at this frontier.
- Voluntary yielding of prior self-state -> not automatically equivalent to fusion/merger.
- Institutional advocacy/critique -> speaker positions, separable from directly depicted behavior.

## Batch-level regression set

1. **Legal accusation versus adjudication:** Freeman's arrest and incriminating footage are later overturned by investigation.
2. **Fabricated record:** surveillance can be persuasive evidence and still be manufactured.
3. **Representation identity:** game Martok and psychic projections are not automatically global persons.
4. **Cultural frame divergence:** hunter and target interpret the same event differently.
5. **Psychic manifestation with physical consequence:** non-objective origin does not mean causal irrelevance.
6. **Manufactured conflict:** observed social/political presentation can be deliberate intelligence deception.
7. **Instrument tampering:** falsified wellness measurements remain falsified data even when produced by real sensors.
8. **Altered agency:** possession and memory-personality takeover require state-sensitive attribution.
9. **Memory provenance:** recovered internal memory can disconfirm implanted autobiographical narrative while leaving actor identity unresolved.
10. **Temporal discipline:** later revelations must not be back-projected into an episode before the source establishes them.
