## S04E06 — Parth Ferengi's Heart Place

- Ferenginar's Federation-accession negotiations contain strategic bargaining by Rom, Leeta, Freeman and others. Negotiating positions are not timeless institutional truth.
- Cerritos personnel conduct undercover tourism/consumer tests. Tendi and Rutherford's newlywed status is an explicit cover role, so actions performed to maintain that role must not be promoted into an actual marriage assertion.
- Commercial/legal statements made by Ferengi hospitality staff remain source-local claims unless independently established.
- Boimler's choices are strongly shaped by engineered advertising/consumer persuasion. Real purchases under persuasive conditions do not establish stable values.
- Regression: cover identity versus actual relationship; bargaining position versus settled policy; persuasive environment versus baseline preference.

- Regression: software fission; creator versus controller; self-description versus conduct; claimed reform versus behavioral evidence.

## S04E08 — Caves

- The present-day four officers tell multiple stories about earlier cave missions. The visualized stories remain recollection/narration frames, not automatically omniscient flashbacks.
- Each narrator selects and interprets remembered events; exact historical propositions need the appropriate memory/testimony provenance.
- Boimler's Steve Levy/Vendorian story includes a real Vendorian intervention and later assistance. One validated Vendorian claim does not validate Levy's entire conspiracy worldview.
- The closing Vendorian observation supplies direct local evidence that Vendorians influenced the current situation to help rekindle the officers' friendship.
- The officers directly acknowledge that promotion/new assignments reduced their shared time. Their renewed connection is a present state change, not evidence the distancing never occurred.
- Regression: narrated memory; partial corroboration; one true conspiracy claim does not generalize; present friendship repair versus prior distancing.

## S04E09 — The Inner Fight

- Mariner's escalating risk-taking is observed by friends and command. Freeman interprets it as self-endangerment and tries to place Mariner on a low-risk assignment while the crew investigates attacks involving former Starfleet personnel.
- The supposedly safe mission is compromised by external tampering. Stranded personnel from several factions give similar accounts of manipulation aboard their ships, creating convergent evidence for a common organizer.
- Mariner shifts from seeking danger to organizing cooperation among the stranded groups.
- In conversation with Ma'ah, Mariner connects much of her recent behavior to grief surrounding Sito Jaxa and to fear of rank/responsibility that could place junior officers at risk. This is direct self-report about motive, but it should not be backfilled as the sole cause of every earlier reckless act.
- Sito's historical fate is still primarily referenced/testimonial evidence at this episode frontier.
- Mariner is removed from the stranded group and the ending identifies Nick Locarno as connected to the operation; the full plan is deferred to E10.
- Regression: retrospective motive without monocausal rewrite; convergent testimony; referenced history; delayed causal resolution.

## S04E10 — Old Friends, New Planets

- The opening Academy-era flashback directly depicts younger Locarno, Sito Jaxa, Wesley Crusher and Nova Squadron associates. This supplies LD-local depiction for history E09 only referenced through testimony.
- Present Locarno frames Nova Fleet as liberation from senior-command hierarchy. His ideological claims remain speaker-position evidence and must be assessed separately from his coercive methods and system-level risks.
- The season's prior ship disruptions are connected to Locarno's recruitment/force-building operation, resolving the earlier causal uncertainty.
- Locarno appeals to shared Sito/Nova Squadron history while recruiting Mariner; Mariner rejects his interpretation of that history.
- Mariner's presence inside Locarno's operation is constrained and adversarial, so it must not be coded as voluntary ideological allegiance.
- The Genesis-device crisis and its resulting new planetary body are directly depicted technological consequences. Expected effects stated beforehand remain predictions until the device acts.
- Tendi uses her Orion family resources to obtain rescue assistance and thereby incurs a concrete family obligation. Her resulting departure from Cerritos is an assignment/location transition, not a personal replacement.
- Regression: referenced history versus direct flashback; ideology versus conduct; constrained participation versus allegiance; delayed mystery resolution; role departure versus identity change.
