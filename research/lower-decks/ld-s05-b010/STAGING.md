## S05E06 — Of Gods and Angles

### Evidence / frame discipline

- The Cerritos hosts peace talks between the photonic Orb and Cube peoples, who have a long-running conflict. Delegates' claims about the opposing people remain participant positions until stronger evidence appears.
- Ensign Olly has repeatedly been removed from assignments because apparently ordinary engineering work is followed by electrical accidents. She initially hides the actual source: she possesses poorly controlled electrical abilities tied to her unusual ancestry.
- Olly discovers a damaged room and hides a display because she expects the electrical damage will be blamed on her. Hiding evidence is directly depicted wrongdoing, but it is not evidence that she caused the apparent disappearance or harmed the missing Cube.
- Mariner initially projects her own history with discipline and second chances onto Olly. She later recognizes that Olly's problems and motives are not simply another version of Mariner's.
- The missing Cube and a missing Orb are later revealed alive. They had hidden because they were in a relationship their communities would reject; the damaged room and residue were not evidence of an attack.
- The apparent crime investigation therefore demonstrates how physical traces can support a plausible but false causal model when key context is missing.
- Olly's attempt to absorb/divert photonic energy with her powers initially destabilizes the situation, but her engineering solution using the Cerritos tractor/nacelle systems successfully interrupts the larger confrontation.
- The Orb/Cube relationship and child give the delegations new evidence that coexistence is possible, and negotiations resume around that shared fact.
- Freeman allows Olly to remain aboard and work in engineering while still imposing consequences for lying/hiding evidence. Institutional retention and discipline coexist rather than one cancelling the other.

### Regression candidates

- Electrical accidents -> hidden personal capability, not malicious intent.
- Hidden evidence -> wrongdoing distinct from the supposed underlying crime.
- Apparent attack/murder evidence -> later disconfirmed by the missing pair's return.
- Mariner/Olly similarity -> analogy, not identity or guaranteed shared motives.
- Failed power-based intervention -> later successful engineering intervention; capability is context-sensitive.

## S05E07 — Fully Dilated

### Evidence / frame discipline

- A purple alternate-universe Enterprise has crossed through a fissure and left a Starfleet power signature on pre-warp Dilmer III. Alternate-universe artifact identity must remain reality-qualified.
- Mariner, Tendi and T'Lyn receive physical disguises and go undercover to avoid contaminating the pre-warp society. Their Dilmerian presentations are mission covers, not new personal identities.
- The planet's extreme time differential means a short interval aboard Cerritos becomes roughly a year for the away team. Time elapsed must therefore be recorded per frame rather than assuming one shared clock.
- The recovered object is the detached head of an alternate, purple Data. This is a counterpart occurrence from another quantum reality, not a silent global merge with another Data occurrence.
- Transporter failure strands the team long enough for them to construct sustained local lives while attempting to preserve cultural noninterference. Mariner's imprisonment/social ties and T'Lyn's local enterprises occur within that undercover temporal interval.
- Tendi and T'Lyn each interpret the other as competing for Cerritos senior science officer. T'Lyn later explains that some of her actions were attempts to restore camaraderie rather than outcompete Tendi. Tendi's earlier competitive interpretation is therefore partially disconfirmed.
- The Data counterpart gives advice based on his own experience and observes both officers' complementary strengths. His recommendation remains testimony by this local counterpart, not an omniscient evaluation system.
- Freeman ultimately appoints Tendi and T'Lyn jointly as co-senior science officers. The decision is a real institutional status change for both rather than proof that they have become a single functional identity.

### Regression candidates

- Alternate Data -> COUNTERPART_OF, never name-based SAME_AS.
- Surgical/undercover presentation -> cover state, not personal replacement.
- Ship time versus surface time -> separate temporal frames.
- Perceived competition -> later evidence of complementary intentions.
- Joint office -> shared role, not merged identity.

## S05E08 — Upper Decks

### Evidence / frame discipline

- The episode deliberately shifts focus from the usual junior-officer perspective to Freeman, Ransom, T'Ana, Shaxs and Billups while the familiar lower-deck group spends much of the day at a pumpkin-carving gathering.
- The narrative structure demonstrates that events absent from the usual protagonists' attention can still be occurring concurrently; lack of lower-decker observation is not evidence of inactivity elsewhere aboard ship.
- Freeman moves among numerous unrelated responsibilities while a Clicket infiltration develops. Her view is broad but still partial; she learns pieces from other officers rather than functioning as an omniscient narrator.
- Ransom appears to behave as a distracting exercise-obsessed supervisor while Beta/Delta personnel manage buhgoons. The later result shows his performance was partly a management tactic intended to unite the groups around solving the problem themselves.
- T'Ana responds to criticism of her pain-management practice by testing/recalibrating her approach. Her own high pain tolerance is not valid evidence that patients share that tolerance.
- Shaxs's confrontation with an internal/astral representation of his unresolved rage is a subjective/mental or metaphysical frame. Actions by that representation should not be flattened into ordinary external crew events without frame qualification.
- Billups takes a junior engineer through a dangerous repair and models leadership through direct participation. His technical confidence coexists with genuine system risk.
- The Clicket infiltration eventually intersects the separate senior-officer threads. Earlier isolated events gain a common immediate operational context without becoming one person's complete account.
- Freeman recalls a Clicket social vulnerability and uses praise rather than force to end the immediate confrontation. The resolution depends on species-specific behavioral information supplied/remembered in this episode.

### Regression candidates

- Offscreen-to-protagonists does not mean nonexistent.
- Managerial performance can be deliberate strategy rather than transparent incompetence.
- Self-measured pain tolerance is not transferable patient data.
- Internal/metaphysical Shaxs experience requires frame qualification.
- Multiple concurrent partial perspectives can converge on one crisis without granting any participant omniscience.

## S05E09 — Fissure Quest

### Local entity / identity discipline

- William Boimler, the transporter-created duplicate previously reported dead, is directly depicted alive and serving as captain of the U.S.S. Anaximander under a classified mission. The prior death report remains a false institutional cover event that had real consequences for Brad Boimler.
- William's crew consists of interdimensional castaways from different quantum realities, including local counterpart occurrences of T'Pol, Curzon Dax, Garak, a holographic Bashir, numerous Harry Kims and later an alternate Engineering Mariner.
- These people are explicitly variants/counterparts from different realities. Shared names, memories or recognizable traits do not authorize global SAME_AS merges.
- Garak and holographic Bashir are married in their local reality relationship. This is direct evidence for those counterpart occurrences only and must not be imported into other Garak/Bashir identity histories.
- Multiple Harry Kims differ in rank/history. Their coexistence is direct counterevidence to any keying scheme that equates name plus appearance with one person.

### Causal / epistemic discipline

- William initially believes the fissure creator is intentionally attempting to damage the multiverse. That operational hypothesis motivates pursuit but is later disconfirmed.
- Engineering Mariner discovers that each deliberate reality crossing produces an additional rift elsewhere because of energy conservation. Her model explains why William has repeatedly pursued the wrong fissure.
- The pursued ship is the Beagle, commanded by an alternate Lily Sloane. Her society developed quantum-reality travel rather than the prime-reality warp-development history.
- Sloane and her crew explain they are peaceful explorers with noninterference rules for realities unable to cross dimensions. They did not understand that their drive left damaging secondary fissures. The 'multiversal villain' model becomes an unintended-consequence model.
- Two-Pip Harry Kim later takes the Beagle in an attempt to bring the other Kims to a reality where he believes they will receive more respect. The other Kims reject that choice. Shared grievance does not equal shared consent.
- The damaged Beagle opens an unstable rift, producing soliton effects threatening multiple realities. Lily determines that the wave can be redirected into one reality as an emergency containment strategy.
- Engineering Mariner selects her own reality because she trusts her counterparts/friends there to solve the problem. William sends a message to Brad Boimler. The ensuing threat to the prime reality is therefore a deliberate emergency risk transfer based on trust, not a hostile attack from William.

### Regression candidates

- Transporter duplicate William -> independent continuing local person, not replacement Brad.
- False death report -> later directly disconfirmed.
- Multiversal cast -> COUNTERPART_OF/reality-scoped identities.
- 'Villain causing fissures' -> later disconfirmed as unintended exploration consequence.
- Shared group grievance -> does not confer consent to Two-Pip Kim's plan.
- Cross-reality trust -> causal explanation for redirecting danger, not evidence of malicious intent.

## S05E10 — The New Next Generation

### Crisis / reality-frame evidence

- William's message and the directed soliton wave establish an imminent threat to the prime quantum reality. Starfleet determines the Cerritos is uniquely positioned to respond because of the Boimler connection and limited time.
- The rift is surrounded by a possibility field that temporarily remaps matter into alternate multiversal forms. Each transformed Cerritos is a transient state caused by the field; these are not ordinary historical refits or evidence that the prime ship always had those designs.
- Klingon commander Relga pursues Ma'ah and Malor and presents a purported Starfleet order. Kayshon later exposes that the message was forged/manipulated, so the apparent institutional directive is disconfirmed.
- Ransom had hidden/protected Malor without fully informing Freeman, preserving her plausible deniability. His omission is deliberate command strategy and should not be conflated with Freeman's knowledge.
- Olly uses her electrical capability as part of the Cerritos response against Relga's ship, showing improved control/operational use relative to E06.

### Science / engineering / identity evidence

- Tendi and T'Lyn function as co-senior science officers during the crisis. Their disagreement under pressure is real but they re-coordinate; shared office does not eliminate distinct judgment.
- Rutherford repeatedly interprets incompatibility between his implant and changing/older Cerritos systems as a ship problem. When the Cerritos returns to California-class form, he recognizes the vessel's flexibility and identifies his implant dependence as the constraint instead.
- Rutherford disconnects the implant to perform the required reconfiguration and later chooses not to reinstall it. His bodily continuity, memory, friendships and institutional identity persist through removal, while his affect changes because the implant's dopamine inhibition is gone.
- The implant removal therefore supplies explicit evidence that cybernetic continuity and personal continuity are separable dimensions in this source.
- Cerritos reconfiguration stabilizes the quantum rift rather than sealing it. The result is a permanently open portal to other quantum realities, converting an emergency anomaly into a new exploration frontier.

### Final institutional state

- Starfleet assigns Starbase 80, whose older systems tolerate the nearby conditions, to support the permanent portal frontier.
- Carol Freeman voluntarily leaves command of Cerritos to supervise multiversal exploration from Starbase 80, joined by Alonzo Freeman and the Anaximander-linked exploratory personnel. This is a career/assignment transition, not disappearance or identity replacement.
- Jack Ransom becomes captain of the Cerritos.
- Ransom names Mariner and Brad Boimler as co-provisional first officers. The status is explicitly provisional/shared; neither should be silently projected as sole permanent first officer.
- Tendi and T'Lyn remain co-senior science officers.
- Rutherford remains aboard Cerritos without his former implant.
- The series closes with the Cerritos continuing ordinary exploratory/service work under the changed command structure while Starbase 80 assumes responsibility for the new multiversal frontier.

### Regression candidates

- Possibility-field ship transformations -> transient alternate states, not historical refits.
- Forged Starfleet order -> institutional-looking record later disconfirmed.
- Deliberate command omission -> Ransom knowledge is not Freeman knowledge.
- Implant removal -> identity continuity despite cybernetic discontinuity.
- Permanent rift -> resolved crisis can produce a new stable but radically changed world-state.
- Freeman transfer / Ransom promotion / shared provisional XOs -> distinct role changes, not identity changes.

## Final-batch regression set

1. Plausible physical evidence can support a false crime model when context is withheld.
2. Undercover/alternate bodily presentation must stay separate from identity.
3. Time dilation requires frame-specific chronology.
4. Joint institutional roles do not merge people.
5. Viewpoint omission is not evidence of nonoccurrence.
6. Multiversal counterparts require explicit reality-qualified relations.
7. False death/institutional records can remain causally important after disconfirmation.
8. Exploration can cause systemic harm without malicious intent.
9. Cybernetic removal can alter capacities/affect while preserving other continuity dimensions.
10. Finale role transitions must be versioned as state changes rather than retrospective rewrites.
