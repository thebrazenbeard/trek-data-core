## Source-relative evidence notes

### 1x07 — First Con-tact

**PRO-B002-E07-01 — prior-crew material is recorded evidence**  
The opening investigation replays incomplete prior-Protostar distress material associated with Captain Chakotay. This establishes a recording being examined, not Chakotay's physical presence in the current scene and not independent verification of the unseen boarding event.

**PRO-B002-E07-02 — Nandi uses deceptive distress framing**  
Nandi initially presents an emergency involving sick orphans. Dal recognizes the signal as deceptive, and later conduct confirms that the mission pitch is exploitative rather than genuine rescue.

**PRO-B002-E07-03 — Dal/Nandi caretaking history is mutually recognized**  
Dal and Nandi recognize a prior caretaker/raised-child relationship and Dal initially responds with attachment. Later evidence sharply qualifies the quality of that relationship without making Dal's attachment unreal.

**PRO-B002-E07-04 — Hologram Janeway carries Starfleet institutional guidance**  
Janeway explains the Prime Directive as a high-priority non-interference rule and warns of developmental consequences. This is evidence of the holographic advisor carrying institutional knowledge, not proof that every later crew interpretation of the rule is infallible.

**PRO-B002-E07-05 — Cymari communication is materially grounded**  
The contacted civilization communicates through harmonics and uses resonant remalite crystals in matter-shaping activity. Crew understanding develops through direct interaction after initial failed translation.

**PRO-B002-E07-06 — Nandi admits exploitation and sale of Dal**  
Nandi admits she sold Dal and used the encounter to obtain both the desired crystal and Protostar chimerium. This disconfirms a benevolent/trustworthy-caretaker interpretation.

**PRO-B002-E07-07 — corrective return does not erase first-contact harm**  
Dal enables return of the stolen crystal. Janeway explicitly distinguishes that correction from undoing the harm and loss of trust caused by the exploitative encounter.

### 1x08 — Time Amok

**PRO-B002-E08-01 — training exercise is explicitly a simulation**  
The fox/chicken/grain exercise ends by computer command. Its animals and events are simulation entities, not physical shipboard organisms/events.

**PRO-B002-E08-02 — Janeway learns the crew are not cadets and remains advisor**  
Dal admits the group stole the ship and are not Starfleet cadets. Hologram Janeway states that, cadets or not, they are still her crew and she is programmed to help.

**PRO-B002-E08-03 — temporal split is phase divergence, not ordinary disappearance**  
The crew remain aboard one ship while experiencing different temporal rates. Their apparent absence from one another is a time-phase effect.

**PRO-B002-E08-04 — remembered explosions are phase-relative experienced outcomes**  
Several crew members experience the Protostar exploding in their own phases and later remember being dead. The eventual repaired sequence leaves them alive. Evidence therefore supports remembered/experienced phase outcomes, not simple globally final deaths followed by resurrection.

**PRO-B002-E08-05 — Hologram Janeway changes operating rate across phases**  
Janeway adjusts temporal settings to interact with divergent phases. This capability belongs to the holographic program occurrence and must not be projected onto biological Kathryn Janeway.

**PRO-B002-E08-06 — Drednok is replicated locally**  
The vehicle replicator produces a Drednok agent from transmitted/loaded data. The agent follows the Diviner's mission and uses Chakotay authorization to terminate the Janeway holo-program. The work supports a derived/replicated local agent, not automatic global identity with every Drednok occurrence.

**PRO-B002-E08-07 — Janeway program termination followed by reconstruction**  
The holo-program is terminated. Rok later reconstructs it because its memory buffers were not overwritten. Evidence supports discontinuity in active execution plus retained/reused memory state; it does not resolve global numerical identity.

**PRO-B002-E08-08 — Rok's extended subjective duration leaves persistent knowledge**  
Rok experiences a much longer subjective interval and teaches herself quantum science, engineering, and mathematics. The learned capability persists after temporal normalization.

### 1x09 — A Moral Star

**PRO-B002-E09-01 — Diviner ultimatum is explicitly a recording**  
The crew identifies the ultimatum as recorded media. It establishes the communicated threat and terms, not the Diviner's physical presence aboard the Protostar.

**PRO-B002-E09-02 — rescue planning becomes collective**  
The crew rejects waiting for distant Starfleet intervention and jointly plans a return to free the miners. Dal explicitly moves away from unilateral improvisation toward group planning.

**PRO-B002-E09-03 — Janeway evaluates the rescue through a Starfleet value frame**  
Hologram Janeway characterizes risking themselves to save others and bring hope as aligned with Starfleet values. This is program-delivered institutional/value testimony, not an omniscient moral verdict.

**PRO-B002-E09-04 — Gwyn's bargain is agency under coercive constraint**  
Gwyn chooses to accompany her father in exchange for the miners' release and transfer of the Rev-12 to her crew. The choice occurs under hostage leverage and should not be flattened into ordinary preference or renewed allegiance.

**PRO-B002-E09-05 — Diviner changes terms and attacks life support**  
After accepting the bargain, the Diviner targets the Rev-12 power generators and distinguishes promising the miners a ship from promising survival. This disconfirms treating his bargain language as a good-faith assurance.

**PRO-B002-E09-06 — replicated Protostar is a decoy**  
The crew uses the vehicle replicator to create a highly convincing physical Protostar replica while the real Proto-Core is covertly removed. The replica is explicitly a decoy and must not be merged with the actual ship occurrence.

**PRO-B002-E09-07 — Zero distinguishes true appearance from ordinary presentation**  
Zero references the dangerous effect of their true appearance, continuing the distinction between the Medusan person and the containment/interface presentation used for ordinary interaction.

### 1x10 — A Moral Star (Part 2)

**PRO-B002-E10-01 — Hologram Janeway operates under program authorization constraints**  
Under the Diviner's command she initially follows local instructions, then crew upgrades allow her to reject his override and assert Starfleet authority. This is evidence about program control state, not biological Janeway's contemporaneous choices.

**PRO-B002-E10-02 — imposed language isolation is materially reversed**  
Short-wave and later repurposed ankle-monitor translation lets formerly mutually unintelligible captives communicate directly, demonstrating an institutional/material barrier to relationships rather than inherent inability to communicate.

**PRO-B002-E10-03 — Rok applies knowledge carried forward from Time Amok**  
Rok reasons through restoration of the Rev-12 engine, continuing the technical competence acquired during her extended subjective interval.

**PRO-B002-E10-04 — Solum presentation is explicitly a simulation frame**  
The Diviner orders `Solum-1` activated and the computer reports simulation upload. Depictions of present and later Solum therefore establish what the simulation presents, not independent objective historical truth.

**PRO-B002-E10-05 — Diviner's temporal-history account is testimony**  
He states that present Solum still exists, that he was sent back in time, and that later first contact with Starfleet precedes a Vau N'Akat civil war. These are high-impact claims requiring separate corroboration; this work directly establishes his testimony and the simulation he uses to support it.

**PRO-B002-E10-06 — anti-Starfleet weapon is described as a future-triggered system**  
The Diviner describes a system aboard the Protostar intended to emit a corrupting signal when it contacts Starfleet ships, turning linked systems against one another. The work establishes his described design and motive; later works must establish actual operation/effects.

**PRO-B002-E10-07 — Zero deliberately exposes true Medusan form**  
Zero opens containment and uses their normally shielded appearance against the Diviner. This reinforces that the visible containment suit is not the entirety of Zero's embodied identity.

**PRO-B002-E10-08 — Gwyn receives only reflected exposure and loses event memory**  
Gwyn is harmed after seeing a reflection rather than Zero directly. Janeway later reports that memory of the event was lost/blurred. The work supports memory-state change in one locally continuous person, not replacement by a copy.

**PRO-B002-E10-09 — Vice Admiral Janeway and Hologram Janeway are distinct simultaneous local occurrences**  
The episode separately depicts a physically present Vice Admiral Janeway commanding a Starfleet vessel while Hologram Janeway remains aboard the Protostar. Same name/persona does not license the worker to collapse them.

## Neutral cross-work coding

The four complete works move from a failed first-contact encounter, through a temporal survival problem, into a collective rescue and confrontation that reveals a larger temporal/political threat.

### Agency and decision points

Dal chooses between loyalty to a former caretaker and institutional/ethical constraints. Crew members solve a temporal catastrophe cooperatively despite separation. The crew collectively chooses rescue despite explicit no-win framing. Gwyn bargains under coercion and later rejects her father's destruction plan. Zero chooses dangerous self-exposure to stop the Diviner.

### Institutions/rules

Starfleet norms are repeatedly taught and tested before formal membership: Prime Directive constraints, first-contact obligations, rescue ethics, crew responsibility, and chain-of-command/program authorization.

### Identity/self-concept

Dal revises his understanding of a formative relationship after new evidence. Zero's containment presentation versus true Medusan appearance remains persistent. Hologram Janeway carries institutional memory/advice while a physically present Vice Admiral Janeway separately exists. Janeway program termination/rebuild creates unresolved continuity evidence. Gwyn remains locally continuous across coercion, reflected exposure, and memory impairment.

This tranche is frame-heavy: distress recordings, deceptive signals, training simulation, phase-relative experiences, replicated agents, recorded ultimatum, explicit Solum simulation, antagonist historical testimony, scanner outputs, and impaired memory. These must remain source-relative.

### Consequences/unresolved threads
