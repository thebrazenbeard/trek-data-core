## Source-relative evidence

### 1x12 — Let Sleeping Borg Lie

**PRO-B003-E12-01 — opening antagonist history is replayed material.** The crew reviews the Diviner's prior explanation of the Living Construct. It is a replay/recording frame, not new live testimony.

**PRO-B003-E12-02 — hidden ship architecture exposes limits in Hologram Janeway's inherited knowledge.** A concealed subdeck associated with the Construct is discovered despite Janeway having no record of it. Her institutional/personality knowledge is useful but demonstrably incomplete.

**PRO-B003-E12-03 — the Borg cube is initially dormant for an identified pathological reason.** The crew attributes drone dormancy to a neurolytic pathogen. This is local technical evidence, not a claim about all Borg.

**PRO-B003-E12-04 — Zero enters the Borg vinculum deliberately for information.** The interface is a chosen action under urgent constraints rather than ordinary passive assimilation.

**PRO-B003-E12-05 — collective language temporarily subsumes Zero's self-presentation.** While linked, the Borg collective denies Zero as a separate individual. That is strong evidence of temporary domination, not sufficient evidence of permanent person replacement.

**PRO-B003-E12-06 — Zero later reasserts self-directed agency.** Zero resists the collective and chooses loyalty to the crew, directly countering a permanent-replacement reading.

**PRO-B003-E12-07 — Borg analysis of the Living Construct is interested evidence.** The Borg say it cannot simply be removed/deactivated while also seeking to assimilate/use the ship. Technical evidence and Borg motive must remain linked.

**PRO-B003-E12-08 — Vice Admiral Janeway's relay interpretation is an inference.** She reasons from the wreck toward what may have happened to Chakotay/Protostar command. Her conclusion is investigative inference until independently supported.

### 1x13 — All the World's a Stage

**PRO-B003-E13-01 — Diviner recollection is impaired.** The recovering Diviner supplies fragments tied to the Protostar, his daughter and Chakotay while explicitly experiencing memory loss. These are recollection/testimony under impairment.

**PRO-B003-E13-02 — Enderprizian Starfleet knowledge is culturally transformed inheritance.** The colony performs distorted Starflight/Starfleet practices transmitted across generations. Similarity establishes transmission, not exact institutional continuity.

**PRO-B003-E13-03 — stage performances are representations.** Enderprizian dramatizations are evidence of belief and cultural memory, not direct depictions of the historical events they imitate.

**PRO-B003-E13-04 — Garrovick history reaches the crew through derivative cultural memory plus material/contextual corroboration.** The episode links the tradition to an antecedent Starfleet contact while preserving the distinction between that contact and later mythologized retellings.

**PRO-B003-E13-05 — inaccurate imitation still preserves meaningful values.** The crew recognizes factual/practical distortions while also finding durable Starfleet-like prosocial norms. This resists a binary authentic/fake model.

**PRO-B003-E13-06 — the health crisis is materially investigated.** Observation, diagnosis and intervention provide evidence independent of the colony's mythic explanations.

**PRO-B003-E13-07 — Second Contact uncertainty is acknowledged.** Hologram Janeway treats this inherited-contact situation as institutionally unusual rather than inventing a simple universal rule.

**PRO-B003-E13-08 — Murf's bodily transformation proceeds without replacement framing.** The narrative continues relationally to treat the changing being as Murf, supporting local continuity across major bodily change.

### 1x14 — Crossroads

**PRO-B003-E14-01 — Diviner memory recovery changes available testimony.** He recovers Gwyndala's name and further fragments; memory access changes within one locally continuous person.

**PRO-B003-E14-02 — Frex supplies hostile eyewitness testimony.** His description of the Protostar crew as station destroyers materially shapes Starfleet pursuit but is contradicted by crew intent and the Living Construct mechanism.

**PRO-B003-E14-03 — Janeway acts under an incomplete causal model.** Vice Admiral Janeway has Frex's account and Chakotay concerns but lacks the crew's Living Construct information. The conflict contains genuine epistemic asymmetry.

**PRO-B003-E14-04 — Gwyn and Asencia meet under concealed-role conditions.** Asencia presents as a Starfleet ensign; covert Vindicator role belongs to separate linked evidence, not retroactive deletion of the presented role.

**PRO-B003-E14-05 — Hologram Janeway points the youths toward physical Janeway as a different present actor.** Her reference to the 'real' Janeway reinforces representational distinction.

**PRO-B003-E14-06 — the Living Construct blocks safe direct communication.** The crew avoids direct system linkage while Starfleet interprets silence/evasion without knowing the reason.

**PRO-B003-E14-07 — Neutral Zone pursuit creates an internal Starfleet command conflict.** Janeway prioritizes pursuit while Tysess invokes treaty/war risk and refuses an order that would cross the boundary.

**PRO-B003-E14-08 — Murf's new form retains local relational continuity.** The crew immediately recognizes and treats the transformed being as Murf.

### 1x15 — Masquerade

**PRO-B003-E15-01 — Romulan attackers explicitly distinguish Hologram Janeway as holographic.** They target holo-emitter infrastructure rather than treating her as the physical admiral.

**PRO-B003-E15-02 — Jago's Dal analysis is scientist/scanner evidence.** Doctor Jago concludes Dal is an artificial hybrid drawing from multiple species and associates the work with protégés of Arik Soong. This remains provenance-bearing technical testimony.

**PRO-B003-E15-03 — Dal's epigenetic implant exposes latent traits temporarily.** Activation causes multiple abilities/physical traits to manifest in one continuing local person.

**PRO-B003-E15-04 — implant removal precedes permanent stabilization.** The source supplies counterevidence against treating every manifested phenotype as a lasting identity state.

**PRO-B003-E15-05 — Okona is physically present locally.** This lane records the local occurrence only and does not globally reconcile prior franchise appearances.

**PRO-B003-E15-06 — physical Janeway remains simultaneous with Hologram Janeway.** Shared name/persona does not collapse two contemporaneous occurrences.

**PRO-B003-E15-07 — Asencia/Vindicator uses a concealed Starfleet role.** The covert mission and Drednok coordination require role/alias state handling rather than erasing earlier Starfleet-presented evidence.

**PRO-B003-E15-08 — Dal's origin revelation changes self-concept, not local numerical continuity.** Learning he was engineered changes classification and self-understanding while the source continues to treat him as the same actor.

### 1x16 — Preludes

**PRO-B003-E16-01 — crew backstories are recollection/story frames.** Rok-Tahk, Zero, Jankom and others narrate earlier experiences; these should not be flattened into unframed omniscient depiction.

**PRO-B003-E16-02 — Rok's exploitation history is her recollection.** Her account directly establishes testimony and present emotional meaning while preserving the memory frame.

**PRO-B003-E16-03 — Zero recounts pre-containment Medusan existence, capture and rescue.** The self-testimony distinguishes Zero from the containment/interface presentation without requiring a global ontology verdict.

**PRO-B003-E16-04 — Jankom's origin history is narrated personal history.** Cryogenic/deep-space and engineering experiences arrive through his account.

**PRO-B003-E16-05 — Hologram Janeway recounts antecedent Janeway biography from inherited program knowledge.** This supports inherited informational/personality content, not literal proof the hologram personally lived the biological person's past.

**PRO-B003-E16-06 — Diviner/Vindicator future-Solum mission history is participant recollection/testimony.** Time travel makes the claims consequential, not omniscient.

**PRO-B003-E16-07 — Diviner and Vindicator are explicitly adopted mission titles/roles.** The account distinguishes operational identities from an assertion that persons were destroyed/replaced when names changed.

**PRO-B003-E16-08 — Vindicator attacks the recovering/amnesiac Diviner to protect the mission.** Present agency and mission priority coexist with the Diviner's impaired memory state.

## Candidate interpretive assertions

1. Prodigy repeatedly separates inherited informational/personality continuity from physical-person identity; Hologram Janeway can transmit institutional/biographical knowledge without global merger with Vice Admiral Janeway.
2. Temporary Borg collective domination of Zero is identity-relevant but countered by later self-directed agency; permanent replacement is unsupported here.
3. Enderprizian Starflight is a culturally transformed descendant of Starfleet contact/information, not straightforward institutional sameness.
4. A performed or mythologized account is evidence about cultural memory first; represented historical events require independent support.
5. Murf's metamorphosis supplies strong local continuity-across-body-change evidence without establishing a universal biological identity rule.
6. The `Crossroads` conflict is substantially driven by asymmetric evidence rather than simple irrationality or bad faith.
7. Dal's induced multi-species trait expression is an altered biological state of one local person, not a sequence of species-person identities.
8. Dal's engineered-origin revelation changes self-concept/classification evidence without breaking local person continuity.
9. Asencia's public Starfleet presentation and covert Vindicator mission are temporally scoped role/alias states.
10. Recollection-heavy `Preludes` requires explicit memory/testimony framing; later corroboration can strengthen propositions without changing this source's frame.
11. Mission titles such as Diviner/Vindicator are adopted operational identities, not evidence of literal replacement.
12. Institutional affiliation, self-concept, physical embodiment, memory access and representational continuity vary independently enough to justify lazy continuity dimensions.

## Explicit counterevidence / unresolved points

- Borg collective language temporarily denies Zero's individuality, but Zero later returns to self-directed action; neither half should be discarded.
- Enderprizians preserve substantial Starfleet-derived values while distorting facts/practices; neither `SAME_AS Starfleet` nor `mere fake` fits the evidence.
- Frex is genuinely an eyewitness but is causally incomplete and hostile; observation status does not make attribution infallible.
- Jago provides strong technical evidence about Dal, yet later works may refine the engineered-lineage account.
- Hologram Janeway's inherited biography may be accurate while remaining inherited/programmed knowledge rather than autobiographical lived memory.
- Diviner/Vindicator future-history claims are participant testimony; temporal origin does not itself make causal interpretation omniscient.
