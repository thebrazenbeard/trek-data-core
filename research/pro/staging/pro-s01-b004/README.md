## Source-relative evidence

### 1x17 — Ghost in the Machine

**PRO-B004-E17-01 — the opening battle is explicitly a simulation.** Repeated apparent combat ends on command with `End simulation`; casualties/damage inside that drill are not external fleet events.

**PRO-B004-E17-02 — later reality-like sequences remain holodeck program frames.** The crew moves through multiple apparently independent scenarios before recognizing they are inside a composite/trapping simulation. Simulated persons and places are not silently promoted to physical counterparts.

**PRO-B004-E17-03 — disabled safety constraints make simulated threats materially consequential.** A simulated frame can injure or endanger physical participants without becoming objective external history.

**PRO-B004-E17-04 — crew uncertainty about whether a scene is real is itself evidence.** The source repeatedly destabilizes their frame judgments; staging therefore preserves UNKNOWN/contested-frame moments rather than coding every apparent event at face value.

**PRO-B004-E17-05 — Hologram Janeway's behavior is compromised by the Living Construct.** Her actions assist the Construct while she lacks ordinary awareness/control of what she is doing. The source supports an externally compromised program state rather than an inference that her enduring personality became malicious.

**PRO-B004-E17-06 — Hologram Janeway lacks memory for some compromised actions.** Missing access to actions supplies a program-memory/agency distinction and later motivates keeping her apart from operational plans.

**PRO-B004-E17-07 — the Construct gains increasing control of Protostar operations.** Locked controls and forced course changes are material ship-state evidence, not voluntary crew navigation.

**PRO-B004-E17-08 — simulation experience still has durable interpersonal/epistemic consequences.** Recognizing manipulation changes crew strategy even though many depicted events were simulated.

### 1x18 — Mindwalk

**PRO-B004-E18-01 — Hologram Janeway explicitly reports involuntary Construct control.** She states the Construct used her and she had no control, then withdraws so ignorance can prevent further sabotage. This is direct testimony about compromised agency.

**PRO-B004-E18-02 — Vindicator conceals the physical admiral's condition and role.** Asencia/Vindicator and Drednok hide Vice Admiral Janeway and falsify the appearance of normal command continuity. Presented Starfleet role and covert mission remain distinct evidence streams.

**PRO-B004-E18-03 — Dal and Vice Admiral Janeway undergo a neural-pattern/body swap.** The crew explicitly identifies Janeway's mind/neural pattern in Dal's body and Dal's in Janeway's body. Psychological/neural and biological continuity dimensions therefore diverge locally.

**PRO-B004-E18-04 — social/legal authority follows presented body imperfectly.** Dal in Janeway's body can be treated as the admiral despite behaving unlike her; Janeway in Dal's body possesses Janeway's memories/intent but not her institutional embodiment. Identity dimensions have practical consequences without a worker-level global verdict.

**PRO-B004-E18-05 — the proposed mechanism is an in-story scientific inference.** Zero/Rok attribute the swap to Dal's augmented Organian DNA and an energy/telepathic conduit. Preserve this as their technical analysis, not an unqualified universal law.

**PRO-B004-E18-06 — Hologram Janeway is rebuilt after Construct corruption.** Admiral-level access is used to purge/rebuild corrupted hologram files; memory returns, including first-crew/Chakotay material. Program repair plus memory recovery is continuity evidence without proving metaphysical numerical identity.

**PRO-B004-E18-07 — restored body/mind pairing occurs through deliberate recreation of the conduit.** Dal and Janeway physically contact across the shared warp-field event and return to their ordinary bodies. The source supports continuity across the swap and reversal.

**PRO-B004-E18-08 — Janeway revises her judgment of the youths after direct embodied interaction.** She recognizes earlier assumptions were wrong and promises a fair hearing, showing an epistemic change based on newly acquired evidence.

### 1x19 — Supernova, Part 1

**PRO-B004-E19-01 — the Living Construct reaches its intended Federation target environment.** Protostar arrival in dense Starfleet/Federation space permits the Construct's corruption mechanism to operate at scale.

**PRO-B004-E19-02 — infected Starfleet ships attack one another under systems corruption.** Observed fleet violence is linked to network/system takeover, not evidence that each crew independently chose hostility.

**PRO-B004-E19-03 — Starfleet initially possesses a distorted attribution model.** Some officers understand the Protostar as stolen by imposters/criminals while lacking the youths' causal evidence. Their statements are institutionally consequential testimony, not omniscient narration.

**PRO-B004-E19-04 — the crew attempts to stop damage rather than exploit it.** Their actions supply direct counterevidence to the hostile-imposter narrative.

**PRO-B004-E19-05 — Vindicator/Drednok actions remain covert hostile agency distinct from Starfleet command.** Their infiltration and mission cannot be attributed to the Starfleet role being impersonated.

**PRO-B004-E19-06 — Vice Admiral Janeway's restored understanding separates crew intent from Construct effects.** Her evidence state changes after `Mindwalk`; this helps resolve the prior pursuit's epistemic asymmetry.

**PRO-B004-E19-07 — networked institutional infrastructure amplifies a single malicious mechanism.** Repeated apparent corroboration across multiple ships is not independent hostile intent; it derives from one Construct attack path.

**PRO-B004-E19-08 — the crisis remains unresolved at the part-one boundary.** Finale-part-two outcomes are not back-projected into Part 1 evidence.

### 1x20 — Supernova, Part 2

**PRO-B004-E20-01 — destroying Protostar is selected as the only available way to stop the long-range signal.** Crew technical discussion distinguishes local Proto-Core destruction risk from spreading the release across space-time via simultaneous Proto-Jump.

**PRO-B004-E20-02 — Hologram Janeway volunteers to remain because physical escape requires someone to control detonation.** Her decision is agency by the active holographic program occurrence, not biological Janeway's act.

**PRO-B004-E20-03 — the youths explicitly attribute person-like qualities to Hologram Janeway.** They cite memories, personality and friendship when objecting that she is 'not just a hologram.' This establishes their personhood judgment; ontology remains an interpretive/reconciliation question.

**PRO-B004-E20-04 — Janeway proposes making a portable program copy, but the copy cannot actually be made.** Upload reports insufficient capacity; she later reveals her program had grown too large/complex for the chip and that the apparent portable Janeway was only a prerecorded message. Do not create a surviving Janeway-copy entity from the proposal alone.

**PRO-B004-E20-05 — prerecorded farewell Janeway is recorded media distinct from the destroyed active program.** The tiny Janeway message explicitly identifies itself as prerecorded. It preserves information but is not evidence that the full active hologram survived.

**PRO-B004-E20-06 — the active Hologram Janeway remains aboard and is lost with Protostar.** Her final actions include ship control and wormhole-parameter manipulation. The source supplies an actual termination/loss event for this active program state.

**PRO-B004-E20-07 — Protostar destruction creates an interspatial rift/wormhole with minimized local damage.** Starfleet later reports the simultaneous core destabilization/warp maneuver and receives a signal from approximately the same space-time coordinates.

**PRO-B004-E20-08 — Chakotay's newly received mayday is a recording from another time.** The message is dated 52 years in the future and reports capture/weapon plans. It establishes a recorded future-origin transmission, not Chakotay's physical presence in the hearing room.

**PRO-B004-E20-09 — Starfleet's legal/institutional judgment changes but does not equal Academy admission.** Charges are dropped; five youths are taken as warrant officers in training under Janeway rather than fast-tracked into the Academy. Institutional aspiration, legal status and membership/training status remain distinct.

**PRO-B004-E20-10 — Gwyn chooses a separate Solum mission.** She declines the five-person training path and accepts aid to return home ahead of Federation first contact, explicitly seeking to affect her people's future without collapsing present Solum and future-war Solum into one temporal state.

## Candidate assertions

1. `Ghost in the Machine` demonstrates why simulation-frame identity cannot be inferred from phenomenological realism; simulated events can have physical consequences while remaining simulations.
2. Construct-compromised Hologram Janeway is one local program occurrence across an agency/control discontinuity; malicious actions under external control do not by themselves prove personality replacement.
3. `Mindwalk` directly separates psychological/neural continuity from biological embodiment, making a single binary identity dimension inadequate.
4. Dal-in-Janeway-body and Janeway-in-Dal-body must remain distinct local person/body-state records until reconciliation defines the required relation.
5. Hologram Janeway rebuild after corruption supplies informational/memory continuity evidence but should not be flattened into a metaphysical identity verdict.
6. Fleet-on-fleet violence in `Supernova` is derivative evidence from one networked Construct mechanism, not independent corroboration of voluntary Starfleet hostility.
7. The attempted Hologram Janeway copy in Part 2 fails; the surviving portable artifact is a prerecorded message, so proposal, recording and active program must remain separate.
8. The active Hologram Janeway has a source-supported terminal loss aboard Protostar while biological Vice Admiral Janeway remains separately present.
9. Future Chakotay transmission is temporally displaced recorded evidence; its content requires explicit temporal scope.
10. Starfleet charges, Academy admission, warrant-officer training and Gwyn's Solum mission are separate institutional/legal states rather than one `joined Starfleet` flag.
11. Season 1 repeatedly demonstrates independent biological, psychological, informational, legal, institutional, memory and timeline dimensions; lazy identity modeling is required by evidence rather than imposed in advance.

## Explicit counterevidence / unresolved points

- Hologram Janeway's compromised actions are real actions of the program state, even though she lacks ordinary control/awareness; external control does not make the events unreal.
- The body swap is reversed and both participants retain continuity, but the episode itself uses mind/body language rather than supplying a general metaphysical rule.
- The crew's personhood language for Hologram Janeway is significant testimony, not automatic objective fictional truth.
- The attempted portable copy fails. A prerecorded message survives temporarily, but it does not contain the full grown program according to Janeway's own explanation.
- Future Chakotay's mayday establishes his recorded testimony at a future coordinate/time, not independent verification of every causal claim in the message.
