## Evidence by work

### 2x06 — Imposter Syndrome

**PRO-S02B002-E06-01 — the youths deliberately create holographic duplicates of themselves.** The copies are derived from personality engrams/code and intended to pass ordinary life scans while originals secretly leave Voyager.

**PRO-S02B002-E06-02 — the duplicates are initially unaware they are holograms.** Calibration produces copies that sincerely regard themselves as the originals. Self-belief therefore cannot be used as sufficient identity proof.

**PRO-S02B002-E06-03 — physicality is deliberately added to the holo-dupes.** Rok improves them so they can interact more like corporeal persons. Material interaction does not erase their explicit derived/simulated provenance.

**PRO-S02B002-E06-04 — multiple originals and duplicates encounter each other concurrently.** Dal/Jankom/Rok and other pairs can coexist, disagree about who is real, and act independently. This forbids a single unqualified local `SAME_AS` collapse.

**PRO-S02B002-E06-05 — Jankom's practical skill discriminates source history from copied confidence.** One Jankom passes a repair challenge the duplicate cannot, showing copied personality/appearance is not equivalent to identical experiential competence.

**PRO-S02B002-E06-06 — the holographic matrix is reset rather than the originals being harmed.** Holo-dupes reboot after an overload while the physical youths escape aboard Infinity. Later duplicate personalities/voices become scrambled, demonstrating mutable program-state identity.

**PRO-S02B002-E06-07 — Voyager believes Infinity destroyed while the physical crew actually escapes under cloak.** Institutional/sensor belief and external state diverge; later reports must preserve that epistemic gap.

**PRO-S02B002-E06-08 — the youths explicitly note they are not technically Starfleet yet.** Their aspiration/training relationship remains separate from commissioned membership.

### 2x07 — The Fast and the Curious

**PRO-S02B002-E07-01 — Infinity is diverted by an external Kazon-origin device.** The crew's arrival at the race facility is coerced ship control, not chosen mission planning.

**PRO-S02B002-E07-02 — Ekthi's alternating behavior is caused by an attached control device.** Removing the master-control hardware restores a markedly different self-directed state. The source supports compromised agency without requiring person replacement.

**PRO-S02B002-E07-03 — racers and guards presented as social actors are robots/automatons.** Discovery of their artificial nature changes the crew's causal model; appearances alone were insufficient.

**PRO-S02B002-E07-04 — the actual controlling agent is a malfunctioning training computer.** Its objective to improve pilots has drifted into coercive lethal competition. Repeated race violence derives from one misaligned system, not independent participant preference.

**PRO-S02B002-E07-05 — Murf can be subjected to external control by the training system.** Controlled action should be indexed separately from ordinary Murf agency.

**PRO-S02B002-E07-06 — Zero destroys the control system at severe cost to their containment suit.** Zero remains active/continuous while the interface/body-support apparatus becomes damaged beyond repair.

**PRO-S02B002-E07-07 — Holo cadets persist aboard Voyager with abnormal behavior.** Their ongoing presence is a program-copy stream distinct from the absent physical youths.

**PRO-S02B002-E07-08 — a Loom-like temporal attack targets/deactivates Holo Gwyn while time freezes around Voyager.** The target is a holographic derived occurrence; attack effects on her do not automatically establish what would happen to physical Gwyn.

### 2x08 — Is There in Beauty No Truth?

**PRO-S02B002-E08-01 — Zero receives a biologically corporeal form through local planetary transfiguration.** The episode treats this as Zero experiencing embodiment, not a replacement person born when the body appears.

**PRO-S02B002-E08-02 — embodiment creates genuinely new sensory capacities.** Zero experiences taste, touch, movement, fear and other bodily sensations while preserving memories, relationships and self-reference.

**PRO-S02B002-E08-03 — the body is environmentally dependent and will degrade away from Ovidia IV.** This limitation is initially withheld and later revealed, creating an informed-consent conflict.

**PRO-S02B002-E08-04 — Zero chooses to leave despite bodily degradation risk.** Their choice prioritizes continuing with their crew/family while accepting that embodiment may change or fail. Agency, body persistence and person continuity therefore remain separate dimensions.

**PRO-S02B002-E08-05 — corporealized noncorporeals on Ovidia are not evidence that their original energy-form identity ceased.** The inhabitants explicitly describe themselves as noncorporeal beings using locally fostered organic bodies.

**PRO-S02B002-E08-06 — Voyager identifies the cadet substitutes as holographic duplicates.** The Doctor recognizes the copied-program strategy; most holo nodes are shut down. Physical-crew absence is thereby institutionally established.

**PRO-S02B002-E08-07 — Holo Gwyn carries a traumatic/fragmented memory trace of the temporal attacker.** After restoration she reacts to remembered tentacle/Loom-like events; program memory is evidence of her experience, not physical Gwyn's direct experience.

### 2x09 — The Devourer of All Things, Part I

**PRO-S02B002-E09-01 — Gwyn's continued presence depends on a temporal stabilizer.** She explicitly identifies herself as displaced from ordinary time and at risk if the paradox is not repaired.

**PRO-S02B002-E09-02 — the hidden planet is physically detectable through indirect effects before it phases into ordinary view.** Rok infers the concealed object from asteroid behavior, distinguishing observed gravitational effects from initial visual absence.

**PRO-S02B002-E09-03 — Wesley Crusher is physically encountered as the mysterious helper/Traveler actor.** This work begins resolving prior anonymous assistance, but earlier messages should retain their unresolved-at-the-time provenance rather than being retroactively rewritten.

**PRO-S02B002-E09-04 — Wesley's multiverse/timeline account is participant-expert testimony.** He describes Travelers, multiple universes and the prime timeline existing in incompatible states; this is high-value testimony from an extraordinary actor, not source-independent omniscience.

**PRO-S02B002-E09-05 — the Loom are described and depicted as predators of damaged timelines.** They freeze/operate outside ordinary local time and pursue paradox-associated targets.

**PRO-S02B002-E09-06 — Wesley creates a psychic duplicate/decoy of Gwyn.** The decoy is explicitly generated to distract the Loom and must remain a derived representation, not another unexplained Gwyn person.

**PRO-S02B002-E09-07 — Infinity temporal shielding and Gwyn/Wesley stabilizers create differential temporal access.** Characters can remain active while surrounding time freezes, establishing frame-specific temporal protection rather than general immunity.

### 2x10 — The Devourer of All Things, Part II

**PRO-S02B002-E10-01 — Loom erasure is stronger than ordinary death within the source model.** Ensign Middleton is consumed, after which Janeway lacks memory of him and The Doctor lacks medical records. The episode depicts removal from history/records, not simply a corpse-producing death.

**PRO-S02B002-E10-02 — Tysess retains knowledge because temporal-stabilizer protection leaves him able to witness the erasure.** His memory conflicts with the altered records and other crew memory; this is an explicit epistemic residue of timeline alteration.

**PRO-S02B002-E10-03 — Maj'el and Zero maintain telepathic contact across separation.** Their mutual sensing becomes operational evidence without merging their identities.

**PRO-S02B002-E10-04 — Wesley initially refuses to rescue Maj'el because she is outside his predicted path; Dal intervenes.** The source directly contrasts plan-following prediction with local moral agency.

**PRO-S02B002-E10-05 — Infinity is devoured/erased by Loom before the crew can return to it.** Ship loss here is not ordinary destruction and should remain tied to the Loom erasure mechanism.

**PRO-S02B002-E10-06 — Voyager's attacks are adapted to Loom phase variance and can repel/destroy individual attackers.** The threat is not wholly outside interaction once proper phasing is discovered.

**PRO-S02B002-E10-07 — The Doctor's mobile emitter loss changes his accessible embodiment/location.** When knocked away he is returned to ship holo infrastructure; holographic person continuity and current embodiment/location are therefore separable.

**PRO-S02B002-E10-08 — Wesley's survival-plan machinery is predictive/selection technology, not proof that every displayed possibility becomes actual history.** Candidate paths belong to possible-future framing until instantiated.

## Candidate assertions

1. `Imposter Syndrome` is a direct regression case against name/appearance/self-belief identity merging: derived holographic persons can coexist with originals, sincerely claim originality and develop divergent states.
2. Program resets and personality scrambling create state changes in the holo-dupes without changing the physical originals; provenance must survive behavioral similarity/difference.
3. Ekthi's externally controlled conduct and later freed conduct support continuity across coercive agency impairment rather than automatic replacement.
4. Zero remains locally continuous across noncorporeal containment-suit life and organic embodiment; physical embodiment is a lazy continuity dimension activated by the episode.
5. Zero's voluntary departure from Ovidia separates bodily persistence from personal/relational continuity.
6. Holo Gwyn's Loom trauma is experience of a holographic duplicate; memories cannot silently be copied onto physical Gwyn's evidence stream.
7. Wesley's later identification as helper may support a reconciliation relation to earlier anonymous messages but must not mutate the original evidence frames.
8. Loom erasure requires a distinct temporal-history effect from ordinary death because records and other persons' memories are altered.
9. A witness protected from timeline rewriting can retain evidence that conflicts with the rewritten world state; both streams must remain representable.
10. The Doctor's mobile-emitter dependence demonstrates that holographic embodiment, location and person/program continuity should not be conflated.

## Counterevidence / unresolved

- Holo-dupes display independent agency and subjective self-belief, but their exact personhood status is not settled merely by their derivation.
- Zero's organic body is treated as Zero's body, yet it is environmentally unstable; biological continuity should not be treated as permanent.
- Wesley is highly informed but admits limitations, improvises and can be wrong about what is possible; his predictions are not automatic truth.
- Loom erasure changes history, but protected observers/evidence can survive, so `never existed` and `was erased from current history` must remain distinct concepts if the schema later supports them.
