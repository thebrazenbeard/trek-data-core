### 2x11 — Last Flight of the Protostar, Part I

**PRO-S02B003-E11-01 — Chakotay and the Protostar are physically reached on a hidden world.** Wesley's intervention deposits the travelers at the location where Chakotay has survived; this is not a recording or simulation encounter.

**PRO-S02B003-E11-03 — asymmetric memory does not license duplicate collapse or replacement.** The youths' remembered Hologram Janeway includes later experiences; this earlier instance lacks them. Any reconciliation relation must preserve temporal/program-state order.

**PRO-S02B003-E11-04 — Chakotay has endured roughly a decade of isolation in his local chronology.** His hopelessness, defensive behavior and reluctance to resume the mission are consequences of lived duration, not evidence that a different Chakotay replaced him.

**PRO-S02B003-E11-05 — Adreek belongs to Chakotay's earlier isolation history and is no longer a present crew actor.** References to Adreek are recollection/history until physical evidence of his fate is encountered; do not create contemporaneous presence from dialogue alone.

**PRO-S02B003-E11-06 — the youths explain an altered history to a Chakotay who did not experience their remembered version.** Their account is testimony about another temporal path from his perspective, not a memory he suddenly shares.

**PRO-S02B003-E11-07 — the Protostar is physically damaged/depleted rather than ontologically absent.** Recovery depends on material repair and energy resources.

**PRO-S02B003-E11-08 — Chakotay's return to purposeful action is a character-state change within one local continuous person.** New evidence and the youths' arrival reverse his prior resignation.
