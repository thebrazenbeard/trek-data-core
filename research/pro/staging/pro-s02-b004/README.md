## Evidence by work

### 2x16 — Ascension, Part II

**PRO-S02B004-E16-01 — Asencia's temporal weapons create localized accelerated aging/time exposure.** Voyager and nearby actors encounter chronometric radiation capable of causing decades of biological aging in moments. This is a temporal-effect frame, not ordinary disease or baseline aging.

**PRO-S02B004-E16-02 — Zero volunteers because noncorporeal continuity can survive where the Ovidian body cannot.** The crew explicitly recognizes that approaching the radiation will destroy/age the organic body while Zero's underlying noncorporeal existence offers a different survival relation.

**PRO-S02B004-E16-03 — loss of the Ovidian body is not framed as Zero's death.** Zero continues to communicate/act after the organic embodiment fails, directly separating body termination from local person continuity.

**PRO-S02B004-E16-04 — Jankom builds a replacement embodiment rather than a duplicate person.** The new body is engineered with sensory interfaces for Zero's continued use; the source treats Zero as the same self entering/using a new embodiment.

**PRO-S02B004-E16-05 — Zero explicitly evaluates the new body through first-person continuity.** The desired criterion is whether the body lets Zero feel like themself, not whether it reproduces a prior biological form.

**PRO-S02B004-E16-06 — Asencia's military advantage derives from temporally accelerated development.** Her technological progress is materially observed and linked to temporal manipulation; this is not ordinary years-long R&D elapsed for outside observers.

**PRO-S02B004-E16-07 — Starfleet response remains constrained by Solum political/treaty consequences.** Security necessity and risk of wider conflict remain separate institutional considerations.

**PRO-S02B004-E16-08 — Protostar recovery and temporal-loop repair remain unresolved while the Solum crisis escalates.** Immediate tactical success does not complete the season's causality problem.

**PRO-S02B004-E20-10 — Wesley's desire that the group remain together is remembered as guidance, not command authority over Starfleet commissions.** Dal interprets later events through that earlier statement; the legal commission comes from Starfleet/Janeway.

## Candidate assertions

1. Zero provides one of the corpus's clearest cases where person continuity survives termination of one biological embodiment and continues into an engineered replacement body; body identity and person identity are distinct evidence dimensions.
2. The new Zero body is not a duplicate Zero merely because it is manufactured; the source depicts transfer/continued agency of the locally continuous Zero occurrence.
3. Wesley's strategic foreknowledge is fallible/actor-bound expert evidence, not an omniscient narrator privilege.
4. Deliberate capture can coexist with genuine physical vulnerability; later intent does not make the experienced danger fictional.
5. The finale creates two Hologram Janeway program-provenance branches: a preserved full-memory backup aboard Voyager and a reset historical Protostar instance. Global reconciliation must not erase the branching event.
6. Season 1's failed Hologram Janeway copy attempt and Season 2's successful EMH-backup copy are disconfirming/contrasting evidence against any rule that holographic programs are inherently copyable or inherently uncopyable; capacity/context matter.
7. Returning Protostar and deliberately placing Dal's combadge are causal-loop maintenance acts performed by agents with knowledge of the loop, not grounds to discard provenance as 'always happened.'
8. Restored prime history does not automatically license deletion of alternate-reality evidence; `Cracked Mirror` realities remain source-supported scoped realities unless the source explicitly states their annihilation.
9. Starfleet institutional status changes across the series must be versioned: asylum applicant → trainee/warrant-officer path → probation/assignment states → field-commissioned ensign.
10. USS Prodigy is a later distinct ship occurrence, not an identity continuation of the returned Protostar solely because of class/design lineage.

## Explicit counterevidence / unresolved points

- Zero's new body is engineered and explicitly intended for the same Zero, but future evidence could challenge continuity of sensation/memory/agency; this work supports continuity without creating a universal transfer rule.
- Hologram Janeway's successful backup produces strong copy/derivation evidence. Whether the backup and reset instance count as numerically the same person is a reconciliation question, not a worker assertion.
- The timeline is described as repaired/reintegrated, but the source does not thereby prove every alternate universe ceased to exist.
- The self-consistent placement of Dal's combadge can be described as a causal loop without asserting that free agency is impossible inside the loop.
- Field commissions establish Starfleet ensign status; they do not retroactively turn earlier actions into official commissioned service.
