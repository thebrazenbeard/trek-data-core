## Cross-work hypothesis result

Four provisional hypotheses were tested across the staged title set:

1. constrained self-presentation/concealment as a recurring agency mechanism;
2. institutional and machine-mediated information as actionable but non-omniscient;
3. frame transitions as a recurring cause of retrospective evidence reclassification;
4. agency as action under fear/social/institutional pressure rather than simple elimination of pressure.

No hypothesis survived as a universal Short Treks rule. Each has neutral and/or disconfirming cases. The strongest cross-work result is methodological: source type, narrative frame, institutional origin, repeated appearance, and testimony materially change evidentiary weight and must not be flattened during later governed conversion.
