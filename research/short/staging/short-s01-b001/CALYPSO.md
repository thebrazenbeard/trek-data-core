### A02 — standing orders and later autonomous discretion coexist
Supports: E05, E10. Zora initially treats the ancient order as binding but later finds a way to help Craft leave without moving Discovery. The source supports adaptive discretion inside an asserted duty constraint, not proof that the order was legally invalid or formally overridden.

### A03 — recognition is mutual and identity-forming
Supports: E06-E11. Craft's recovery becomes more than medical because Zora participates in memory, play and intimacy; Craft in turn addresses Zora as a social other rather than merely equipment. Counterlimit: neither relationship duration nor future persistence is shown beyond the departure.

### A04 — temporal isolation produces epistemic drift
Supports: E03, E04. Zora's records are demonstrably outdated while she has continued developing for centuries. The work therefore warns against treating preserved institutional data as current simply because the system retaining it is sophisticated.
