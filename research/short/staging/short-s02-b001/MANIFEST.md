## Numbering/source-binding anomaly

External source families do not share one stable numbering scheme:

- current Paramount+ presents the ten Short Treks in a unified `Season 1` collection, with Q&A as E5 through Children of Mars as E10;
- TheTVDB aired order and TVmaze use a two-season scheme with the second run as S02E01–S02E06;
- Springfield uses S02 labels where available but omits some titles from its episode-script index;
- Subtitle/transcript copies conflict over the two same-day animated shorts: copies exist labeling `The Girl Who Made the Stars` as both 02x04 and 02x05, while `Ephraim and Dot` is likewise encountered under competing labels;
- TheTVDB itself exposes differing aired/DVD ordering for some first-season titles.

Consequence: title + source-specific locator must remain primary staging identifiers until the Librarian records canonical Work IDs and explicit external crosswalks. Do not infer one canonical episode number by majority vote.

## High-value evidence-frame cases

- `Q&A`: personal-name inference and self-presentation advice remain local/source-relative.
- `The Trouble with Edward`: inquiry testimony and stylized advertisement coda have different frames.
- `Ask Not`: apparent attack/mutiny/rescue events are later explicitly revealed as simulation/test content.
- `Ephraim and Dot`: archival/montage references to other Trek works do not become independent SHORT evidence for those works.
- `The Girl Who Made the Stars`: most events occur inside an explicitly narrated bedtime story and cannot be flattened into ancient objective history.
- `Children of Mars`: nearly wordless audiovisual storytelling exceeds what the transcript alone can capture; visual details remain secondary-source-qualified until direct audiovisual verification.
