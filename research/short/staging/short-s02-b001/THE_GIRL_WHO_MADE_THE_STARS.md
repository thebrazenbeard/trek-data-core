## Interpretive assertions

### A01 — the inner tale is evidence of parenting/storytelling, not ancient objective history
Supports: E02-E10. The work explicitly embeds the African/cosmic events inside a father's bedtime narration. The evidence ledger should preserve the story propositions and their effect on Michael without silently asserting that stars were literally created by the narrated child/visitor.

### A02 — fear is reframed through curiosity and action rather than denied
Supports: E01, E05-E10. Both Michael and the story protagonist experience fear; movement comes from acting while afraid and changing interpretation through closer encounter.

### A03 — social rules and cosmology can share a narrative fear function
Supports: E03-E04. The inner community's night-beast explanation and travel prohibition mutually reinforce one another in the tale. Counterlimit: because this is a fable, it cannot establish an anthropological claim about a real historical culture.

### A04 — the ending demonstrates local meaning uptake, not permanent personality transformation
Supports: E10. Michael's rejection of the night-light shows immediate change after the story; it does not prove all future fear is resolved.
