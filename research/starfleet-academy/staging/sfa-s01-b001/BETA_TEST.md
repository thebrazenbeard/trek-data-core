## Neutral coding summary

### Plot/problem structure
Two linked problems drive the work: Caleb's attempt to locate his mother and decide whether to remain at the Academy, and Federation negotiations with Betazed over political reintegration and security.

### Agency and decision points
Caleb chooses whether to remain, whether to disclose motives, and whether to ask Tarima to re-engage her father. Tarima chooses whether to trust Caleb, advocate for a larger world, and ultimately attend the War College. Nahla chooses to stop treating Caleb as a child to be contained and proposes a political concession that changes the negotiation.

### Institutions/rules
The Academy enforces room assignments, curfew, permitted movement, classes, admissions expectations, and disciplinary labor while simultaneously presenting itself as rebuilding and willing to revise tradition. The War College remains a neighboring but distinct institutional option.

### Interpersonal dynamics
Caleb/Darem roommate hostility, Caleb/Tarima attraction and distrust, Nahla/Caleb guardian-authority tension, Genesis/Sam reassurance, and Vance/Nahla strategic disagreement all coexist without being collapsed into simple affiliation labels.

### Identity/self-concept
Caleb resists institutional and criminal labels; Nahla distinguishes present Caleb from the child she remembers; Tarima describes unusually intense empathic ability requiring regulation; several cadets question whether they fit.

### Epistemic/evidence patterns
The Goja V sequence is the clearest example: a computer's initial non-recognition is later changed by access to a different dataset. Political-history claims about the Burn, Betazed, raids, and prior Federation actions remain testimony unless separately corroborated.

### Ethics/value conflict
Security versus democratic constraint, protection versus autonomy, institutional rules versus exceptional pathways, privacy versus trust, and tradition versus change are all explicit conflicts.

### Technology/material constraints
Self-replicating mucus, holo-guides, database access controls, Betazoid psionic barriers, neuro-inhibition, star charts, and communication/intelligence channels shape what characters can know and do.

### Culture/politics
Betazoid isolation, youth activism, Federation reconstruction, council representation, security guarantees, and government-seat location are central political material.

### Humor/form
The episode uses classroom absurdity, roommate jokes, the unreliable Turner-history riff, the Doctor's operatic performance, and the digital dean announcements to puncture otherwise high-stakes political and identity material.

### Character-state changes
Caleb moves from active escape attempts toward conditional investment in the Academy and a more explicit search path for Anisha. Nahla loosens a containment/guardian stance. Tarima broadens direct engagement with the outside world but chooses the War College. Ocam chooses the Academy.

### Consequences/unresolved threads
- Anisha's manifest creates a new lead but does not locate her directly.
- Betazed appears to accept renewed negotiations, but implementation and accession remain unresolved.
- Tarima chooses the War College, leaving the Caleb/Tarima relationship unresolved.
- Caleb remains at the Academy at episode end, but long-term belonging is not established.

## Explicit counterevidence ledger

- Caleb says he does not need connection, while the same work depicts him repeatedly seeking or forming connection.
- Caleb tries to leave, while also continuing to use Academy resources and eventually remaining.
- Nahla argues Starfleet can grow by correcting mistakes; Emrin challenges whether returning to former institutional patterns counts as progress.
- Federation representatives stress Betazed's strategic importance; Betazoid representatives emphasize Federation abandonment and security failure.
- Tarima wants access to a larger universe but chooses the War College, not the Academy.
- Nahla says she will not wall Caleb in, while Academy systems still impose curfew, permitted bounds, room assignments, and discipline.
- The negotiation ends on an affirmative response, but no completed accession or completed government relocation is depicted.
- The computer first reports Goja V unrecognized, then locates it after Betazoid chart access; neither output should be mistaken for omniscient truth divorced from its data context.
