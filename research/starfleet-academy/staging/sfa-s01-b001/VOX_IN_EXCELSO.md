## Explicit counterevidence

- Klingon tradition is presented as vital, but Jay-Den and Lura embody nonidentical ways of being Klingon.
- Federation asylum may preserve lives, but forced rescue risks cultural erasure and coercion.
- Jay-Den initially interprets his father's departure as rejection; Lura offers a plausible but unverified alternative intent.
- Caleb argues from legal/institutional facts yet is criticized for lacking lived understanding.
- The controlled battle ends without casualties in the depicted exchange, but this does not establish long-term political stability.
- News and official reports repeatedly carry uncertainty and must not be flattened into omniscient history.
