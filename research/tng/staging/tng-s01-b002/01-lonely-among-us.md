### Staging assertions

- `tng-s01-b002-staging:e07:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — The source supports an episode-local chain in which a nonmaterial entity affects successive crew members and later combines with Picard, but the entity's own account of motive and transit remains testimony. Supports: ev02, ev03, ev05, ev07.
- `tng-s01-b002-staging:e07:as02` **PROVISIONAL** — Picard's relief from command is supported as an institutional response to behavioral change and perceived incapacity, not proof that Picard had ceased to exist or lacked all agency. Supports: ev06, ev08.
- `tng-s01-b002-staging:e07:as03` **UNRESOLVED** — Post-transport identity continuity cannot be safely reduced to simple sameness: the source depicts energy-only departure, reconstruction using a retained physical pattern, incomplete memory, and successful return without supplying a metaphysical adjudication. Supports: ev08, ev09, ev10.
