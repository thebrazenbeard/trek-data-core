### Staging assertions

- `tng-s01-b002-staging:e09:as01` **PROVISIONAL / counterevidence** — The incriminating Maxia confession is explicitly disconfirmed by computer-log integrity evidence and must not be treated as a reliable historical record. Supports: ev04, ev05.
- `tng-s01-b002-staging:e09:as02` **PROVISIONAL** — Picard's altered battle experience is strongly linked in-source to external thought manipulation rather than an unsupported spontaneous psychiatric explanation. Supports: ev06, ev07, ev09, ev10.
- `tng-s01-b002-staging:e09:as03` **PROVISIONAL / identity boundary** — The Picard Maneuver's apparent two-location presentation is described as a warp/sensor tactical effect, not physical duplication of the Stargazer. Supports: ev03, ev10.
- `tng-s01-b002-staging:e09:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Bok's revenge motive is supported as his testimony and behavior; Kazago's removal of him supplies institutional counterpressure against the vendetta. Supports: ev08, ev10.
- `tng-s01-b002-staging:e09:as05` **PROVISIONAL** — Picard's self-doubt during the induced episode should not be generalized into lasting identity discontinuity. The source depicts manipulation, recovery, and restored orientation without a duplicate or replacement person. Supports: ev06, ev09, ev10.
