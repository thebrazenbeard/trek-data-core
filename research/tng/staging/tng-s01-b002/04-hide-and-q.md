### Staging assertions

- `tng-s01-b002-staging:e10:as01` **PROVISIONAL / frame rule** — The game-world dangers and transformations occur inside a Q-controlled altered frame and should not be projected as ordinary physical events without preserving that frame. Supports: ev02, ev04, ev07, ev09.
- `tng-s01-b002-staging:e10:as02` **PROVISIONAL** — Q's stated reason for testing Riker is source-supported testimony but not an independently verified account of Continuum motives. Supports: ev03, ev06.
- `tng-s01-b002-staging:e10:as03` **PROVISIONAL / identity boundary** — The source depicts reversible changes in age, embodiment, vision, and companionship; it does not establish that these temporary states globally replace the recipients' identities. Supports: ev07, ev09, ev10.
- `tng-s01-b002-staging:e10:as04` **UNRESOLVED** — The crew's experienced duration and Enterprise's uninterrupted instrumentation conflict at the ordinary-frame level. Picard attributes the mismatch to Q's control of time, but the source supplies no mechanistic reconciliation. Supports: ev05, ev10.
- `tng-s01-b002-staging:e10:as05` **PROVISIONAL** — Riker's arc supports a command/ethics conflict about power, consent, and self-determination because intended beneficiaries explicitly reject gifts chosen for them. Supports: ev08, ev09.
