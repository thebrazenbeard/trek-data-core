[identity; memory; continuity]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e13:ev02` — **depiction/action, ordinary frame, colony search.** The away team finds a second android body disassembled into components and physically resembling Data. The crew transports the components aboard for assembly. [identity; synthetic persons; technology]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e13:ev03` — **depiction/action, ordinary frame, activation sequence.** Crusher and the crew assemble and activate the second android, who identifies himself as Lore and behaves independently from Data. - `tng-s01-b003-staging:e13:ev05` — **dialogue, ordinary frame, command discussion.** Picard resists language that reduces Data and Lore to mere things, while Data himself objects to being referred to as an impersonal object. [institutions; identity/self-concept; ethics]

- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e13:ev08` — **dialogue/testimony + depiction, ordinary frame, confrontation.** Lore acknowledges deception and communication with the crystalline entity, undermining his earlier self-serving account of colony events and giving the crew reason to reassess his testimony. - `tng-s01-b003-staging:e13:ev10` — **depiction/action, ordinary frame, final confrontation.** Data and Lore physically confront one another; Wesley uses the transporter to remove Lore from the Enterprise. Data remains aboard. [identity; agency; consequence]

- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e13:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — Data and Lore are episode-locally distinct concurrently instantiated android entities, not a transporter-style duplication of one continuously tracked individual. Supports: ev02, ev03, ev10.
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e13:as02` **CONTESTED** — Lore's initial origin narrative is not safe to promote as history because later admissions and actions materially undermine his credibility. Supports: ev04, ev08.
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e13:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — Lore's successful impersonation demonstrates that appearance and shared design are insufficient for identity recognition; behavioral evidence becomes operationally important to Wesley. Supports: ev07.
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e13:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Data's remembered beginning and colonist knowledge are evidence about memory content and provenance, not evidence that Data is identical to any colonist whose records he carries. Supports: ev01.
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e13:as05` **PROVISIONAL** — The source presents institutional and interpersonal treatment of synthetic beings as morally and operationally significant, while leaving global legal/personhood resolution outside this episode-local record. Supports: ev05, ev06.
