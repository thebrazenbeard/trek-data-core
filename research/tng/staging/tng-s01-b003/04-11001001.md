### Evidence

- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e15:ev01` — **log/record, ordinary frame, opening sequence.** Enterprise docks at Starbase 74 for maintenance and upgrades, including holodeck work, under Commander Quinteros and Bynar technicians. [institutions; technology; continuity]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e15:ev02` — **dialogue/testimony, ordinary frame, Bynar observation.** Riker and crew describe Bynars as working in pairs and as closely linked with their planetary computer; these descriptions are crew observations and explanations within the episode. [culture; technology; epistemic pattern]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e15:ev03` — **simulation/holodeck, simulation frame, Minuet sequence.** Riker encounters Minuet, an unusually adaptive holodeck character. Picard later joins and remarks on the program's sophistication. [simulation; identity/self-concept; technology]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e15:ev04` — **computer report + command decision, ordinary frame, emergency sequence.** Apparent antimatter-containment failure produces a red alert. Based on available computer information and an imminent deadline, Data speaks for the captain and orders evacuation. [command; computer report; institutions]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e15:ev05` — **depiction/action, ordinary frame, evacuation/commandeering.** The crew evacuates while the Bynars remove Enterprise from the starbase. Picard and Riker remain aboard after the holodeck diversion. [deception; command; consequence]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e15:ev06` — **command decision, ordinary frame, engineering sequence.** Assuming hostile commandeering and lacking control, Picard and Riker jointly authorize the ship's auto-destruct under the rule requiring both officers' agreement, then later cancel it after regaining access. [institutions; command; risk]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e15:ev07` — **computer/system evidence, ordinary frame, bridge investigation.** Picard and Riker determine that the earlier red alert was a programmed response and find that Enterprise carries a large transfer of Bynaus computer data. [computer report; deception; epistemic pattern]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e15:ev08` — **dialogue/testimony + corroborating system state, ordinary frame, Bynar explanation.** The Bynars say a stellar event threatened their planetary computer and that they used Enterprise as temporary storage because they feared Starfleet would refuse. The stored data and disabled planetary system materially support parts of that account. [testimony; provenance; technology]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e15:ev09` — **simulation testimony, simulation frame, Minuet explanation.** Minuet states that the Bynars programmed her to keep Riker, and later Picard, occupied in the holodeck. Her behavior is therefore explicitly linked to a designed diversion. [simulation; deception; agency]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e15:ev10` — **depiction/action, simulation frame, ending.** After the Bynar operation ends, Riker returns to the holodeck but cannot recover Minuet; other generated figures appear instead. The source depicts loss of that specific program state, not a globally adjudicated death event. [simulation; continuity; unresolved]

### Staging assertions

- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e15:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — Minuet is explicitly source-bound to a Bynar-authored adaptive holodeck program; behavioral sophistication alone does not authorize this worker to globally resolve her personhood or identity class. Supports: ev03, ev09.
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e15:as02` **PROVISIONAL** — Data's evacuation order is supported as a command decision made from apparently urgent computer evidence that is later revealed to have been manipulated. Supports: ev04, ev07.
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e15:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — The Bynars' explanation is not mere unsupported testimony because system evidence corroborates the data-transfer and planetary-computer portions, while their motives for not asking remain their own account. Supports: ev07, ev08.
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e15:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Picard and Riker's auto-destruct action demonstrates a formal dual-authorization command rule under perceived hostile capture, followed by reversal when the factual situation changes. Supports: ev06.
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e15:as05` **UNRESOLVED** — Minuet's failure to reappear after the Bynar program ends establishes discontinuity of the encountered simulation instance but does not settle whether any meaningful psychological/person continuity existed or could persist elsewhere. Supports: ev03, ev09, ev10.
