## 15 — Too Short A Season

**Source:** `https://www.voyager.cz/tng/epizody/012tooshortaseasontrans.htm`  
**Cross-check:** `https://www.chakoteya.net/NextGen/112.htm` and `https://www.chakoteya.net/NextGen/episodes.htm`  
**Source anomaly:** Springfield's season-one index jumps from `s01e15` to `s01e17`; this work is therefore bound only provisionally in staging by title, production 112, airdate, and the alternate complete transcript source.  
**Local entities:** Mark Jameson, Anne Jameson, Picard, Riker, Crusher, Data, Karnas, Enterprise, Mordan IV government, Federation hostages, Starfleet, Cerebus II rejuvenation treatment.

### Evidence

- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e16:ev01` — **log/record, ordinary frame, opening sequence.** Enterprise receives orders to carry Admiral Mark Jameson to Mordan IV after Karnas reports Federation hostages and specifically demands Jameson as negotiator. [institutions; command; plot/problem]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e16:ev02` — **dialogue/authority claim, ordinary frame, arrival sequence.** Jameson states that Starfleet has designated him Senior Mission Officer with control of the away team while Picard retains command of the ship. [institutions; command; authority]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e16:ev03` — **medical record + observation, ordinary frame, early sickbay sequence.** Crusher discovers that Jameson's supplied medical tests are older than represented and later observes rapid cellular, DNA, and physical changes along with disappearance of detectable Iverson's disease. [medical evidence; deception; character state]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e16:ev04` — **dialogue/testimony, ordinary frame, treatment confession.** Jameson says he obtained an alien rejuvenation treatment from Cerebus II, normally administered slowly, and took both his and Anne's doses at once to become fit for the mission. Details of the treatment's general operation remain primarily his testimony. [testimony; identity; technology]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e16:ev05` — **dialogue + secured communication, ordinary frame, Karnas sequence.** Jameson infers that Karnas fabricated the dissident crisis; Karnas confirms there are no dissidents and that he controls the hostages, shifting the mission from the publicly reported premise. [deception; epistemic pattern; command]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e16:ev06` — **confession/testimony, ordinary frame, observation-lounge sequence.** Jameson admits that forty-five years earlier he supplied weapons both to Karnas and Karnas's rivals and falsified Starfleet records. He attributes the ensuing civil war heavily to his choice, while Picard notes Karnas also chose continued warfare. [institutions; ethics; responsibility]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e16:ev07` — **command dispute + depiction, ordinary frame, away-team sequence.** Jameson invokes Senior Mission Officer authority to lead an armed rescue using remembered tunnels; Picard accompanies him. The old tunnel knowledge is partly obsolete, the raid meets armed resistance, and Jameson collapses without being shot. [command; memory; consequence]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e16:ev08` — **medical report, ordinary frame, final sickbay sequence.** Crusher reports that Jameson's forced rejuvenation is destabilizing his cells and that she cannot stop the process; his condition becomes terminal. [medical evidence; identity/body continuity; consequence]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e16:ev09` — **recognition test + dialogue, ordinary frame, Mordan confrontation.** Karnas initially rejects the young-looking man as Jameson. Private historical knowledge and a scar from their old bargain persuade Karnas that the transformed man is the same Jameson he demanded. [identity recognition; memory; social/legal continuity]
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e16:ev10` — **depiction/log record, ordinary frame, ending.** Jameson dies after surrendering himself; Karnas frees the hostages. The final log records Jameson's burial on Mordan with Anne's request and Karnas's permission. [consequence; institutions; continuity]

### Staging assertions

- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e16:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — The episode supports continuous social and narrative tracking of Jameson through extreme bodily rejuvenation: wife, crew, memory, scar, and eventual Karnas recognition all treat the young body as Jameson. This is episode-local evidence, not a global identity rule. Supports: ev03, ev04, ev09.
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e16:as02` **PROVISIONAL** — Jameson's treatment explanation should be split epistemically: Crusher independently supports rapid physical rejuvenation and cellular destabilization, while claims about Cerebus II procedure, mortality, and dosage remain substantially Jameson's testimony. Supports: ev03, ev04, ev08.
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e16:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — Jameson exercised formal mission authority despite Picard's objections, and the source depicts that authority as institutionally real rather than equivalent to unrestricted wisdom or correctness. Supports: ev02, ev07.
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e16:as04` **CONTESTED / responsibility** — Jameson's arms transfer materially contributed to Mordan's long civil war, but the episode itself supplies counterevidence against sole-cause attribution through Picard's emphasis on Karnas's subsequent choices. Supports: ev06.
- `tng-s01-b003-staging:e16:as05` **PROVISIONAL** — Karnas's fabricated hostage narrative is explicitly overturned within the source; the initial official crisis report must therefore remain superseded/contradicted evidence rather than surviving as objective world-state fact. Supports: ev01, ev05.
