## 10 — The Loss

**Primary research artifact:** complete final-draft production script (`#40274-184`, final draft dated 1990-10-01 with revision pages through 1990-10-11), Star Trek Minutiae. **Cross-check:** Chakoteya episode metadata/transcript.  
**Local entities:** Troi, Picard, Riker, Data, La Forge, Worf, Crusher, Guinan, Janet Brooks, Enterprise, two-dimensional beings, cosmic string fragment.

### Evidence
- `tng-s04-b002-staging:e10:ev01` — counseling depiction, script 79–193: Troi helps Janet Brooks confront grief she had been avoiding after her husband's death.
- `...:e10:ev02` — sensor/depiction, 195–319: Enterprise intermittently detects an unusual plane-polarized field while Troi becomes dizzy and disoriented.
- `...:e10:ev03` — medical + empathic-state evidence, 669–788: Troi reports loss of empathic perception; Crusher obtains unusual readings while ordinary ship sensors remain unable to explain the force acting on Enterprise.
- `...:e10:ev04` — probe/sensor report, 1113–1274: probe analysis depicts a cluster of entities existing on a single two-dimensional plane and explains why edge-on sensor geometry initially concealed them.
- `...:e10:ev05` — counseling testimony/action, 1135–1241 and 2266–2330: Troi fears she cannot counsel without empathy, but Brooks later reports Troi's non-empathic session still helped expose renewed avoidance.
- `...:e10:ev06` — dialogue/self-concept frame, 1629–1703: Troi describes other people as hollow projections without empathic access; Riker challenges her assumption that loss of an advantage makes others less real.
- `...:e10:ev07` — scientific inference, 2046–2163: crew identifies a cosmic string fragment and initially hypothesizes the two-dimensional beings are being pulled toward a destructive gravitational hazard.
- `...:e10:ev08` — failed intervention + epistemic limit, 2149–2258: attempts to redirect or affect the beings with torpedoes fail; Data explicitly notes uncertainty about their response and nature.
- `...:e10:ev09` — hypothesis/action, 2425–2806: Troi proposes the beings may be intentionally stimulus-driven toward the string; Data and La Forge simulate the string's characteristic frequencies, causing the cluster to slow and letting Enterprise escape.
- `...:e10:ev10` — empathic report/revision, 2829–2959: as the beings enter the string Troi's empathic sense returns; she reports they regard it as home, overturning the crew's prior danger model for the beings while explaining her initial overload as an inference about her own condition.

### Staging assertions
- `tng-s04-b002-staging:e10:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — Troi's empathic loss is temporally associated with the two-dimensional cluster and reverses when it reaches the string; exact physiological mechanism remains incompletely specified. Supports ev02–ev04, ev10.
- `...:e10:as02` **PROVISIONAL / epistemic revision** — the crew's initial model that the string threatens the beings is a reasonable inference later disconfirmed by Troi's restored empathic report and the beings' continued course. Supports ev07–ev10.
- `...:e10:as03` **UNRESOLVED** — the beings' sentience level is not firmly established; Data reports no direct evidence of sentience and only simple-order behavioral indications. Supports ev04, ev08–ev09.
- `...:e10:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Troi's professional competence is not reducible to empathic ability: her confidence collapses, yet Brooks reports therapeutic benefit and Troi later contributes a successful behavioral hypothesis. Supports ev05–ev06, ev09.
- `...:e10:as05` **PROVISIONAL** — Troi's statement that others seem like holodeck projections is evidence of her altered self-concept/perception during loss, not evidence that surrounding persons became simulations. Supports ev06.
