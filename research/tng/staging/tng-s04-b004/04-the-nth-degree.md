## 19 — The Nth Degree

**Local entities:** Barclay, Troi, La Forge, Picard, Riker, Data, Crusher, Worf, Enterprise, Argus Array, unknown probe, Cytherians, Einstein holodeck representation.

### Evidence
- `tng-s04-b004-staging:e19:ev01` — simulation/character frame, Springfield 15–75: Barclay performs Cyrano on the holodeck and discusses persistent social anxiety and self-confidence with Troi.
- `...:e19:ev02` — probe/depiction, 80–130: an unknown probe affects shuttle systems and then exposes Barclay to an intense flash; medical examination initially finds no conventional injury.
- `...:e19:ev03` — cognitive change, 135–200: Barclay abruptly demonstrates engineering/medical insight beyond prior capability and rapidly solves technical problems around the Argus Array.
- `...:e19:ev04` — medical evidence, 265–298: Crusher finds greatly elevated neurotransmitter production, brain integration and cognitive capacity, while Geordi recognizes the change began after probe contact.
- `...:e19:ev05` — simulation use, 268–288: Barclay consults a holodeck Einstein representation during advanced theoretical work; the simulated scientist functions as a computational/dialogue aid, not the historical Einstein.
- `...:e19:ev06` — control integration, 300–407: Barclay increasingly interfaces his body/brain with Enterprise systems, eventually creating a direct neural-computer link and becoming difficult to separate without lethal risk.
- `...:e19:ev07` — command conflict, 408–468: Barclay initiates a radical propulsion event, refuses Picard's direct order to stop, blocks control restoration, and describes his expanded perspective as justification.
- `...:e19:ev08` — displacement/outcome, 469–489: Enterprise is transported roughly 30,000 light-years to the galactic center; ship control returns and Cytherian contact follows.
- `...:e19:ev09` — testimony/reveal, 485–499: restored Barclay says the Cytherian probe reprogrammed him because their technology seeks to bring outsiders to them for knowledge exchange; this explanation is supported by the successful contact but still partly testimony.
- `...:e19:ev10` — aftermath, 500–522: Enterprise returns safely after ten days; Barclay retains memory of acting but not the detailed how/why, and his enhanced state is no longer present.

- `...:e19:as05` **PROVISIONAL** — post-event Barclay shows continuity of autobiographical memory with a significant gap in procedural/motivational understanding, so memory continuity is partial rather than binary. Supports ev09–ev10.
