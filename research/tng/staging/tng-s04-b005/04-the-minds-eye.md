## 24 — The Mind's Eye

**Local entities:** Geordi La Forge, Picard, Data, Worf, Crusher, Troi, O'Brien, Kell, Vagh, Romulan interrogators, Enterprise, Kriosian rebels, conditioning simulation.

### Evidence
- `tng-s04-b005-staging:e24:ev01` — depiction/abduction: en route to Risa, Geordi encounters a Romulan warbird and is captured rather than completing the trip as his later cover memories imply.
- `...:e24:ev02` — conditioning/simulation: Romulan captors expose Geordi to repeated neural/visual conditioning and simulated scenarios, including commands to kill O'Brien, until obedience improves.
- `...:e24:ev03` — memory manipulation: Geordi returns to Enterprise reporting an uneventful seminar and does not consciously recall the abduction, while covert conditioning remains active.
- `...:e24:ev04` — political frame: Enterprise is mediating accusations that the Federation supplied weapons to Kriosian rebels; Klingon Governor Vagh distrusts Federation denials.
- `...:e24:ev05` — forensic evidence: Data and Geordi analyze a rebel rifle that resembles Federation technology but has energy characteristics consistent with Romulan design/replication, supporting a false-flag hypothesis.
- `...:e24:ev06` — conditioned action: under covert signals, Geordi redirects transporter/sensor systems, erases records and performs actions supporting the appearance of Federation interference.
- `...:e24:ev07` — medical clue: Crusher notices a minor visual-cortex vascular irregularity and sleep disturbance but initially lacks evidence tying it to manipulation.
- `...:e24:ev08` — technical inference: Enterprise detects E-band events; Data identifies their spectrum as similar to human neural frequencies and infers a system capable of transmitting directly to a brain.
- `...:e24:ev09` — conspiracy reveal/action: evidence connects the conditioning operation to Romulan manipulation and Klingon Ambassador Kell; Geordi is directed toward assassination before intervention stops the act.
- `...:e24:ev10` — aftermath: Geordi survives and begins recovery, but his conscious memory does not simply restore all conditioning history, leaving a source-supported trauma/memory gap.
