- `...:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — Scott survives as a maintained transporter pattern for 75 external years; Franklin does not survive due pattern degradation. Supports ev01–ev03.
- `...:as02` **UNRESOLVED** — The source establishes practical continuity after rematerialization but does not settle philosophical transporter identity questions. Supports ev01–ev03.
- `...:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — Holodeck Enterprise is a simulation of Scott's remembered ship, not that historical vessel itself. Supports ev05.
- `...:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Scott's adaptation crisis concerns social/technical displacement rather than loss of competence; later engineering contribution directly counters perceived uselessness. Supports ev04, ev06, ev08–ev10.
