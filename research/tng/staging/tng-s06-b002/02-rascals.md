## 07 — Rascals

**Local entities:** Picard, Ro, Guinan, Keiko, Riker, O'Brien, Crusher, Alexander, Lurin, Ferengi raiders, Enterprise, transporter anomaly.

### Evidence
- `tng-s06-b002:e07:ev01` — shuttle/transport event: Picard, Ro, Guinan and Keiko are caught in an energy field and rematerialize in child-sized bodies while retaining adult memories.
- `...:ev02` — medical report: Crusher finds their DNA/body development regressed while cognitive memories and skills remain substantially intact.
- `...:ev03` — institutional consequence: Starfleet command authority becomes contested because Picard's body appears pre-adolescent; Riker assumes command.
- `...:ev04` — family/interpersonal: Keiko and O'Brien retain marital memories/affection while bodily age creates discomfort and practical disruption.
- `...:ev05` — self-concept contrast: Ro experiences childhood regression as distressing; Guinan encourages exploration rather than assuming a uniform response.
- `...:ev06` — attack: unaffiliated Ferengi seize Enterprise and force adults into labor, underestimating the de-aged officers/children.
- `...:ev07` — strategy: Picard reframes apparent childhood as tactical cover and organizes children/de-aged adults to retake the ship.
- `...:ev08` — deception/action: Picard exploits Ferengi ignorance of ship systems with invented technical jargon while accomplices restore transporter/security control.
- `...:ev09` — resolution: Ferengi are captured and Alliance disclaims the renegades.
- `...:ev10` — restoration: transporter process is reversed, returning the four to adult bodies without loss of autobiographical memory.

### Assertions
- `...:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — Bodily age and autobiographical/social identity diverge during the anomaly; child appearance does not imply child memory/personhood. Supports ev01–ev05.
- `...:as02` **PROVISIONAL** — Picard's temporary command displacement is institutional response to bodily status and capability assumptions, not evidence of identity replacement. Supports ev02–ev03, ev07.
- `...:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — Keiko/O'Brien marital continuity persists psychologically while bodily circumstances materially alter intimacy/social recognition. Supports ev04.
- `...:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Restoration is depicted as reversal of the same local persons rather than creation of adult duplicates. Supports ev01–ev02, ev10.
- `...:as05` **PROVISIONAL** — De-aged appearance becomes tactical advantage because Ferengi classify the group as children and discount their retained expertise. Supports ev06–ev09.
