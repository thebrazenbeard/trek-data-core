## 08 — A Fistful of Datas

**Local entities:** Data, La Forge, Worf, Alexander, Troi, Frank Hollander, Eli Hollander, Western holodeck characters, Enterprise computer, holodeck program.

### Evidence
- `tng-s06-b002:e08:ev01` — technical experiment: Data and La Forge connect Data's positronic systems to Enterprise computer as a redundancy/interface test.
- `...:ev02` — simulation frame: Worf, Alexander and Troi run a Western holodeck story with ordinary fictional characters and safety expectations.
- `...:ev03` — computer anomaly: ship files begin retrieving Data's poetry/personality material after the interface experiment.
- `...:ev04` — cross-contamination: holodeck antagonist Hollander acquires Data's appearance, strength and abilities; other characters similarly become Data-like.
- `...:ev05` — safety failure: program no longer responds normally to exit commands and simulated weapons become materially dangerous.
- `...:ev06` — identity distinction: transformed holodeck figures retain role-specific Western motives/dialogue despite Data-derived appearance/capabilities.
- `...:ev07` — Data state: Data exhibits fragments of Western-program behavior/persona in ordinary ship context, evidencing reciprocal data contamination.
- `...:ev08` — engineering diagnosis: La Forge traces the anomalies to shared data paths created during the interface experiment and begins clearing them.
- `...:ev09` — holodeck action: Worf/Troi improvise technical defenses and defeat Hollander without assuming the figures are the physical Data.
- `...:ev10` — resolution: program ends and Data's systems are restored; residual Western mannerism briefly persists before normal behavior returns.

### Assertions
- `...:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — Data-like holodeck characters are simulation entities contaminated with Data-derived appearance/abilities, not duplicate physical Datas. Supports ev02–ev06.
- `...:as02` **PROVISIONAL** — Data's Western persona is software/data contamination rather than a new enduring person. Supports ev01, ev03, ev07–ev10.
- `...:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — Holodeck safety failure converts simulated threats into material risk without changing the simulation provenance of the characters. Supports ev05–ev09.
- `...:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Shared computer pathways create bidirectional information contamination between Data and recreational systems. Supports ev01, ev03–ev08.
- `...:as05` **PROVISIONAL** — Role continuity and Data-derived traits coexist in Hollander/Eli, demonstrating partial attribute copying rather than identity equivalence. Supports ev04–ev06.
