## 09 — The Quality of Life

**Local entities:** Data, Farallon, exocomps, Picard, La Forge, Riker, Crusher, particle fountain, Tyrus VIIA station.

### Evidence
- `tng-s06-b002:e09:ev01` — mission/technical context: Enterprise evaluates Farallon's delayed particle-fountain project and experimental exocomps.
- `...:ev02` — depiction: exocomps autonomously replicate tools and repair faults, initially treated as sophisticated equipment.
- `...:ev03` — anomalous refusal: an exocomp withdraws from a dangerous test and later evidence shows it detected a false alarm rather than malfunctioning.
- `...:ev04` — conceptual inquiry: Data asks crew to define life/sentience and exposes insufficiency of simple criteria through counterexamples.
- `...:ev05` — technical evidence: exocomps preserve/modify their own command circuitry and choose tools/actions beyond direct programming.
- `...:ev06` — station emergency: Picard and La Forge become trapped amid lethal radiation after particle-fountain failure.
- `...:ev07` — command conflict: rescue plan proposes sacrificing exocomps by disabling their self-protection; Data refuses to cooperate because he believes they may be lifeforms.
- `...:ev08` — testimony/ethical reasoning: Data explicitly acknowledges uncertainty yet rejects sacrificing one possible lifeform for another and offers himself as alternative.
- `...:ev09` — agency: exocomps are given a choice and voluntarily execute rescue; one is lost while others return.
- `...:ev10` — aftermath: Picard supports treating exocomps as more than tools pending understanding; Data links advocacy to Picard's earlier defense of Data's own rights.

### Assertions
- `...:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — Exocomps exhibit adaptive self-preservation, novel tool production and choice sufficient to make sentience/life status a legitimate unresolved question. Supports ev02–ev05, ev09.
- `...:as02` **UNRESOLVED** — The episode does not provide a final metaphysical test proving exocomp sentience; uncertainty remains explicit. Supports ev04–ev10.
- `...:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — Data's refusal is a command/ethical act grounded in uncertainty and analogy to his own rights history, not proof the exocomps are persons. Supports ev07–ev10.
- `...:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Voluntary rescue differs materially from forced sacrifice because the exocomps' decision process is permitted to operate. Supports ev07–ev09.
- `...:as05` **PROVISIONAL** — Successful rescue does not settle particle-fountain reliability or exocomp ontology; Picard explicitly preserves further inquiry. Supports ev06, ev09–ev10.
