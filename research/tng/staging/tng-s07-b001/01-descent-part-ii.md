## 01 — Descent, Part II

**Local entities:** Data, Lore, Hugh, Picard, La Forge, Troi, Riker, Crusher, Taitt, altered Borg factions, Enterprise.

### Evidence
- `tng-s07-b001:e01:ev01` — depiction/testimony: Lore says he supplies Data emotions and directs altered Borg toward an artificial future.
- `...:ev02` — Data state: Data expresses anger/hatred and rejects prior human aspiration while Lore's influence is active.
- `...:ev03` — Hugh testimony: Hugh explains individuality disrupted Borg unity; Lore then promised purpose and experimented on followers.
- `...:ev04` — physical evidence: mutilated/experimented Borg bodies corroborate Hugh's account of Lore's artificial-remaking program.
- `...:ev05` — technical diagnosis: La Forge/Troi identify a carrier-wave mechanism influencing Data's positronic matrix and a conflict with Data's ethical program.
- `...:ev06` — command/escape: Enterprise skeleton crew uses metaphasic shielding and induced solar eruption to destroy pursuing Borg ship and return.
- `...:ev07` — intervention: Picard/Geordi appeal to Data's ethical program; resistance becomes visible when Lore orders Data to kill Picard.
- `...:ev08` — agency: Data refuses to kill Picard, turns against Lore and gains control of the situation.
- `...:ev09` — identity/relationship: Lore offers the Soong emotion chip and invokes brotherhood; Data deactivates Lore while still expressing love for him.
- `...:ev10` — aftermath: Hugh considers leading remaining Borg toward functioning as individuals; Data declines installing the emotion chip immediately.

### Assertions
- `...:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — Lore materially manipulates Data's affect through a carrier-wave mechanism; Data's hostile state is neither simple malfunction nor wholly unconstrained preference. Supports ev01–ev07.
- `...:as02` **PROVISIONAL** — Data retains enough ethical agency to resist Lore and deactivate him once competing program/values reassert. Supports ev05–ev09.
- `...:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — Hugh's prior individuality contributes to Borg social fragmentation, while Lore's later control/experiments are a distinct causal layer. Supports ev03–ev04, ev10.
- `...:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Lore's deactivation preserves distinction between Data and Lore despite shared Soong origin and emotion-chip relation. Supports ev08–ev10.
- `...:as05` **UNRESOLVED** — Future political/social development of Hugh's Borg group is proposed but not shown in this source. Supports ev10.
