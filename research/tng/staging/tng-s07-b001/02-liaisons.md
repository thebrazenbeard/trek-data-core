## 02 — Liaisons

**Local entities:** Picard, Voval, Anna representation, Loquel, Byleth, Troi, Worf, Riker, Iyaaran cultural-exchange mission, Enterprise.

### Evidence

- `...:ev02` — crash depiction: Voval's shuttle appears disabled by an energy field and crashes; Voval enters a low-metabolic injured state Picard initially interprets as potentially fatal.
- `...:ev03` — encounter/testimony: Picard meets Anna, presented as a human survivor stranded seven years after a freighter crash.

- `...:ev06` — model revision: Picard notices impossible/contradictory details including repaired necklace, false injury and Voval's timely return.
- `...:ev07` — revelation: Voval identifies himself as ambassador and says Anna was a role/constructed scenario used to study human love/intimacy.
- `...:ev08` — source basis: Iyaarans derived their experiment from logs of an actual human woman's seven-year survival and later love story found in a wreck.
- `...:ev09` — institutional/ethical response: Picard states the direct manipulation would be considered criminal in his culture rather than accepting it as neutral diplomacy.
- `...:ev10` — outcome: three Iyaaran representatives identify pleasure, antagonism and love as targeted experiences; cultural exchange ends with explicit awareness of method conflict.

- `...:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — Iyaaran study of emotion uses direct experiential manipulation whose diplomatic legitimacy is explicitly contested by Picard. Supports ev05, ev07–ev10.
- `...:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Voval's apparent pilot role and later ambassador disclosure are role deception within one local person occurrence, not separate identities. Supports ev01–ev02, ev06–ev07.
