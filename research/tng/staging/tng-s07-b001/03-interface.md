## 03 — Interface

**Local entities:** La Forge, Hera/mother representation, Captain Silva La Forge referent, Data, Picard, Troi, Crusher, Raman crew, atmospheric subspace beings, interface probe.

### Evidence
- `tng-s07-b001:e03:ev01` — technical setup: Geordi's VISOR/neural pathways allow unusually complete sensory/motor interface with remote probe.
- `...:ev02` — safety evidence: increasing interface intensity produces real neurological/physical risk to Geordi; Crusher monitors tolerance.
- `...:ev03` — personal context: Geordi's mother and USS Hera are missing; Data explicitly notes Geordi's interpretation of available evidence is biased by hope.
- `...:ev04` — interface perception: through Raman probe Geordi sees/hears a figure appearing as his mother who asks him to bring the ship toward planet atmosphere.
- `...:ev05` — command conflict: Picard treats the mother-survival model as extremely unlikely and orders a safer recovery plan; Geordi later disobeys to continue descent.
- `...:ev06` — counterevidence: expected Hera ship/signatures are absent as probe descends, undermining literal mother-rescue explanation.
- `...:ev07` — revelation/testimony: communicating being says atmospheric subspace lifeforms became trapped aboard Raman and used Geordi's thoughts to assume his mother's form.
- `...:ev08` — causal explanation: beings say contact with Raman crew accidentally killed them; interface protected Geordi from same effect.
- `...:ev09` — rescue action: Geordi returns Raman lower to release beings while Enterprise engineers safely decouple his overloaded neural interface.
- `...:ev10` — aftermath: Geordi survives; mother/Hera remain missing rather than being recovered or confirmed dead by this encounter.

### Assertions
- `...:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — Mother figure is a thought-derived communication representation used by trapped subspace beings, not evidence Captain Silva La Forge is physically present. Supports ev03–ev08.
- `...:as02` **PROVISIONAL** — Geordi's grief/hope biases his hypothesis while real external communication still occurs; psychological bias does not mean the entire experience is hallucination. Supports ev03–ev08.
- `...:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — Picard/Geordi command conflict turns on risk and evidentiary standard, not simple indifference versus loyalty. Supports ev02, ev05–ev09.
- `...:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Raman crew deaths are attributed to accidental alien communication effect rather than malicious intent. Supports ev07–ev09.
- `...:as05` **UNRESOLVED** — Hera and Geordi's mother remain missing at episode end; this source does not resolve their actual fate. Supports ev03–ev06, ev10.
