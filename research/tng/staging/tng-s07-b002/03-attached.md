## 08 — Attached

**Local entities:** Picard, Crusher, Riker, Mauric, Lorin, Kes, Prytt, neural-link implants, Enterprise.

### Evidence
- `tng-s07-b002:e08:ev01` — mission/institutional context: Enterprise evaluates Kes request for Federation associate membership despite planet's political division with isolationist Prytt.
- `...:ev02` — transport interception: Picard/Crusher transport is deflected by Prytt tractor/antigraviton interference and they are detained as alleged spies.
- `...:ev03` — device effect: neural implants link Picard/Crusher thoughts, emotions and some sensations while also preventing easy separation.
- `...:ev04` — escape: they remove external restraints but implants remain active; nausea/pain increases with physical distance, constraining movement together.
- `...:ev05` — epistemic intimacy: involuntary thought-sharing exposes previously withheld reactions, memories and mutual romantic feelings.
- `...:ev06` — political testimony: Kes and Prytt each present paranoid conspiracy models about the other; Riker finds neither side capable of basic trust/cooperation.
- `...:ev07` — border action: Picard/Crusher cross toward Kes territory despite pursuit and force-field obstacles, using their linked awareness tactically.
- `...:ev08` — institutional judgment: Riker tells Kes their suspicion/manipulation and planetary division make Federation membership unsuitable despite formal progressiveness.
- `...:ev09` — recovery: Prytt transmit coordinates/release pathway; Picard/Crusher are returned and neural implants removed.
- `...:ev10` — aftermath: after link ends, Picard/Crusher acknowledge feelings exposed by the connection but choose not to begin relationship and value restored mental privacy.

### Assertions
- `...:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — Neural-link implants create involuntary partial mental transparency without merging Picard and Crusher into one mind/person. Supports ev03–ev05, ev09–ev10.
- `...:as02` **PROVISIONAL** — Thoughts revealed through the link are episode-local mental-state evidence; they do not automatically resolve every past/future relationship question. Supports ev05, ev10.
- `...:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — Kes/Prytt political paranoia is reciprocal and materially obstructs membership evaluation; neither faction's conspiracy claims are accepted at face value. Supports ev01–ev02, ev06, ev08.
- `...:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Picard/Crusher physical proximity is technologically compelled during escape, distinct from their voluntary emotional decisions afterward. Supports ev03–ev05, ev07, ev10.
- `...:as05` **PROVISIONAL** — Federation rejection is a governance judgment based on unresolved planetary division, not a claim that Kes institutions lack all democratic/progressive features. Supports ev01, ev06, ev08.
