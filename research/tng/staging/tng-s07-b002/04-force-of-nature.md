## 09 — Force of Nature

**Local entities:** Serova, Rabal, Picard, La Forge, Data, Fleming crew, Ferengi Prak, Hekaras II, Hekaras Corridor, subspace rift, Enterprise.

### Evidence
- `tng-s07-b002:e09:ev01` — mission: Enterprise searches Hekaras Corridor for missing Fleming amid tetryon fields that constrain warp routes.
- `...:ev02` — attack/revision: apparently disabled Ferengi ship attacks Enterprise, then explains disguised verteron probe disabled them and was believed Federation weapon.
- `...:ev03` — confession/argument: Hekaran scientists Serova/Rabal admit deploying disabling probes to force attention to claim warp fields damage local subspace.
- `...:ev04` — scientific review: Data finds Serova's model plausible but initially unprovable at available energy scale; crew agrees further research is warranted.
- `...:ev05` — coercive experiment: Serova creates extreme warp-field event with her ship, dying and producing a measurable subspace rift as predicted.
- `...:ev06` — empirical revision: rift and surrounding instabilities validate key causal claim that warp energy can rupture susceptible subspace regions.
- `...:ev07` — rescue constraint: Fleming is trapped inside rift; Enterprise cannot safely sustain warp there and instead uses brief pulse/coasting/transport rescue.
- `...:ev08` — rescue outcome: Fleming crew are evacuated; distortion-wave dynamics are used to escape rift after ordinary warp would expand damage.
- `...:ev09` — institutional response: Federation restricts vessels to warp five except emergencies and limits travel in susceptible regions while distributing findings.
- `...:ev10` — consequence: existing rift continues affecting Hekaras II climate; temporary weather control is planned because damage cannot immediately be reversed.

### Assertions
- `...:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — Serova's scientific claim moves from plausible/unproven to strongly supported by deliberately induced rift plus subsequent measurements. Supports ev03–ev06, ev09–ev10.
- `...:as02` **PROVISIONAL** — Validation of the hypothesis does not validate Serova's coercive attacks/suicidal experiment as acceptable scientific method; crew explicitly distinguishes evidence from method. Supports ev03–ev06.
- `...:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — Warp damage is spatially conditional/cumulative in susceptible regions rather than source evidence that every warp use everywhere instantly creates rifts. Supports ev01, ev04–ev06, ev09.
- `...:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Federation warp restriction is an institutional precaution responding to new evidence, not proof all other polities will comply. Supports ev09–ev10.
- `...:as05` **PROVISIONAL** — Fleming rescue requires adapting propulsion to avoid further rift expansion, linking technical constraint directly to accepted causal model. Supports ev07–ev08.
