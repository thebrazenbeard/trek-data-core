## 13 — Homeward

**Local entities:** Worf, Nikolai Rozhenko, Dobara, Vorin, Boraalan community, Picard, Enterprise, holodeck cave-journey simulation, Vacca VI settlement.

### Evidence
- `tng-s07-b003:e13:ev01` — planetary disaster: Boraal II atmosphere is collapsing; Prime Directive bars normal rescue of pre-warp population.
- `...:ev02` — covert intervention: Nikolai secretly beams Boraalans aboard and places them in holodeck cave simulation without informed consent.
- `...:ev03` — command conflict: Picard condemns violation but accepts continuing deception because immediate reversal would expose/kill rescued people.
- `...:ev04` — simulation frame: Boraalans experience a continuous cave migration while crew manipulates weather, terrain and timing to conceal starship environment.
- `...:ev05` — malfunction: Vorin leaves simulation and encounters Enterprise reality; Crusher cannot safely erase integrated knowledge.
- `...:ev06` — autonomy: Picard explains situation and explicitly permits Vorin to choose return, remain aboard, or reveal truth despite risks.
- `...:ev07` — testimony: Vorin says he cannot return and lie, but also cannot live as alien among Enterprise crew.
- `...:ev08` — consequence: Vorin dies in ritual suicide after being unable to bridge the two world models.
- `...:ev09` — relocation: crew uses staged holodeck storm/transport to establish Boraalans on Vacca VI without their knowing offworld transfer.
- `...:ev10` — family/social outcome: Nikolai stays with Dobara/community, who is pregnant with his child; Worf acknowledges Nikolai saved them despite conflict.

### Assertions
- `...:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — Boraalan relocation is a deliberate Prime Directive violation later managed to minimize cultural exposure, not a standard authorized first-contact process. Supports ev01–ev04, ev09.
- `...:as02` **PROVISIONAL** — Holodeck migration is a simulated environment whose effects on Boraalan decisions are real while its geography/weather are fabricated. Supports ev02–ev04, ev09.
- `...:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — Vorin receives genuine choice after exposure; his death is a consequence of irreconcilable knowledge/social identity rather than being killed to protect secrecy. Supports ev05–ev08.
- `...:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Nikolai's paternal/social commitment to Dobara/community coexists with his unauthorized intervention; successful outcome does not erase governance breach. Supports ev02–ev03, ev09–ev10.
- `...:as05` **CONTESTED** — Whether saving the Boraalans justifies the deception/non-interference violation remains explicitly morally contested. Supports ev01–ev10.
