- `...:ev02` — altered experience: Crusher initially experiences Ronin during sleep/half-conscious state and later meets him in visible corporeal form.

- `...:ev08` — forensic evidence: anaphasic energy is found in Felisa's buried body at cellular level and tied to cemetery/candle source.

- `...:ev10` — aftermath: Ronin is gone; Data/La Forge recover; Crusher recognizes manipulation across her family while also acknowledging journals show grandmother experienced happiness.

- `...:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — Felisa's corpse is reanimated/used through Ronin's energy; this is not evidence Felisa herself returned to life. Supports ev08–ev09.
- `...:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Quint's death/weather failures are linked to the same anaphasic mechanism by residue and Ronin's defensive admission. Supports ev05–ev09.
- `...:as05` **CONTESTED** — Howard women's reported happiness and Ronin's coercive dependence can coexist; source does not reduce the multi-generation relationships to either pure consent or pure fabrication. Supports ev01, ev04, ev07, ev10.
