## 17 — Masks

**Local entities:** Data, Ihat/personality variants, Masaka personality, D'Arsay archive, Picard/Korgano performance, Enterprise, generated city/artefacts.

### Evidence
- `tng-s07-b004:e17:ev01` — discovery: Enterprise scans 87-million-year-old D'Arsay archive embedded in comet; archive transmits information through sensor link.
- `...:ev02` — system transformation: alien symbols/artefacts appear as ship computers/replicators are reconfigured.
- `...:ev03` — Data state: Data develops intuitive knowledge and then distinct D'Arsay personalities begin replacing/overlaying ordinary Data behavior.
- `...:ev04` — diagnostic evidence: multiple behavioural nodes appear in Data's positronic net; La Forge describes his normal personality as buried under archive-generated cultural persons.
- `...:ev05` — material transformation: archive converts Enterprise matter, alloys and biological material into plants, aqueducts, temples and other D'Arsay city elements.
- `...:ev06` — testimony/cultural frame: personalities describe Masaka, Korgano, sacrifice, sun/moon cycles and ritual meanings through mythic language.
- `...:ev07` — causal crisis: Masaka activation accelerates transformation until ordinary ship systems and recognizable structure are threatened.
- `...:ev08` — interpretation: Picard infers Masaka/Korgano ritual balance from symbols and archive testimony rather than treating myths as literal cosmology.
- `...:ev09` — role-play intervention: Picard adopts Korgano mask/ritual role and persuades Masaka to resume cyclical chase/sleep pattern.
- `...:ev10` — recovery: transformation program is disabled, ship returns to normal, generated personalities/artefacts disappear, and Data reports impression of thousands of personalities.

### Assertions
- `...:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — Data's D'Arsay personalities are archive-generated behavioural/personality patterns instantiated through his positronic system, not global identity-equivalent Datas. Supports ev03–ev04, ev10.
- `...:as02` **PROVISIONAL** — Masaka/Korgano material is a reconstructed cultural archive whose mythology guides system behavior; mythic testimony is not automatically objective cosmology. Supports ev01–ev09.
- `...:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — Enterprise transformation is physical/molecular, not merely visual holodeck simulation. Supports ev02, ev05–ev07, ev10.
- `...:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Picard's Korgano identity is deliberate ritual role-play used to interact with archive logic, not identity transformation. Supports ev08–ev09.
- `...:as05` **PROVISIONAL** — Data's original personality is restored after archive disengagement without depicting permanent fragmentation into thousands of independent persons. Supports ev03–ev04, ev10.
