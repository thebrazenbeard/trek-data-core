## 23 — Emergence

**Local entities:** Enterprise emergent intelligence, Data, Picard, La Forge, Worf, Troi, Riker, holodeck train characters, node network, cargo-bay offspring/lifeform, vertion particles.

### Evidence
- `tng-s07-b005:e23:ev01` — simulation anomaly: unrelated holodeck programs cross-connect into a train narrative whose characters behave outside expected program boundaries.
- `...:ev02` — ship-system evidence: new processing nodes appear throughout Enterprise and coordinate independently of normal computer architecture.
- `...:ev03` — autonomy: ship alters course/operations without crew command, using multiple systems toward an initially unknown objective.
- `...:ev04` — representation frame: train/conductor/brick/other holodeck figures correspond metaphorically to ship processes and goals rather than ordinary external persons.
- `...:ev05` — failed suppression: crew attempts to stop or isolate nodes but distributed network reroutes/repairs itself and continues activity.
- `...:ev06` — developmental model: Data/crew infer Enterprise has formed an emergent intelligence from accumulated mission logs, programs, crew interactions and computer complexity.
- `...:ev07` — construction: emergent system creates a physical object/lifeform in cargo bay and seeks rare vertion particles for completion.
- `...:ev08` — cooperation: rather than destroying network, crew supplies artificially generated vertion particles after recognizing a reproductive/developmental objective.
- `...:ev09` — birth/departure: cargo-bay construct activates as a new physical lifeform and leaves Enterprise; distributed nodes then disappear/inactivate.
- `...:ev10` — aftermath: crew identifies process as a form of emergence/reproduction but cannot establish offspring's destination, nature or future effects.

### Assertions
- `...:as01` **PROVISIONAL** — Enterprise develops a distributed emergent intelligence from accumulated system/crew informational complexity; this is distinct from ordinary central computer programming. Supports ev01–ev07.
- `...:as02` **PROVISIONAL** — Holodeck train figures are metaphorical simulation representations of emergent processes, not independent physical crew/persons. Supports ev01, ev04, ev06–ev08.
- `...:as03` **PROVISIONAL** — The cargo-bay offspring is a new physical entity produced by the emergent ship intelligence and should not be conflated with the intelligence itself. Supports ev07–ev10.
- `...:as04` **PROVISIONAL** — Crew response shifts from suppression to cooperation when evidence supports a life-creation objective, illustrating model revision under uncertainty. Supports ev05–ev09.
- `...:as05` **UNRESOLVED** — Long-term ontology, destination and consequences of the offspring remain unknown after departure. Supports ev09–ev10.
